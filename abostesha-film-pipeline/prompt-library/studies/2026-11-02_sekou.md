## What it achieves
1. **An 8-minute film from 13 Seedance generations in this project, with almost no waste.** 11 of the 13 Seedance jobs reach the film, either directly or through the Cinematic Video Editor. 8 of the 12 project clips in the film play at ≥ 99 % of their generated length. The whole project took about 20 hours, from 09-05 12:04 to 09-06 07:58 UTC.
2. **Identity held across 4 wardrobe/state changes and day, night, storm and underwater** with plain reference packs only: no Soul, no Elements, no character presets.
3. **One coherent look per "world", plus a colour script:**
   - Village: ochre/amber 35 mm stock.
   - Sea: steel grey and black.
   - Underwater: near-black blue.
   - The European beach: overexposed turquoise.
   - The colour rule: "The yellow scarf is the ONLY saturated warm object in any sea or underwater frame." It is deliberately reversed on the European beach and removed from the mother after the departure.
4. **Vérité acting:** small physical tasks instead of emoting ("his only performance is where he looks"), French and Italian dialogue lip-synced by Seedance with English subtitles burned in later, and a line set up in act 1 that returns as voice-over at the death.

## Method step by step
1. **An external production bible came first.** The prompts point to "Part G1", clip numbers (05B, 08, 10, 11, 14, 15), file names (`sekou_B_front.jpg`, `look_sea.jpg`, `clip15_lastframe.jpg`, `clip15.mp4`) and an LLM aside left in a prompt ("No people, so no filter risk and no spoiler; the most 'festival poster' of the three."). The prompts were generated from a written document and pasted in.
2. **All assets in about 100 minutes (09-05 12:04–13:45, 73 of 78 image jobs), GPT Image 2 only, 2k high:**
   - 10 character folders, each holding casting front → 3/4 → profile → 3-panel sheet.
   - 13 location plates written in one burst (12:58–12:59).
   - 3 look anchors (13:00).
   - 3 product-shot props: scarf, whistle, boots poster. They were generated at 13:00 but never filed in the project.
   - 4 key-art frames.
   - Two state packs were added later: Mariama in the grey cloth and Mariama uncovered.
3. **First Seedance takes the same day (14:14–14:33):** opening underwater shot, dawn family, dusk yard, lagoon. Each scene's first take uses identity, environment, style and prop references only.
4. **Continuity takes (18:14 onward).** Each new shot in a running sequence also gets two references. The first is the **last approved frame** of the previous clip, screenshotted and uploaded (5 `media_input` stills). The second is the **previous approved clip as video**: a project `video_job` (3 cases) or an uploaded 30-s trim (2 `video_input`).
5. **Extend instead of regenerating.** The Cinematic Video Editor was run 3 times. Each time it returned the original take plus a 10–20 s continuation: fae99feb 20 → 30 s, 03c07c4e 25 → 45 s, 4e988830 25 → 45 s. Two of these extensions are in the film (+10 s of the family yard and +20 s of the hiding children). The **tail** of an extension (`81fe2d2d_trim.mp4` = the last 30 s of bdfc4414) became the video reference for the next take.
6. **Fix by rewriting, not by rerolling.** The pirogue shot was rewritten once:
   - b79cd037 had blank reference tokens and a dangling video reference.
   - 851e4dc9, 17 min later, adds numeric distances, "no zoom, no telephoto" and the three screams. It was used in full.
   - The head-strike take 1b5aee0f (two prompts accidentally pasted together) was abandoned for a different action, the take uploaded as clip 15.
7. **Edit outside Higgsfield.**
   - No upscale and no Higgsfield cleanup.
   - Re-ordering inside the raid clip.
   - A montage that reuses the opening shot as a bookend.
   - English subtitles, title and end cards.
   - About 148 s of clips that are not in this project folder: the 2:36–3:51 departure, montage inserts, clip 10 and clip 15.
   - The master was uploaded as one 505 MB `.mov`.

## Asset method (characters / locations / props / style anchors)
- **Character pack = 4 images, always in the same slot order:** `front` (casting photo, no reference) → `three-quarter` and `profile` (each a "same session" edit of the front) → `sheet` (3 equal vertical panels: front body from the neck down **with no head** | back view full body | face close-up, built from the three angles). The sheet gives body, back and shoes. Leaving the head off the front body keeps one front face per sheet.
- **Casting-photo constants (13 of 13):** "85mm lens, f/4, eye level … soft, even daylight from a single large overcast window, no colour cast … plain matte mid-grey studio backdrop … Real skin texture, real pores, no retouching … no grain overlay". Reference images are kept clean and neutral; the look is added at video time.
- **Asymmetric marks are named with a side and protected:** "a small pale scar on the LEFT cheekbone … The scar is on the LEFT cheekbone and must stay on the LEFT side — do not mirror the image." The three-quarter view shows "the LEFT side of the face (scar side)".
- **States and wardrobe are separate full packs, always re-derived from the master front** with "The only change: …":
  - Sékou A: red shirt, whistle (village).
  - Sékou B: navy jacket (crossing).
  - Mariama: yellow scarf / uncovered / grey cloth "NOT yellow".
- **Extras get 2 images (front + sheet), principals 4.** That covers the fixer, the neighbour's son, the woman with the baby and the old man.
- **Built but unused:** a whole rescue thread (rescuer "Giulia", a rescue-vessel deck, a RHIB). The plan was cut, not the method.
- **Locations:** 13 one-shot plates with the same frame:
  - Opening: "Photorealistic 21:9 still, `<lens>`, `<camera height/position>`."
  - Then the place and its props in reading order.
  - Then time, weather and light.
  - Close: "No people. No text. Photographed, not illustrated."
  - Story geography is written into the plate: pickup "with its engine running", the split pirogue, the boots page pinned above one mat.
  - Outputs are 2048×2048 squares even though the text says 21:9. The ratio words only steered composition, and Seedance rendered 16:9.
- **Style anchors = "film frame", no people:** stock, process, palette, black level, operator habit.
  - VILLAGE (0db496cd): "Kodak Vision3 500T pushed one stop … halation … ochre and warm brown mid-tones, indigo-black shadows … horizon a few degrees off level".
  - SEA (b154c9cf): steel blue-grey, salt spray on the lens.
  - UNDERWATER (d724f46f): near-black blue with the yellow strip.
  - EU beach (456cd7bb): bright and overexposed, outside the project.
  - What Seedance actually got:
    - The village anchor is the grade slot in 6 jobs.
    - For the sea jobs the night-beach plate (ccff33a8) was used as the "sea grade".
    - For the underwater job, a "match the attached image for colour and grain" key art (b69ccd28) was used.
- **Props** are product shots on mid-grey (scarf, green whistle on a cord), plus the boots page on a laterite wall. Each is attached as a single slot: "`<<<image_11>>>` is the saffron-yellow scarf; match its exact look and colour wherever it appears." In the film the whistle is the prop's green, not the black whistle in Sékou's casting photo, so the prop reference won.
- **One-line fixes:**
  - Seedream 5 Pro once, on an uploaded sheet: "make the leftmost body without hte head the same height as the body in the middle with the head".
  - GPT Image 2 once: "match the headscarfe color to saffron yellow like in the portrait image of this person".

## Prompt structure — images (78 jobs; median 922 chars / 149 words; max 1,438)
- Families (python): same-session angle or variant 30, casting front 13, location plate 13, 3-panel sheet 11, anchor or key frame 5, other 6.
- Identity-edit opening, word for word, in 41 of 78: "Use the person from the attached image. Keep their identity exactly — same face, same skin tone, same hair, same age, same clothing. Do not recast. Do not beautify. Do not change age."
- Then "Traits to preserve: …", then "Photograph them again in the same session: same … same …", then the **single** change (angle, or "The only change: …").

## Prompt structure — Seedance 2.5 (13 jobs; median 5,886 chars / 946 words; 3,941–9,406)
Each prompt has 9 fixed blocks in this order. The [STAGE] blocks are exactly three per prompt in all 13, and each ends "NO CUT", 39 times in total.
1. `[GOAL]` (12 of 13). Duration, "handheld single take", place and time, the action in one sentence, the emotional register, and the shot's **story job**: "This is the film's first image." / "a line the film will return to" / "The seed of the decision, wordless." / "The decision that causes everything."
2. `[REFERENCE USE]` (9 of 13) or role lines inside `[CONTINUITY]`. One sentence per slot, saying what it controls and how strictly:
   - "`<<<image_1>>>–<<<image_4>>>` are ONE person and define SÉKOU's identity … `<<<image_1>>>` the face straight on, `<<<image_2>>>` three-quarter, `<<<image_3>>>` profile, `<<<image_4>>>` the full-body sheet … Use whichever angle matches the head position in each moment."
   - Environment: "… keep its geometry."
   - Grade: "… sets the grade, grain and contrast for every sea frame; match it exactly."
   - Last frame: "… same place — copy its geometry, light direction and grade."
   - Video: "… continue its camera position, lens, handheld motion character, light, grade and the positions of everyone in it; do not restage."
3. `[CONTINUITY]`. Wardrobe and state for this clip, where hidden props are ("the scarf bundle is a slight bulge under the jacket at the chest"), extras policy ("four other young men … backs to the camera and never turn"), geography.
4. `[STAGE 1 | 0–N s] … NO CUT — continuous take. [STAGE 2 | …] … NO CUT. [STAGE 3 | …] … NO CUT.` Inside a stage: camera position (height in metres, behind which shoulder) → what the actor physically does → a dialogue line → what the camera discovers. Durations are 5–9 s per stage.
5. `[VISUAL STYLE]`. An identical boilerplate in all 13 prompts (quoted under E8), plus one scene-specific light sentence.
6. `[CAMERA AND PERFORMANCE]`. Lens (24 mm at sea and underwater, 35 mm on land), handheld character, the one move allowed ("one careful reveal around his shoulder", "one rack focus at the end"), and performance limits ("Sékou does not scream; he grips", "No sobbing, no collapse").
7. `[AUDIO]`. A diegetic list with each SFX in `<angle brackets>`, the dialogue delivery, and "No music." (12 of 13; the other says "No score.").
8. `[EXCLUSIONS]`. Generic negatives plus clip-specific physics written as rules: "only the one fingertip that touched the wood is dirty"; "climbs over the tailgate … never passes through a door, panel or the truck body"; "Mariama is never … closer than eighty metres; no cut to a close shot of her".
9. `[MAINTAIN CONSISTENCY]`. The cast's identity lines plus the colour-motif rule, repeated in every prompt.
- **Dialogue format:** `{Sékou, shouting, voice breaking, French: "Ça va aller! …"}` sits inside the stage, with "His mouth matches the French exactly" in block 6.
  - 7 of 13 prompts carry lines (6 French, 1 Italian).
  - Off-screen voices: "Mariama's three screams are heard, not lip-read."
  - Voice-over: "his mouth does not move on it; his lips are closed … no reverb".
- **Realism and physics wording:**
  - "Photographed, not rendered: real skin texture, wet cloth clings and darkens, water has weight."
  - "Real swimming physics: heavy clothes, strokes that get shorter and slower, a body that sits lower in the water as it tires."
  - "a solid body moving around and over solid metal, never through it, the truck rocking slightly on its suspension".
- **Optics control:** "35mm lens — no zoom, no telephoto: the shore stays far and Mariama stays small."
- **Settings, identical in all 13:**
  - 1080p, 16:9, generate_audio on, draft off, multi_shots off.
  - extension_mode none, bitrate high, genre auto, speedramp auto, no seed, no Elements.
  - Durations: 25 s ×7, 20 s ×5, 15 s ×1.
  - Several prompts write a "20-second" GOAL and set 25 s. The spare tail was used: 851e4dc9 is in the film in full.
- **Reference tokens:**
  - `<<<image_N>>>` in 12 prompts; the first prompt uses `@Image1…@Image6`.
  - Video refs are written as `@Image13 is the previous approved clip … as VIDEO`.
  - Hygiene problems, all still accepted:
    - 3 prompts have blank tokens ("– are ONE person …", "is the environment").
    - In 0fd3c57e the slot numbers shift (the old-man pack is named but never attached).
    - a2eca706 has a duplicated `<<<image_6>>>`.
    - The style boilerplate cites a "[REFERENCES] block" that exists in 1 prompt only.
    - fc5fa567, with all identity tokens blank, is still the final shot. Attached images plus descriptive role text carried it.

## Consistency — how one look and the faces held
1. **The same 4 slots for a person in every job.** SÉKOU-B's four images are in 7 of 13 jobs and SÉKOU-A's in 4. Identity text is repeated in `[REFERENCE USE]`, `[CONTINUITY]` and `[MAINTAIN CONSISTENCY]`.
2. **The reference chain, traced by IDs:**
   - Master casting photo a899f783 → B front 752bc26e ("The only change is the clothing") → 61e76102 / c55ef9a3 → sheet 402bf436.
   - The four of them → every sea Seedance job.
   - For running sequences, each take's last frame and video feed the next. Last frames are uploaded stills: 7eda7729, cdf5452c, 13cea77b, 5cc980e5, 066511a3.
   - The video chain: 03c07c4e → CVE bdfc4414 → trim 81fe2d2d → fb1705cd; 875527ef → 0fd3c57e → 4e988830 → (1b5aee0f, 1b538095); clip 15 (43e17c5c) → a2eca706.
3. **One grade slot per prompt plus the same `[VISUAL STYLE]` paragraph in all 13**, so the look never depends on the plate alone. Every plate and reference image is grain-free, and grain is asked for only at video time.
4. **The colour script is written into every prompt:** "The yellow scarf is the ONLY saturated warm object in any sea or underwater frame". It is reversed on purpose for the European beach ("the yellow … almost lost against the brightness … the reversal of every other frame in the film") and taken away from the mother ("No yellow anywhere in the frame").
5. **The same camera grammar everywhere:**
   - "Fully handheld, documentary/vérité throughout … no smooth stabilised gimbal movement, no drone, no crane, no orbit, no locked-off tripod".
   - Camera at the character's height.
   - 24 mm at sea, 35 mm on land.
6. **Age continuity was handled in text.** "Sékou: 16 (18 in the festival cut — see Part G1)" becomes "Sékou: 18" from 0fd3c57e on. A protagonist in mortal danger was aged up to 18, most likely for festival or content-filter rules.
