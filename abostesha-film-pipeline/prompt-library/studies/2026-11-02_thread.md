- Source type: Higgsfield project (open-source; prompts and canvas public) + finished film. File = 344.1 s: a 5:30 film (one continuous shot, 0:00–5:14, plus a 16 s repeat of its own opening), a "MADE BY BART BIALOBRZESKI" card (5:30–5:35) and the 8 s Higgsfield "$1,000,000 Global Film Festival" outro. 837 jobs, 12 Aug → 14 Sep 2026, 2,125 credits; final submitted 15 Sep 10:02 UTC. Logline from the page: "The hour when gutters run dark and grief and joy pass like strangers. Behind one window, something is about to happen — or already has."
- What it achieves: a 5 min 14 s period piece (1780s Central-European town, dawn) that plays as ONE unbroken camera move with no visible cut. The camera starts on a grieving man's face through window glass, follows a falling wooden spindle down into the street, then relays from subject to subject (rat → fishwife → dog → coffin cart → mourners → kissing couple → pickpocket boys → a mother → her baby's foot → a fly → a sparrow) over the rooftops, back in through a window, into a birth that turns into a fatal haemorrhage, and finally back out through the same window glass so that the last frame IS the first frame. The film is a loop. Same face, same room, same grade and the same spindle prop across 21 generated pieces.
- Method step by step:
  1. **Look-dev by engine shoot-out (12 Aug, 173 image jobs).** The same 300–550-word "cinematic film still from a European period drama … Arriflex … Kodak Vision3 250D … 50mm" prompt was run on Soul Cinematic (free, 119 renders), Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro and GPT Image 2.   2. **Seed one-take in the middle of the story (12–13 Aug, 25 Seedance text+image jobs).** "ONE continuous 25-second handheld take" of the spindle → rat → fish stall → dog with 3 image refs (spindle, crowd, look). The 11th try, `65a87cab` (21:9, 25 s, 2,206 words), became the anchor. It is film 0:24–0:48.
  3. **Grow it forward with video_extension (14 Aug → 6 Sep, 147 forward jobs).** Each approved clip was cropped to the film's 2.333:1 frame, trimmed at the last good frame, and its TAIL (2.4–30 s) was re-uploaded as the source video for the next extension. 15 forward links made the rest of the film.
  4. **Grow it backward once (24 Aug, 22 backward jobs).** The seed's first 4 s were the source of a backward extension ("PREQUEL — THIS SHOT ENDS WHERE THE SOURCE VIDEO BEGINS") whose first frame was pinned with an image ref (the man's face through glass). Result `9265b427` = film 0:00–0:24 (its 10–25 s part was later replaced by video edits of itself). Both ends of that link were pinned.
  5. **Assets made only when the story reached them.** The man's 3-panel sheet (23 Aug, before the prequel), the parlour and bedroom plates (25 and 28–31 Aug), and the four women's sheets (27–28 Aug) all came just before the extensions that needed them. Rooms were locked with "Keep this exact picture, unchanged … Change ONLY the WINDOW SILL" edits and a GPT Image 2 reverse angle.
  6. **Close the loop (5–6 Sep).** A frame grab of the film's own first frame (`4cc0a538`) was uploaded and used as a FIXED LAST FRAME in the last forward extensions. The final 9 s link `3a3c9779` orbits the man, wipes the lens with the window frame, passes through the glass and "ARRIVES at <<<image_3>>> exactly on the LAST frame". The edit then simply plays the opening again.
  7. **Repair with video_edit, one change at a time (8 jobs).** These made the frozen man breathe and sob, fixed a helper's face drift and added a glance, removed a fish from a dog, made the opening's blood on the hands match the later-designed ending (half-covered, fresh red), and, the night before submission, covered the naked newborn with linen at 1080p on the logo-stamped master.
  8. **Finish outside.** NLE assembly at 1470×630 ("THREAD_ukladka02" = assembly v02), Topaz Video (model thd-3) on ~35 s chunks: a 2K test (2048×878, 11 jobs) and then 4K (3840×1646, 8 jobs). Final file `THREAD_4k_V3.mp4` 3840×1648. ## Focus: how ~5.5 minutes became ONE continuous shot

### 1. Architecture: seed in the middle, grow both ways, close the loop
```
         backward ext (1×, pinned start image)        forward ext ×15 (tail → extend → trim → tail …)
[0:00 prequel 9265b427]──►[0:24 SEED 65a87cab]──►[0:48]──►…──►[5:05 3a3c9779 ends ON frame 0:00]──►(replay 0:00–0:16)
```
- The anchor was never the opening. It was the most "physical" moment, the rat-height relay through the market, made first as a plain text+image one-take. That proved the look and the camera language before any character work.
- Forward extensions carried the story. ONE backward extension created the opening, and it was pinned at BOTH ends: the first frame by an image ref, the last frame by the extension itself, which ends on the seed's first frame.
- The ending was steered to land on the opening frame (image ref as a fixed last frame), so the cut back to 0:00 is invisible.

### 3. The link procedure (what they did 17 times)
1. **Generate** a Seedance 2.5 extension at 720p, 16:9, 9–30 s (25 s most often), generate_audio on. The prompt describes the source's last frame and ends on a deliberately "continuable" state (§4).
2. **Judge and trim.** In the NLE, crop to 2.333:1 and find the last frame where the take is still good. Everything after it is thrown away. Typical use: 13–25 s of a 25 s output; 6.5 s of a 30 s output once.
3. **Export the tail** as the next source: 1470×630, 24 fps, no grade, mostly muted, named by in/out points ("07_01_11s_od_2s_mute.mp4" = 11 s from 2 s). Short tails (2.4–5.4 s) for fast exterior motion. Long tails (6–30 s) once people, a room and blocking had to carry over. The 30 s source `43e73005` was a whole previous take.
4. **Upload → video_extension (forward)** with the same reference set as the scene plus the new prompt. The output starts after the source; in the timeline it butts directly onto the trimmed previous clip.
5. **Repeat with prompt amendments** until one take holds. A failure is turned into a new "= a failed take" sentence, and the identical prompt is re-rolled 2–5× in parallel.
6. **If a good take has one flaw, do not re-extend; video_edit it** (§7). If a later scene changes a design (blood on the hands), edit the earlier footage to match.

### 4. Prompt wording that holds camera and action continuity (verbatim)
- **Docking sentence (136 of 169 extensions):** "This 25-second shot continues the source video from its exact last frame, in the same unbroken take. … The first frame of this extension is identical to the source video's last frame and the action flows on without a pause or a cut."
- **Describe the last frame in words (61 prompts, "The source ends on …"):** framing, thirds, who stands where, prop state and colour. For example, "The source ends on the HUSBAND standing pressed back against the dark oak panelling, on the LEFT third of the frame, medium-wide, the crying newborn wrapped in linen in his arms — the lower half of the bundle red and wet, his hands red — and, on the RIGHT third of the frame, the OLD MIRROR …".
- **Carry the camera's velocity, not just its position:** "The camera in the source has been PUSHING IN toward him very slowly for 25 seconds, and it goes on pushing in at EXACTLY the same speed." / "the camera's forward motion flows on without a pause or a cut" / "the door continues opening without a pause or a cut" / "the pan continues without a pause or a cut".
- **Every framing change is physical:** "Every change of framing is reached PHYSICALLY, on screen … There is NO cut, NO jump, NO new angle that the camera did not travel to. An instant change of framing at any moment = a failed take." Also "ONE camera movement only … No pan, no tilt, no zoom, no re-frame, no second movement" when a link should be calm.
- **Leader rule for relays (exteriors):** "THE CAMERA NEVER STOPS — IT ALWAYS HAS A LEADER … in every moment it is FOLLOWING something that moves … and when one leader leaves, the next one is already moving inside the frame in the same instant." Hand-offs are written as a numbered list ("Hand-off 1 — dog to rolling cart …"). "NOTHING APPEARS OUT OF THIN AIR — Every creature and object enters the frame by physically travelling in from outside the frame edge."
- **Foreground wipes as in-take punctuation:** "A porter's legs sweep across the near foreground and wipe the frame — and behind them the wooden cart is already rolling across the shot".
- **End every link on a continuable state:** "still advancing, still in motion, as the video ends mid-push"; "The camera is still creeping forward on the last frame, never settling"; "the shot ENDS THERE, on his hand on the latch, the door STILL CLOSED. We never see it open."; "THE CHILD IS NOT BORN IN THIS SHOT … the shot ends in the MIDDLE of her labour"; "it ends in calm". The next link's beat is never started at the end of the current one.
- **Speed lock (187 prompts):** "Natural real-time speed throughout. No slow motion anywhere." Also "no speed ramping, no time remapping, no frame-rate change". A speed change at a join is the most visible seam.
- **Look lock (177 prompts):** "LOOK LOCK — THE SOURCE GRADE IS THE LAW — Every frame keeps the EXACT look of the source video … A cleaner, brighter, more colourful or sharper digital look at any point = a failed take." For a planned look change: "LOOK LOCK — TWO LOOKS, ONE FILM … the change is the glass, not a new grade."
- **Space locks for interiors:** "THE ROOM IS CLOSED — NOBODY LEAVES, NOBODY ENTERS THE FRAME" (46), "THE GEOGRAPHY OF THIS SHOT — WHERE EVERYTHING IS" (49), "NEVER LOST — THE CAMERA FOLLOWS HIM … A frame without him in it, a man who vanishes and reappears, two men = a failed take" (24), "SINGULARITY — Exactly ONE man … Nothing duplicated" (170). Off-screen people are placed "BEHIND THE CAMERA" and shown only "as reflections in the mirror".
- **References as knowledge, not frames (117):** "REFERENCES — KNOWLEDGE ONLY, NOT SHOTS — The reference images tell you what the room and the people look like. They are NOT framings, NOT camera positions, NOT moments of this shot." This stops the model from cutting to a reference composition mid-take, which is a classic one-shot killer.

### 5. Pinning the ends (first/last frame without start/end-image parameters)
No job used a start_image/end_image parameter. All pins are image refs named in the text, plus the extension mode itself.
- **Backward + pinned first frame (prequel `9265b427`):** "Its last frame must be identical to the source video's first frame … so the two join into one unbroken take with no visible cut, in the middle of the roll." And: "<<<image_1>>> is the exact first frame of this shot … Start precisely on this frame." The closing beat walks the camera into the seed's opening framing: "The camera has arrived — low over the street, in the exact framing of the source video's first frame — and holds that framing for the final two seconds while the spindle rolls away". The 2 s hold at the join likely gives the backward model a stable target (inference).

### 10. Rules for our Seedance 2.5 one-shot (reusable)
1. Make the hardest, most physical moment first as a standalone one-take with 2–4 image refs (prop on grey, world density, look). Make it the seed; reach the opening with ONE backward extension.
2. Fix the master crop (e.g., 2.333:1 from 16:9) on day one. Export every source from the NLE timeline in that crop, ungraded, muted, named by in/out points.
3. Feed short tails (2–5 s) for fast motion and long tails (6–30 s) when faces, rooms and blocking must carry over. Never let the source end mid-glitch: trim to the last good frame first.
4. Open every extension prompt with the docking sentence, then a one-paragraph verbal description of the source's last frame, including the camera's motion and speed in progress.
5. End every link on a continuable state (camera still moving, action not yet resolved, door still closed). Never put the next beat's start at the end of this link.
6. Paste the same LOOK LOCK, SPEED, FILM and LIGHT blocks into every link. Add ROOM IS CLOSED / GEOGRAPHY / SINGULARITY for interiors and LEADER / hand-off rules for exteriors.
7. To land on a target frame, use a SHORT final link (≈9 s) with "THE LAST FRAME IS FIXED — <<<image_N>>>", the camera still travelling until the last frame, no hold. Approach it over 2–3 trimmed links.
8. Hide inside/outside or big scale changes with a physical foreground wipe (a window frame or bodies crossing the lens) or with a subject that travels.
9. Repair with video_edit ("Keep EVERYTHING … change ONLY ONE thing" + "= a failed take" criteria). Re-edit earlier footage when a later design decision changes a continuity detail.
10. Budget ~8 tries per link (3–32 seen) and ~6% yield. Re-roll identical prompts 2–4× in parallel before rewriting.

## Prompt anatomy

### Image prompts (604 jobs)
- **Engine shoot-out:** one look prompt run across Soul Cinematic (free; 275 images from 43 distinct prompts in 13 families, batch 4), Nano Banana Pro, Seedream 5.0 Pro and GPT Image 2.
- **Style anchor order:** shot type ("A cinematic film still from a European period drama shot on 35mm motion-picture film: an Arriflex film camera loaded with Kodak Vision3 250D stock, a spherical 50mm cine prime lens …") → place/time → composition → light ("Fully diffuse overcast pre-dawn skylight") → percentage palette ("60% cold grey-green … 30% weathered brown … 10% warm candle-flame amber") → texture ("every surface damp and organic").
- **Sheets on mid-grey (109 prompts):** "Three photographs of the same man arranged side by side in one wide image on a seamless plain mid-grey background, a film character sheet": front full body, back full body, and a large three-quarter portrait. Flat diffuse light, unretouched skin. Wardrobe and stains specified "identical in every panel". Variant sheets exist per story state (bloody `85415809` → "remove all the blood" `0702c9bc` → "red on the inside" `a3117ab2`).
- **Surgery edits (37 "Keep everything exactly as it is"):** room sill raised ("Change ONLY the WINDOW SILL"), blood removed, face-structure transfer that keeps the frame's own light, grade and expression ("The reference provides ONLY the three-dimensional shapes of the facial features … It provides NOTHING else").
- **Reverse angle by description (GPT Image 2):** "THE REVERSE ANGLE of the reference room: the camera stands in the corner BEHIND THE BED'S TALL HEADBOARD … <<<image_1>>> … controls geometry, materials, light and atmosphere ONLY — not framing".

### Seedance 2.5 prompts (214 jobs)
- **Block order (stable from 25 Aug):** `=== CONTINUATION — THIS SHOT EXTENDS THE SOURCE VIDEO ===` → `=== ONE SHOT — NO CUTS, EVER ===` → scene-specific locks (THE ROOM IS CLOSED / HE DOES NOT MOVE / THE MIRROR IS ALIVE / THE BUNDLE AND THE HANDS) → `=== REFERENCES — KNOWLEDGE ONLY, NOT SHOTS ===` (one line per `<<<image_N>>>`: who or what, and what it controls) → `=== THE GEOGRAPHY OF THIS SHOT ===` → `=== DIALOGUE LOCK ===` → `=== COMPOSITION — NEVER CENTRED ===` → `=== CAMERA ===` with timecoded phases → `=== ACTION ===` with `0-5s:` beats and named stages ("SHOCK, STILL ACTIVE", "HOPE — READING THE SIGNS", "FREEZING", "WAITING") → `=== ACTING ===` → `=== SPEED ===` → `=== SINGULARITY ===` → `=== LOOK LOCK ===` → `=== LIGHT ===` → `=== FILM ===` → `=== AUDIO ===`.
- **Section titles are rules, not labels:** "HE DOES NOT MOVE — ONLY HIS FACE CHANGES", "THE ROOM IS CLOSED — NOBODY LEAVES, NOBODY ENTERS THE FRAME", "NEVER LOST — THE CAMERA FOLLOWS HIM", "THE LAST FRAME IS FIXED".
- **Reference naming:** `<<<image_N>>>` in 95%. Each ref gets its identity plus its scope ("<<<image_4>>> is HANNAH … in this shot only a reflection in the mirror and a voice behind the camera"). Rooms come as two refs of one space ("TWO VIEWS OF ONE SINGLE ROOM … photographed from opposite sides. There is only ever ONE bedroom"). Leftover `@Image 7` mixes appear in 24 prompts.
- **Dialogue:** a DIALOGUE LOCK with an exact count ("Exactly THREE short lines … each once … Apart from those three lines, NOT ONE WORD is spoken"). Lines are in double quotes placed inside the timecoded ACTION, with the delivery stated before each ("says, rough but level:"). Off-screen speakers are placed "BEHIND THE CAMERA, off screen". Anti-narration line: "the descriptive sentences of this prompt are NOT dialogue" (43). Silent shots get "NOBODY SPEAKS … Any spoken word, any moving lips = a failed take".
- **Camera language:** "One camera, 50mm lens" (81%), handheld "breathing very slightly", explicit phases with timecodes, sizes per second ("at 0s the medium-wide … at 12s a medium shot … at 25s a MEDIUM CLOSE-UP"). Movement types are named ("PUSH IN", "DOLLY RIGHT", "CLIMB and a SLOW EASE", "DROP and a CHASE"), and each change is "caused by a new subject taking over the shot".
- **Realism and physics wording:** "Photorealistic 35mm motion-picture film, Kodak Vision3 250D stock, photochemical colour, organic film grain thickening in the shadows, soft highlight roll-off, natural 24 fps motion blur … Not a video game, not a 3D render, not a clean digital look"; "Real textures: damp hair, sweat, unretouched skin with open pores"; "HIS BREATH IS INVISIBLE: no vapour".
- **Negative wording:** "X = a failed take" sentences (85%, up to 10 per prompt) instead of a negative block. Every lock names its failure in concrete visual terms.
- **Audio:** `=== AUDIO ===` last; "Raw location sound only, no music anywhere", then timed `<angle-bracket>` sound events. Sound-only plates: "THIS SHOT IS ABOUT THE SOUND … The picture barely changes; everything that matters happens in the SOUND" (3 jobs, groans of pain, 7 s at 480p).
- **Settings:** 720p, 16:9, 25 s default, 30 s for long acting links, 9–15 s for precise links; no draft, no multi-shot. 1080p only for the final edit on the 1080p master.

## Consistency system
1. **One person = one 3-panel grey sheet**, attached to every shot where the person exists, even when they are only a reflection or a voice. Man `0702c9bc` in 145 Seedance jobs; midwife, wife, Hannah and Jane 86–103 each. Story-state variants (blood) are separate sheets, not text.
2. **One room = two plates from opposite sides** (`952b2c66` from the door wall, `5cb97590` the reverse angle by GPT Image 2), plus written geography ("The camera stands near the head of the bed, close to the door wall of <<<image_2>>> …").
3. **The film's own frames become refs:** frame grabs re-uploaded (`4cc0a538` first frame, `ebe3b123` the man in the parlour, `637d7b84` spindle mid-fall), and the source video itself carries the look forward ("THE SOURCE GRADE IS THE LAW").
4. **Frozen text blocks:** LOOK LOCK, FILM, LIGHT, SPEED and SINGULARITY are pasted almost verbatim into every link (Kodak line in 203/214; "heavy desaturated olive-green grade" in 178).
5. **One recurring prop as the story's thread:** the spindle has its own grey product shot (two versions). Prompts plant it in later rooms ("THE WINDOW — TWO LEAVES, THE SPINDLE ON THE RIGHT OF THE SILL").
6. **Retro-edits** keep early footage consistent with late design decisions (hand blood, frozen acting).
7. **Selection** lived in the NLE and in which tails were re-uploaded. No likes and no folders were used for curation.
