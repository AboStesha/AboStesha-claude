# Study: The Breach — sileent (https://higgsfield.ai/@sileent/projects/the-breach) — 2026-11-02

- Source type: Higgsfield project (open-source, prompts and canvas public) + finished film (8:02 incl. titles/credits, 482 s; story 0:30–6:50). Contest: Higgsfield AI Film Festival. 1,240 jobs, 11 Aug → 2 Sep 2026, ≈40,856 credits.
- What it achieves: a two-hander war drama that looks like one continuous prestige feature — same cold grade, same two faces, same two rooms in every shot; long handheld one-takes that feel physically shot (camera sways, dust, weight), restrained acting with sync dialogue in three languages (English, German, Italian) generated natively by Seedance 2.5 (heard only through prompts and subtitles here), and very small "honest" props (a blue canteen, a stick grenade, a pick) that carry the story.
- Method step by step:
  1. **Doctrine first (11–12 Aug, 532 jobs, almost all images).** One LLM-authored prompt kit with coded IDs (`$$$ LOC-01 …`, `CHAR-K1-ID`, `PROP-03`, `SCENE 0 — LOC-04-PLATE`) was run across many image engines at once (Soul Cinematic, Cinema Studio, Nano Banana 2/Pro, GPT Image 2, Soul V2, Seedream) to find the look. The grade doctrine changed three times — "prestige war epic / Alexa 65 / Kodak 2383" (11 Aug) → "green-gray-brown documentary, milky lifted shadows" (11–12 Aug, 160 prompts) → "COLD STEEL" (12 Aug) → the final **"Cold North-Sea palette — steel-teal shadows, desaturated gray midtones, near-monochrome cold … Underexposed one stop, crushed blacks with detail … Never flat, never TV-lit"** (from 14 Aug; in 391 prompts, 366 of them Seedance).
  2. **Assets as composite sheets on neutral gray.** Every character = ONE uploaded image: face portrait + headless ghost-mannequin costume front + back + weapon, all on flat gray (Tommy, Kurt, Corporal, Hutchins; separate "dinner dress" sheets for the epilogue). Props on flat gray (Walther P38, bloody rag, stick grenade, blue-enamel canteen, shoulder patch). Built by short edit prompts ("make full body uniform of this Canadian soldier … No head", "show me the back of this full body uniform", "remove the head", Angles 180° rotate) and assembled outside Higgsfield, then re-uploaded.
  3. **Blocking with grey-box previz.** For the interior one-take the creator uploaded untextured 3D walkthroughs (grey rooms, a sphere as the actor, bricks under the holes; ≥12 uploaded versions) and told Seedance "Follow the camera movement of the reference video EXACTLY … take ONLY camera movement and room geometry from it" (0-OT v7–v10), later softened to "The attached previz video is a PATH GUIDE only: take Tommy's route and the room layout from it — not its stiff camera, not its empty rooms, not its placeholder shapes" (2-4-OT, 88 prompts).
  4. **Build the film outward from one approved take.** The approved interior one-take (2-4-OT, job `09e63706`; 4 Cinematic Video Editor passes were also tried on it) was downloaded and re-uploaded, then **extended backward** (1-OTa: the street, the sniper, the run into the doorway — most of the 94 backward-extension jobs) and **forward** (5-OT/5a — 34 forward jobs). The exterior opening in the film is that backward extension, cleaned again in Cinematic Video Editor (job `7cd024b9`, 0–25.7 s of `5609c259` with an isolated-helmet image).
  5. **Then cut coverage around the wall.** Scenes 5a–5h are 15–30 s "multi-cut" Seedance generations written as `CUT n (a–bs) — side, lens, size:` blocks, alternating the two rooms, each locked by "MASTER" stills (Kurt's room & pose `5797d446`, Tommy's wall framing `ec4ea6b7`) plus the approved bedroom video `c2b10721` as a location-only reference.
  6. **Iterate cheap, finish at 1080p.** Seedance 2.5: 238 jobs at 480p, 181 at 720p, 95 at 1080p; no draft mode used (draft=False on all 514). The winning prompt is re-run at 1080p, often with a shorter duration than the prompt describes (prompt says 30 s, job set to 8 or 15 s to keep only the first beats).
  7. **Patch the edit at the end (1 Sep).** Bridge shots from two clips ("CREATE A LINK BETWEEN <<<video_1>>> AND <<<video_2>>> THE CHARACTER HAS TO AT THE BREACH"), coverage from a master ("show me some details … the shoes … the hands on the rifle, close-up of the eyes"), a video-to-video fix of the epilogue ("remove the red and white cross from all the uniforms. Change the quote at second 6 …"), dialogue pickups in accents ("VERY OLD GRANMAN ITALIAN, SAYING IN ROMAN ACCENT …"), and 30 s **audio plates** (picture irrelevant, "the AUDIO is the deliverable").
  8. **Post:** letterbox to ~2.35:1 (prompts: "16:9 protecting a 2.39:1 crop"), extra darker/colder grade + heavy vignette vs the raw generations, burned-in English subtitles, serif title cards, archival photos and facts at the end.
- Prompt structure: see "Prompt anatomy" below. Image prompts 24–29 words (edits) or 160–460 words (sheets/plates); Seedance 2.5 median 1,291 words (p10 806, p90 2,124, max 5,308).
- Asset method (characters / locations / props sheets): see "Consistency system".
- Adopt: see "Adopt for our pipeline".
- Do not copy: Tommy/Kurt/Corporal designs, the wall-breach story, the canteen and grenade beats, Ortona settings, the Christmas epilogue, any dialogue line, the "Sileent" title treatment. Take the method only.
- Proposed skill changes: listed at the end → copy to LESSONS.md as "proposed".

- **Look:** 16:9 renders letterboxed to ~2.35:1 (cropdetect 640×272 inside 640×360); subtitles sit in the lower bar. Near-monochrome cold teal/cyan-gray on Tommy's side; olive-khaki uniforms; Kurt's side is warmer ochre/green-yellow mottled plaster — **the two rooms are colour-coded** (prompts call Kurt's red/mottled plaster "the only color in the film, and even that cold"). Deep crushed blacks, heavy vignette and darker grade added in post (raw Seedance frames are visibly brighter than the film), soft halation on window light, shallow depth of field in close-ups, wide primes (24 mm) for movement, 40/85 mm for faces through the hole.
- **Shot sizes:** dominated by close-ups and ECUs (eye at the hole, trigger finger, hands, faces against plaster) and medium shots of each man against "his" wall; very few wides; the breach edges are deliberately kept soft in the foreground of Kurt's close-ups ("never a clean portrait, never without the breach edges in frame").
- **Pacing:** two long one-takes open the film (~30 s each, no cuts); the wall section is cut tight — detector finds 68 shots in 1:27–6:49, mean 4.7 s, median 2.8 s, 26 shots under 2 s (dark passages are under-detected, so real cutting is a bit faster). Long holds return for the emotional beats (locked 30 s framing for the "gift" dialogue).
- **Sound (from prompts, not heard):** every Seedance 2.5 job had generate_audio on; 91% of prompts say "No music", 90% "No subtitles", 27% "FOLEY SOUND ONLY". Music, if any, came from post or the single "anxious ww2 ambient cinematic sfx with one single violino note that keep rising" Seedance job.

## Prompt anatomy

### Image prompts (710 jobs)
- **Two registers.** (a) Long coded spec prompts for sheets and plates (Soul V2 median 163 words, Soul Cinematic 289, GPT Image 2 99, Seedream 5.0 Pro 1,380 because whole video prompts were pasted in to make keyframes); (b) one-line edit prompts on Nano Banana 2 / Pro / GPT Image 2 (median 24–29 words; 142 of 251 Nano Banana 2 jobs ≤30 words): "remove the head", "show me the back of this full body uniform", "isolate just the helmet on a gray background", "recreate this picture in color", "add more tables", "night time", "replace the two character with the one attached. keep the rest the same".
- **Spec prompt order (characters):** `$$$ CHAR-ID · NAME — purpose (state) · 3:4` → one-sentence style anchor ("Color character identity reference from a contemporary historical drama, shot today, set in December 1943. 35mm film, photorealistic … NOT archival, no monochrome, no sepia.") → `LIGHT:` (flat, unglamorous, "NO key light, NO rim light") → `SUBJECT:` (age, build, skin as *living* skin, eyes, stubble, grime, wardrobe collar) → `EXPRESSION:` (with NOT-list) → `FRAMING:` (flat frontal headshot on dull cool-gray, no gradient, no vignette) → "Period-accurate … no modern elements, no text, no watermark."
- **Chaining phrase:** "<<<image_1>>> defines this character's facial features, skin tone, eye color, hair and helmet EXACTLY … Use only the face and wardrobe identity; do NOT copy the emotional expression from the reference." Sheets are neutral; dirt/blood/soot are scene states ("Clean face in this sheet (soot is a scene state)").
- **Prop prompt:** `$$$ PROP-03 · WALTHER P38 — side profile, worn · 4:3` + same style anchor + one object, one state ("the opposite of the clean rifle"), laid on gray or on the set's floor tiles.
- **Location plates** were written as *15 s held video shots* (`SCENE 0 — LOC-04-PLATE · GROUND FLOOR — EMPTY, LIGHT STRIP (15s, single held shot) [CHAIN: bind approved LOC-03 as @Image 1 — the light strip enters through THAT doorway; direction must match]`) but rendered as stills, so the same text could later drive Seedance. Real 1943 photos were bound with strict roles: "(historical photograph) defines ONLY the damage statistics and scale … Do NOT use the people in the photograph, its black-and-white tone, or its vintage photographic look".
- **Style transfer one-liners** between engines: "make <<<image_2>>> in the style of <<<image_1>>>", "<<<image_1>>> in the cinematography of <<<image_2>>>".

### Seedance 2.5 video prompts (514 jobs)
- **Header line** `$$$ SCENE 5 — 5e-b v7 (one continuous take, 30s — the gift, one framing: …)` = scene · shot code · version · take type · duration · dramatic beat. 470/514 have it; versions are bumped in the header (v2…v10), so every iteration is traceable.
- **Block order (late, stable form):** `[Material Roles]` → `Style:` → `Lighting:` → `Color:` → `Grade:` → `Camera:` → `Skin:` → `Acting:` → `Physics:` → `Composition:` → `Continuity:` → `Technical:` → `Audio:` → `Characters:` → `Scene:` (LIGHT GEOMETRY / GEO-SPATIAL / exact cast count) → `THE SHOT — one unbroken take, in continuous time:` with `(0-6s)` beats, or `CUT n (a-bs) — side, lens, size — BEAT NAME:` → `FINAL FRAME:` → `Rules:` → `[Negative Constraints — final exclusions]` split AUDIO / VISUAL / CONTENT. Block usage: Style/Lighting/Color/Camera/Audio ≥93%, Acting 87%, Physics 89%, Characters 88%, Scene 84%, Rules 63%, Material Roles 48% (all prompts after 19 Aug).
- **Early form (12–17 Aug)** used Higgsfield's Seedance template: `[Camera — from the reference video]` / `[Materials]` / `[Generation Goal]` / `[0-5s — ROOM 1, wrecked kitchen]` … / `[Maintain Consistency]`.
- **Reference naming:** `<<<image_N>>>` / `<<<video_N>>>` tokens (343 and 175 prompts), each given ONE job in `[Material Roles]`: "defines KURT's FACE ONLY … NEVER use this image's background, dark backdrop or portrait lighting", "is the MASTER for Kurt's room, pose and geometry — hold it EXACTLY", "is the START FRAME — the FIRST FRAME of this video matches it EXACTLY", "is the GESTURE REFERENCE — it demonstrates the PROCEDURE only … Do NOT copy the man, his clothes, watch, room, rug or daylight", "defines TOMMY'S ROOM ONLY … Do NOT copy its events, its actions or its camera movement; it is a location reference, nothing else". Start frames were passed as normal image refs and described in text (start_image role: 0 Seedance jobs). Warning seen in the data: several prompts lost their tags on paste ("<<<image_3>>> defines …" became " defines …"), so roles silently mis-bound.
- **Dialogue:** early in "quotes", later in `{curly braces}` preceded by language, accent and intention: "a voice — English, hard German accent, flat, unhurried: {You dropped a round. I heard it roll.}"; "in German, LOUD … pitched to be obeyed: {Klar hier! …}"; coughs placed inside lines ("{Merry Chris—} and THE SECOND COUGH BREAKS THE LINE … {...Christmas, kid.}"); hard counts ("Exactly THREE spoken lines in the whole take … NO other voice anywhere — no muttering"); "Dialogue language: authentic Canadian American English"; off-screen voice performances described by *intention per line*, not by emotion words. 27% of prompts use `{}`, 40% quotes, 26% name the German accent.
- **Timing:** 63% contain `(a-bs)` timecodes, 65% end with `FINAL FRAME:`, 20% use `END STATE:` per stage (good for extensions/stitching), one-takes repeat "Still the same shot:" at every beat to stop the model cutting.
- **Camera language:** physical, not cinematic adjectives: "24mm wide prime, carried by a running human operator — it sways with his steps, bobs, drifts off Tommy and catches him again; never locked on him, never stabilized, no gimbal", "ARRI Alexa 65, spherical Master Primes … 180° shutter", lens per cut (24/40/85 mm in 41/37/9% of prompts), "the frame never follows him: HE moves inside the frame", "The camera never passes through the wall" (62 prompts).
- **Realism/physics wording:** "Real weight", "Gravity and inertia respected", "falls like dead weight, no stunt roll", "boots slip on wet rubble", "breath VISIBLE in the freezing air", "Skin: Pore-level realism — vellus hair, asymmetric features, capillary flush" (233), "THE SET IS IMMUTABLE — walls, ceiling, floor identical first frame to last" (127), "No identity drift" (274).
- **Negative wording:** every block ends with "never …" clauses; blood is fenced ("No blood anywhere: every stain OLD, DARK, DRIED, MATTE" — 59%); cast is fenced at both ends of Scene ("Exactly two people … no one else appears at any point"); late prompts add an exhaustive audio negative list ("no music, no score … no drones, no swells, no stingers, no ambient pads …").
- **Settings:** 16:9 on all; 30 s on 392/514 (15 s 39, 10 s 21, 8 s 13); generate_audio on all; multi-shot mode OFF on all (cuts are written in the prompt); bitrate high on 393; draft never used; 480p/720p for exploration, 1080p re-render of the chosen prompt.
- **Video extension:** backward 94 (1-OTa from the approved interior take, plus the "names" shot `b5d8d384`), forward 34 (5-OT/5a). Prompts for extension say what to dock to: "Its LAST FRAME is the first frame of this shot … Do not repeat its events", "This take must end on EXACTLY the same frame the reference video begins on — it is one single continuous cut."

## Consistency system (how one look and two faces survived 514 video generations)
1. **Frozen text blocks.** The same Style/Lighting/Color/Grade/Camera/Skin/Technical strings were pasted into every shot: "Underexposed one stop, crushed blacks with detail" in 411 Seedance prompts; one of two identical `Style:` lines in 360; "Skin: Pore-level realism" 233; "Technical: 24fps smooth motion. 8K detail." 241; the Kodak-2383 Grade line 119. Only Scene/Acting/Audio change per shot.
2. **One sheet per character, attached to every shot.** Tommy's composite sheet (`c9e99c36`) is in 415 of 514 Seedance jobs, Kurt's (`ff6a5b4d`) in 200; Corporal/Hutchins sheets in the street shots; dinner-dress sheets for the epilogue. Faces were never re-described in detail in video prompts — only short identity anchors ("very young Canadian soldier, boyish face, flushed red cheeks, Brodie helmet too big").
3. **MASTER stills for rooms and framings.** Kurt's room + seated pose (`5797d446`, 162 uses, made by iterative Nano Banana edits: "remove the green plaster on the right, and the carpet …"), Tommy's breach-wall framing (`ec4ea6b7`, 92), Kurt's hand on the grenade (`cb02792b`). Each is labelled MASTER/START FRAME in Material Roles.
4. **The approved take becomes the location bible.** The bedroom clip `c2b10721` (an edited 21 s assembly) was attached to 107 later shots as "defines TOMMY'S ROOM ONLY"; the 5e-a job `145cadab` was attached to 29 later jobs; extension sources `df31350d`/`0f9648e4`/`66a7e3ee` carry the light state forward/backward.
5. **Props as separate refs:** the shoulder patch (`70befbc8`, 67 uses), stick grenade (`78423ea6`, 55), Walther, rag, blue canteen (`1906b81e`, 56).
6. **Geography rules in words:** "the two rooms connect ONLY through the 30 cm breach", "Tommy sits LEFT of the breach", "Both head-turns go to frame-RIGHT" — screen direction and 180° continuity written as rules.
7. **No favourites/likes used (liked = 0)** — selection lived in folders (S1–S5, Videos) and in re-uploads of chosen outputs.

## Adopt for our pipeline (concrete rules)
1. **Lock a "look block" before any shot** and paste it verbatim into every image and video prompt (Style / Lighting / Color / Grade / Camera / Skin / Technical). Change only Scene, Acting, Audio per shot. Run the look test across 3–4 image engines on day 1 and pick one doctrine; record version changes in the header.
2. **Coded header on every prompt:** `$$$ SCENE n — <shot-code> vN (<take type>, <dur> — <beat>)`. Keep the code in filenames, folders and the prompt-library entry so finals are traceable.
3. **Character sheet = one composite image** (neutral face portrait + headless costume front/back + hand prop, all on flat gray, no dirt). Separate sheet per costume state. Dirt, blood, soot are scene states written in the shot prompt. Attach the sheet to every shot that shows the character.
4. **`[Material Roles]` first, one job per reference**, with an explicit "use ONLY … / do NOT use its background, lighting, expression, camera". Name masters: `MASTER (room/pose)`, `START FRAME`, `GESTURE REFERENCE`, `LOCATION ONLY`, `SOURCE VIDEO (extend)`. Before rendering, check every `<<<image_N>>>` survived paste and matches the attachment order.
5. **Blocking:** for any camera move longer than ~10 s, make a grey-box previz (Blender/our 3D) and bind it as "PATH GUIDE only: take the route and room layout — not its stiff camera, not its empty rooms, not its placeholder shapes". For a precise hand action, film it on a phone and bind it as gesture reference with a do-not-copy list.
6. **Write cuts in the prompt, keep multi-shot off** for dialogue coverage: `CUT n (a–bs) — side, lens, size — BEAT:` + exact lines + `FINAL FRAME:`. For one-takes write `THE SHOT — one unbroken take, in continuous time:` and start every beat with "Still the same shot:".
7. **Dialogue format:** language + accent + intention, then the line in `{}`; state the exact number of spoken lines and "NO other voice anywhere"; place breaths/coughs inside lines; add "No music, no subtitles" and the long audio negative list; subtitles in post.
8. **Build outward from the best take:** approve one hero take, re-upload it, then extend backward/forward ("Its LAST FRAME is the first frame of this shot … Do not repeat its events") and use it as `LOCATION ONLY` reference for all coverage in that set.
9. **Iterate at 480p, finish at 1080p** with the identical prompt; if only the first beats are needed, render a shorter duration from the same long prompt rather than rewriting.
10. **Fence the failure modes in Rules:** cast count at both ends of Scene; blood state ("old, dark, dried, matte"); "THE SET IS IMMUTABLE"; screen direction ("both head-turns go to frame-RIGHT"); "the camera never passes through the wall"; "No identity drift".
