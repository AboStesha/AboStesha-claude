# Study: CONTROL — pietromalegori (Higgsfield public project `control`, publication 26caf69d-3716-4d7b-a53f-d6e2b086d469) — 2026-11-02

- Source type: Higgsfield project (open source, prompts and canvas public) + finished film (10:19 file: story 0:00–9:14, credits 9:14–10:10, Higgsfield festival bumper 10:10–10:19). Higgsfield Global Film Festival entry, published 13 Sep 2026. Two accounts: `pietromalegori` (all 25 intro jobs, 283 videos, 87 images) and a second account (`user_38JD…`, 165 videos, 173 images — almost all character/location/prop sheets). End credits: "A Forma Studio production · AI artist Manuel Campagna & Pietro Malegori · Edited by Pietro Malegori · AI gen sound design & mix by Bravagente".
- What it achieves: a 56-second "found archive" prologue that sells a fictional history (a world population cap) as if it were real TV news, assembly pool footage, reportage and a documentary interview — then hard-cuts into a 2.39:1 cold-teal fiction drama. The archive works because each clip imitates a specific *recording situation* (who held the camera, why, with what), not a filter. The drama behind it is restrained, locked-off, dialogue-driven Seedance 2.5 with real voice recordings driving lip-sync.
- Method step by step:
  1. **Assets first (mostly 14–27 Aug, mostly the second account).** 216 of 260 image jobs were made by 27 Aug; fixes and new sheets continued to 10 Sep. Character sheets = one 16:9 image with three panels on neutral grey: frontal close-up portrait + headless full-body front + headless back (Nano Banana 2 / Seedream 5.0 Pro, 2k, a few 4k). Locations = wide plates (Soul Cinematic, Seedream). Props = product shots on grey. Each approved sheet becomes a Higgsfield **Element** (`char_captain`, `loc_apt_cap`, `prop_karp`, `birthcode` …; 53 Elements used by Seedance 2.5, 55 across the project).
  2. **Drama scenes (23 Aug → 11 Sep).** Seedance 2.5 at 1080p, 21:9, generate_audio on. Most prompts run 1,100–5,200 words (p10–p90; min 11, max 8,014). Elements are tagged as `<<<element-uuid>>>`. Blocking comes from location stills with **red/yellow stick-figure markers** (≈80 prompts: 80 contain "marker", 23 "stick figure"; the exact count depends on the definition). Composition comes from `@[REFFRAME]` stills (13 tokens in 3 prompts). Dialogue is driven by supplied voice recordings (`<<<audio_N>>>`, `@[REFVOICE]`): 64 jobs carry one of 6 private audio files.
  3. **The intro was made last and fast.** Interview fragment 1 on 29 Aug (2 jobs) and fragments 2–3 on 6 Sep (3 jobs); archive "stock" on 5–6 Sep (18 jobs) plus 2 more on 11 Sep. 25 Seedance 2.5 jobs, all 4:3, 1080p, audio on; 296 s generated, 55.8 s used (19 %) from 12 jobs (48 % of intro jobs reached the film — far above the drama's rate).
  4. **Each archive prompt = a fake "source".** Assembly = "house-system switcher cuts between fixed cameras"; riot = "single shoulder-mounted broadcast camera … working inside the disturbance"; aerial = "gyro-stabilised long-lens camera mounted under a news helicopter"; hospital = "a single camera operator inside a real facility, recording because it is happening"; interview = "backstage documentary — the making-of camera, not the finished interview".
  5. **Generate rushes, not shots.** 15 of 25 intro prompts are 2–6-shot "controlled multi-shot sequences" with hard cuts at exact timecodes; the edit then takes 1–3 s from one shot of each (e.g. 1.7 s of a 15 s, four-shot assembly generation).
  6. **Iterate the treatment, not the subject.** Wrist-tattoo sequence: v1 "Kodak Vision3 500T … real grain" cinema look (0ac791e0) → v2 "STOCK FOOTAGE TREATMENT", tripod, clean (93f7250a) → v3 "REPORTAGE TREATMENT", handheld (2dd6cacb, **used**) — all within 29 minutes. Hall: v1 (e17709ee) → v2 "CAMERA — HANDHELD BUT LEVEL" + "THE HALL — LARGE, WIDE, NOT DEEP" (ef13303b, **used**). Assembly: 84° wide gave fisheye (38d5e7c9) → "OPTICS LOCK … no wide-angle lens character" (de666950, **used twice**).
  7. **Carry continuity with re-uploaded approved clips.** The approved interview take d2e4bdb5 was re-uploaded (as media 54859518) and attached to the next two interview fragments; the approved riot clip 93b3b222 (→ cb789069) to its "continuation" shots; the nursery clip 63a1dabc (→ eb906cd1) to the wide nursery (re-uploads re-checked: frame correlation 1.0 with the originals). A 2048×870 frame grab of the drama's wrist barcode (3316a149) was attached to 4 hospital jobs (2dd6cacb, 63a1dabc and both hall takes e17709ee/ef13303b) but never tagged in any of their prompts; the two hall prompts do not mention the barcode at all. The `birthcode` Element sits on 4 jobs (0ac791e0, 93f7250a, 2dd6cacb, 63a1dabc). The wide-nursery jobs carry neither.
  8. **Post makes it "archive".** 4:3 pillarbox *inside* the 2.39:1 letterbox, slight punch-in, desaturation, lifted blacks, softness; jump cuts inside the interview; white sans subtitles carry a narration ("In 2050, the Birth Control Center was established for the regulation of the world's population. 10 billion. The maximum quota established by law to ensure the survival of the human species.") over the montage; the archive ends on a newborn's barcoded wrist and cuts straight into the drama.

- Asset method (characters / locations / props sheets): three-panel character sheets on grey (face + headless front + headless back), location plates without people, props as product shots on grey; each turned into a named Element; the Element is then described again in words inside every prompt and closed with "100% matches the reference" (421 of 443 Seedance prompts).

### The rules behind the look (each with the evidence)

1. **Fake the recording situation, not a filter.** Almost every archive prompt names in its SCENE CONTEXT who is filming and why ("Reportage footage from …", "Stock footage of …", "Aerial news footage …", "Covered by …"). The exceptions are the cinema-look v1 tattoo prompt 0ac791e0 and the night bonfire 1e2e7a94. Examples: "Covered by the chamber's own house cameras", "Covered by a news crew working inside it", "Broadcast pool footage: functional, unglamorous, recorded to document rather than to impress", "the making-of camera, not the finished interview". The camera body, mounting and operator behaviour follow from that sentence (gyro + airframe vibration for the helicopter; "constant fine tremor from grip and forearm tension … lags half a beat behind fast movement … small overshoot" for the shoulder camera; "4–8mm of operator breath and weight-shift" for the backstage camera).
2. **Ask Seedance for clean video, degrade in post.** 18 of 25 intro prompts say "No film grain"; 6 say "no VHS artefacts, no found-footage aesthetic"; the riot/aerial texture line is "broadcast video, not film. Clean sharpness in the centre of frame, slightly soft at the edges … compression breakup only during the fastest whips, a faint horizontal smear on strong motion". Only the two assembly prompts ask for analogue texture ("Standard-definition interlaced broadcast video, not film. Visible scanlines, light combing on moving edges … one brief horizontal tracking wobble … at 0:13"). The "old" feel in the film comes from the edit: 4:3 pillarbox, desaturation (×0.6–0.7), lifted blacks, softness, subtitles.
3. **Light it badly on purpose.** "Available light only, and clearly so … overhead fluorescent, uneven, slightly green, with poor falloff … Faces are lit badly the way faces in real institutions are lit badly"; "Exposure is set for the room average, so the overhead fixtures bloom"; "Exposure is ridden manually: the iris opens and closes visibly … one correction per shot, never instant"; "The light shifts SLIGHTLY between shots because the operator has moved … That inconsistency is correct."
4. **Imperfection is specified, counted and placed.** "Occasional focus hunting … one visible hunt and recovery per shot, landing within half a second"; "one slow operator reframe at around 0:05 … arriving a beat late"; "a brief focus hunt at 0:01"; "Exactly ONE brief glance toward the lens in the whole sequence, in Shot 4". Then a fix after early results: "CAMERA — HANDHELD BUT LEVEL … The reportage quality comes from movement, not from tilt" (no dutch angles).
5. **Generate rushes with hard cuts at exact timecodes, then steal 1–3 s.** "CONTROLLED MULTI-SHOT SEQUENCE, 15 seconds total. Four shots. HARD CUTS only at 0:05, 0:08 and 0:11"; "The five shots are edit points in the same rushes, not different sources". In the three assembly/square prompts (38d5e7c9, de666950, fc4a075a), each shot opens with "First frame already contains … No empty establishing frame, no reveal." (that exact phrase is in only those 3 prompts project-wide; "No empty establishing frame" is in 44; a FIRST FRAME line or block is in 405 of 443). The editor used a single shot (or two) from each generation and freely reordered them.
6. **Nobody acts.** "PERFORMANCE LOCK — NOBODY ACTS … This is being recorded, not staged. The waiting people are neutral and mildly bored. Not afraid, not resigned, not staring meaningfully"; delegates "react with nothing … covered like a routine budget session"; "WHAT IS HAPPENING — A ROUTINE HEALTH PROGRAMME". The horror is in the banality.
7. **Prevent clones by coverage design, not by asking.** "Instructing against it is not enough, so the coverage is built to make it impossible: NO FACE IN THIS SEQUENCE IS EVER RESOLVED … If no face is legible, no face can be repeated"; "the sequence shows DIFFERENT WRISTS rather than different faces"; "THE VIEW IS ALWAYS PARTLY BLOCKED"; "There is ALWAYS an out-of-focus foreground body"; "THE HALL READS LATERALLY, NOT IN DEPTH … a wall closes the depth"; cots "in IRREGULAR CLUSTERS"; aerial "AT ANY MOMENT ROUGHLY HALF THE GROUND IS OBSCURED" by smoke; "NO ARCHITECTURAL REPETITION … no receding corridor, no vanishing point". 14 intro prompts carry a NO DUPLICATION block.
8. **Optics as field-of-view degrees plus a lens check.** 84°, 47° and 29° "diagonal field of view" per shot, and "LENS CHECK SHOT B: 47° maintained". LENS CHECK appears only in the 3 assembly/square prompts and OPTICS LOCK only in de666950; FOV degrees appear in 250 Seedance prompts. The 84° wide (38d5e7c9) bent the chamber into a fisheye; the next prompt added "OPTICS LOCK ACROSS ALL CUTS … No wide-angle lens character is used anywhere … Wider coverage is achieved only by the camera being farther away, never by a shorter lens" and the interview got "OPTICS — RECTILINEAR, NO WIDE-ANGLE DISTORTION" with explicit straight-line checks.
9. **Sound is a numbered diegetic list; music is banned in its own block.** "NO MUSIC — ABSOLUTE … No drone, no pad, no sustained tone … Nothing timed to a cut"; "Room tone … Running CONTINUOUSLY across all six shots and every cut"; "NO SOUND ON THE CUTS: no sting, no impact, no whoosh"; aerial: "NOTHING FROM THE GROUND … Rotor noise is correct. Music is not."; interview: "Documentary production sound, close and dry, from the lavalier … the very faint high hiss of the mic preamp … No HVAC hum". All 25 intro prompts say no music and no subtitles; generate_audio was on for all (what survived in the mix I cannot hear).
10. **Interview realism = eyeline + rhythm + cut-in.** "His eyeline sits on the INTERVIEWER, just off-lens to screen-right … He NEVER looks into the lens"; "The first syllable of 'We' is clipped by the edit"; "this is the second of three consecutive fragments"; accent spelled phonetically ("even, syllable-timed rhythm … 'r' and 'l' softened toward each other … Never a caricature"); a rhythm map per sentence ("delivered briskly, almost impatiently … decelerates through the phrase and settles on the final word"); "ZERO GESTURES". In the edit the scripted pauses were then cut out with jump cuts — documentary editing on top of documentary acting.
11. **Kill every readable text and logo, allow one.** "No readable text anywhere in frame, no signage, no logos, no insignia" (38d5e7c9; 17 intro prompts carry some "no readable text" ban); the vote board is the single exception ("APPROVED … the only readable text in the entire sequence").
12. **Tie the archive to the fiction with one object.** The barcode is locked as an Element plus a frame grab from the drama: "BIRTHCODE LOCK — EXACTLY AS THE REFERENCE … NO NUMERALS … CODE ORIENTATION — PERPENDICULAR TO THE ARM". The intro ends on that object and the drama opens on the same world.

### Archive prompt skeleton (block order used from 5 Sep, in caps headers)
`SCENE CONTEXT` (what happens + who covers it) → `OUTPUT SETTINGS` / `FORMAT MODE` (n shots, hard cuts at timecodes, real time, no transitions, no subtitles/titles/music) → `<SOURCE> TREATMENT — THIS IS THE GOVERNING STYLE` (camera body + operator, lighting, colour, exposure, presence, FORBIDDEN) → `CAMERA — HANDHELD BUT LEVEL` → `NO MUSIC — ABSOLUTE` → `NO DUPLICATION — ABSOLUTE, AND THE METHOD FOR ACHIEVING IT` → object/geography locks (`BIRTHCODE LOCK`, `CODE ORIENTATION`, `SCREEN DIRECTION LOCK — NEVER CROSSED`, `OPTICS LOCK`) → `ACTIVE REFERENCES` (Element tokens, described in words) → `THE HALL` / `THE PEOPLE` (exact head-count, each one different) → `WHAT IS HAPPENING — A ROUTINE …` → `SHOT n — NAME (a to b)` … `HARD CUT.` → `PERFORMANCE LOCK — NOBODY ACTS` → `PHYSICS` → `LIGHTING` → `AUDIO` (numbered list) → `POSITIVE CONSTRAINTS` (everything restated as a checklist, texture line last).

### Post recipe I derived (my calibration on their frames, not their exact settings)
Raw 1664×1248 → scale to 720×540 and back (SD softness) → `eq=saturation=0.65:contrast=0.9:brightness=0.03` → light temporal noise (`noise=alls=6:allf=t`) → pad 4:3 inside the 2.39:1 band of a 16:9 master. On four test frames this lands saturation, contrast and black level within a few points of the film (e.g. de666950: film sat 42 / src 68 / mine 39; black p2 37 / 27 / 37). The film is softer still — scale through 480×360 if you want their softness. No VHS overlay, no fake timecode.

## Adopt for our pipeline (concrete rules)
1. **Archive module for Seedance 2.5:** write every archive/stock shot as a recording situation — first sentence "Covered by …" (house cameras / a news crew inside it / a helicopter pool camera / a backstage handheld / CCTV), then a `<SOURCE> TREATMENT — THIS IS THE GOVERNING STYLE` block with camera body, operator behaviour, available light, uncorrected colour, ridden exposure, presence, FORBIDDEN.
2. **Prompt clean, degrade in post.** In the prompt: "broadcast video, not film … No film grain, no VHS artefacts, no found-footage aesthetic, no colour grade". In post: 4:3 (or the source's native ratio) inside our 2.39 band, SD down/up-scale, saturation ×0.65, contrast ×0.9, lift blacks (+0.03), light temporal noise. Use analogue texture words (scanlines, interlace, tracking wobble at a timecode) only for one clearly older source.
3. **Write rushes, cut tight.** 2–6 shots per generation with HARD CUTS at exact timecodes and "First frame already contains …"; plan to keep 1–3 s per shot; reorder freely.
4. **Count every imperfection and give it a time:** one focus hunt per shot, one late reframe at ~0:05, one iris correction, horizon level ("movement, not tilt"), at most one glance at the lens per sequence.
5. **Anti-clone by layout** for any crowd, ward, office or city: no resolved faces, foreground soft body, occlusion, lateral depth with a closing wall, irregular clusters, smoke/haze covering part of the frame, explicit head-count with each person different.
6. **Optics in FOV degrees** (≈47° normal, ≈29° insert, ≈18° profile) plus "LENS CHECK"; never above ~70° for archive; add the rectilinear lock and two straight-line checks.
7. **Audio as a numbered diegetic list**, room tone continuous across cuts, "NO SOUND ON THE CUTS", a standalone "NO MUSIC — ABSOLUTE" block; interviews = lavalier close and dry + preamp hiss, no HVAC.
8. **Interview recipe:** unseen interviewer beside the lens, eyeline never in the lens, first syllable clipped, per-sentence rhythm map, accent described phonetically ("never a caricature"), ZERO GESTURES, chained fragments with the previous approved fragment attached; finish with jump cuts in the edit.
9. **Carry continuity with re-uploaded approved clips and frame grabs** — but always tag them with one role ("defines LOOK and VOICE only; do not copy its framing") and check every attached ref still has its tag before sending.
10. **Folder template:** `01_CHARACTER/{01_MAIN,02_SUPPORTING,UNUSED}`, `02_LOCATION/{INT_…,EXT_…,UNUSED}`, `03_PROP`, `04_FOOTAGE/{INTRO_<source type>,SCENE NN,SCENE NNA,END CREDITS}`, `05_MUSIC`, `06_SFX`; Elements named `char_ / loc_ / prop_` with a number for redesigns; rejects go to UNUSED, never deleted.
