# Study: Kimti (slug "bangle"), by ameerahmed (https://higgsfield.ai/@ameerahmed/projects/bangle), 2026-11-02

- **Source type:** Higgsfield project (prompts and canvas public) plus the finished film (3:49 = 229.5 s, 4:3 picture pillarboxed in a 16:9 master, burned-in English subtitles). It was entered in the Higgsfield Global Film Festival and has 4,230 views and 83 likes. The project holds 130 jobs (87 Soul Cinematic stills, 43 Seedance 2.5 videos) made between 25 Aug and 16 Sep 2026 for 2,452 credits. Final submission: 16 Sep 23:52 UTC.
- **What it achieves:** a period folk-horror short (1700s South India) that feels like one arthouse feature (*The Witch* / *Bramayugam* are named in the prompts). It has one consistent woman, one house, one forest and one calf, with restrained acting, real-looking animals (hens, calf, bulls), believable everyday chores, and a dread that comes from darkness and stillness rather than effects. Most of it was made in four days (13–16 Sep: 39 of 43 video jobs) from a small number of generations.
- **Method step by step:**
  1. **Concept phase (25–31 Aug, 20 Soul stills + 4 Seedance clips).** The tone was tried on Soul Cinematic with Cinema-Studio camera presets (Modern / 8mm / 35mm body, *Halation Vintage* lens, *Warm Rustic Glow* grade) and an earlier lead (a young woman with loose wavy hair in a beige linen dress, image `ad6f2aae`). The night departure (bull, lantern, cart) and the creature's belled hooves (`c609b70a`) were generated then. Two of those early clips are still in the final cut (bull close-up, belled ankles), hidden by the black-and-white grade.
  2. **Lock the cast once (12 Sep, outside this project).** A Soul portrait test (`18c8f444`, "potrait image of indian women wearing saree n 1700s in white background", Twilight Fable preset) was followed by a 3-image character sheet on a neutral warm-grey backdrop, all 896×1200: front face `9af68a87`, full-body costume `cfffbbe5` (off-white handwoven sari with a muted red border, bindi, low bun) and profile `ff3e46db`. The file names are Higgsfield generations from 22:45–22:56 that sit outside this project. The 896×1200 size suggests Nano Banana Pro, but the data does not name the model. From then on, the face image rides along in 21 of 43 video jobs and the costume image in 15.
  3. **One Soul "plate" per setup, then swap the actor in.** Each new location or beat starts as a long Soul Cinematic prompt (production plates 84–451 words, median 154 per kept still; 4 variants per run, keep 1–14). The chosen still is downloaded. Where the lead appears, it is usually edited so that the locked woman replaces the Soul woman with the composition unchanged. Examples: courtyard plate `ccd69354` became `38023dd9` (1200×896), and the sleeping shot `64670765` became `bce5e1a9` with the candle removed. Not always: the hair-tying still `76872a71` went in unchanged as `d80a4e7b`, and the face sheet was attached as a second image instead (`1c487453`). The result is re-uploaded as a Seedance start frame. 14 of the 27 reference images trace back to the project's own Soul stills (11 re-uploaded unchanged at 1536×1152, 3 edited or resized: `38023dd9`, `bce5e1a9`, and `53597dfe` = `71ba087d` resized to 1168×880).
  4. **Animate with Seedance 2.5 at 4:3.** Three prompt registers are used. (a) 8–28-word one-liners when the start frame already carries everything ("handheld shot of women sleeping , no zoom in,"). (b) 80–350-word single-shot prompts with timed micro-actions and hard counts ("exactly four birds total"). (c) 300–1,800-word LLM-written multi-shot sequences, mostly 20–30 s but also 8–15 s (`99856c7c` 13 s / 5 shots, `2f7ea882` 15 s / 6 shots, `adea2114` 9 s) (SHOT 1…4 blocks, PERFORMANCE / CAMERA / VISUAL TONE / Negative prompt sections). Each long job is mined for several cuts: one 30 s job (`ba91994d`) supplies 22.5 s of the film.
  5. **Switch to 1080p once the look is set.** All 15 video jobs of 13 Sep were 720p; from 14 Sep on, 21 of 24 were 1080p. 14 of the 21 1080p jobs made the cut, against 9 of 22 at 720p. Keepers were not re-rendered at 1080p: the only 720p prompt re-run at 1080p (`990e40b2` → `516d8d63`, same text, refs reordered) was not used, and the 720p take stayed in the film. Draft mode was never used, and there was no upscaler.
  6. **Fix in place rather than regenerate.** One `video_edit` changed only the pace of an existing clip ("instead of walking slow she should walk very fast to the box,do not change video structure"). One forward `video_extension` (8 s) continued it with a new beat ("she suddenly hears a faint, unsettling voice off-screen…"). An existing clip was also used as a video reference for a reaction continuation (`b797229c`: "Use the provided video reference for exact continuity. Same woman, same room…").
  7. **Post does the heavy lifting:** a black-and-white grade for the prologue (folk-tale narration over the goldsmith making the bangle) and the night climax, with warm muted colour for the day. Other tools: cross-dissolve from the rainy-night POV into the sleeping woman, black holds carrying subtitled lines ("[Husband] Take care, okay?"), slow-down/hold of a 2 s extension into about 6 s, crop-ins on 4:3 renders, a lightning flash montage built from sub-0.5 s cuts (12 of the film's 19 cuts under 0.5 s fall in 3:32–3:40), and a still image (Soul `ff333424`, a burning bull/cart) under the title card. Dialogue and narration exist only as subtitles over shots where no mouth is seen speaking (black, backs, POV, wides, eye-only close-ups such as "[gasp] Break this curse!" over her eyes). No Seedance prompt asks for lip-sync; 8 say "no dialogue" (2 of them also "no lip movement"), and 2 more forbid understandable or clear dialogue from the distant voices. I could not hear the audio, so the voices and score are unverified.
- **Prompt structure:** see "Prompt anatomy" below. Soul stills: scene → subject/action → props → light → palette/film texture → lens/composition → "No/Avoid" list. Seedance: context line (period, place, mood, film references) → identity preservation → set description → numbered SHOTs with size/angle/lens and one action each → PERFORMANCE (a list of "does not…") → CAMERA (static, no whip pans) → VISUAL TONE → SFX-only sound → Negative prompt.
- **Asset method (characters / locations / props):** one 3-view character sheet on a neutral backdrop; one Soul plate per location (courtyard `ccd69354`, interior `6faa28c0` and `72e7a1d7`, calf clearing `71317c1b`, Aghori camp `4984c8dc`, rainy road `d395982d`, bull `f3b9aadb`); props invented inside plates (brass plates, bangle box, wall-niche lamp). There are no prop sheets, and the bangle is designed only in the inserts. Animals are kept consistent through a plate plus counted descriptions (two adult brown hens + two dark chicks), not through sheets.
- **Adopt:** see "Adopt for our pipeline" at the end.
- **Do not copy:** the woman's design (sari, bindi, face), the bull curse / bangle fable, the Aghori ritual and the emaciated ascetic, the belled-hoof creature, the "Kimti" title treatment, and any line of narration. Take the method only.
- **Proposed skill changes:** listed at the end. Copy them to LESSONS.md as "proposed".

## How so few generations made a strong film (lean-production analysis)

1. **The project is curated, not complete.** About 29% of the picture time (62.9 s) does not match any job in the project. That covers the goldsmith montage 0:05–0:31, three rainy-night shots, the woman tying the cow, the window/dead-calf shots, two dark-room shots and the husband lightning flashes. The reference media also show other Higgsfield generations made outside the folder (character sheet, start frames, three source videos). So "130 jobs" is the curated subset. Even so, 23 of the 43 video jobs (53%) are in the film, and they cover 148 s of picture. [INFERRED: the 62.9 s / 148 s split is approximate (±4 s); it depends on how black frames are counted, and 3:12–3:14 may be the end of `b797229c` (her turning away), not outside material.]
2. **Expensive decisions are made in the cheap medium.** Composition, light, wardrobe and set are settled on Soul Cinematic stills (0 credits shown on this account; 4 variants per run). Seedance receives a finished frame plus the locked face. The video prompt then only has to describe motion, which is why five 8–28-word prompts all reached the final cut.
3. **Long multi-shot jobs are mined for coverage.** Eight 20–30 s jobs in the cut supply 76.6 s: `ba91994d` 22.5 s (about 7 shots), `3af69ac7` 20.6 s, `990e40b2` 13.4 s (about 8 shots), `c1fef8d1` 7.9 s, `cd72318c` 3.8 s, `c609b70a` 3.8 s, `b01a9eef` 2.4 s, `ceaf5f80` 2.2 s. One brief, written as SHOT blocks, produces a whole scene's coverage in matching light.
4. **Scenes are designed to avoid AI failure modes.** There is no lip-sync (V.O. and subtitles), the husband is never seen, the creature shows only its legs, and the intruder only his back. Cameras are static or a gentle handheld sway: 30 of 43 prompts say static / locked / fixed / stationary, 13 say no zoom. Acting is internal: 23 prompts carry a restrained / no exaggerated / no horror acting clause. Each of these choices removes a way a shot can fail.
5. **The grade forgives continuity.** The B/W bookends absorb clips from two different eras of the project (the 30–31 Aug bull and creature legs, made with the earlier lead, next to the 14–16 Sep clips), different colour grades and the external goldsmith footage.
6. **Fix rather than reshoot.** A pace correction (`video_edit`), a beat extension (`video_extension`) and a video-referenced reaction (`b797229c`) gave about 18 s of the chest/intruder scene. A 2 s stretch of the extension was slowed and held in the edit.
7. **Edit with holds, black and crop-ins.** The film reuses one take several times (`580b6f24` is intercut three times), holds a 20 s POV, and puts lines over black. The narration carries the plot, so the images only need mood and gesture.

## Prompt anatomy

### Soul Cinematic image prompts (87 stills, 33 distinct prompts)
- **Settings:** 81/87 at 1536×1152 (4:3, "720p"), 5 at 2048×1536 and 1 portrait 1152×1536. `batch_size` 4 on 84, `enhance_prompt` on for all 87, style "General" for all 87. Cinema-Studio presets appear on only 18 early jobs (27 Aug–12 Sep): camera Modern 9 / 8mm Film 5 / 35mm Film 2, lens *Halation Vintage* 14, colour *Warm Rustic Glow* 9 / *Twilight Fable* 1. Presets were dropped after 12 Sep, but two preset plates still fed the final film: the interior `6faa28c0` (27 Aug, Modern + Warm Rustic Glow) via `9cc263f7` into `ceaf5f80` / `b01a9eef`, and the sleeping shot `64670765` (31 Aug, Halation Vintage) via `bce5e1a9` into `bd9ebba6`, as well as the 30 Aug hooves plate `503ea0f1` (35mm Film + Halation Vintage) into `c609b70a`. Every plate made from 13 Sep on carries its look in the words alone.
- **Two registers.** (a) Throwaway search prompts of 6–26 words ("a market in india from 1700", "bull cattle buring in rain in forest 1700s india", "gold bangles on  a table in dark room only very less lights"). These are used to fish for an image, and one of them (`ff333424`) became the title still. (b) Production plates of 84–451 words (median 154 across the 60 stills they produced, 200 across the 24 distinct prompts; the 113 median quoted elsewhere covers all 87 stills, search prompts included) written as film stills.
- **Block order of a production plate:** `[shot type + genre anchor]` ("STRICT EXTERIOR, MORNING, STRAIGHT-ON FRONT VIEW. Cinematic period film still set in 1700s rural India.") → `[subject + exact action]` ("Her left hand steadies a shallow brass plate while her right palm scrubs its surface with fine earth dampened into a thin cleaning paste") → `[props listed by material]` → `[background life, small and placed]` ("a brown hen and two chicks peck… several metres behind the woman") → `[wardrobe]` → `[light by clock and quality]` ("Soft 9 a.m. daylight filtered through a thin veil of cloud") → `[palette]` ("Dusty ochre earth, faded cream, subdued foliage") → `[lens / composition / film texture]` ("Straight-on medium-wide composition, 35mm lens… subtle film grain") → `Avoid:` list.
- **Recurring anchors (count of 87):** "1700s" 79, "cinematic" 57, "35mm" 41, "film grain" 38, "photoreal" 36, "period" 33, "shallow depth of field" 30, "no modern…" 29, "prestige" 16, "halation" 15. Darkness is specified by *where the light stops*: "only a small candlelight glow reveals fragments of the room… most of the room remains in deep black shadow", "warm amber glow fading fast into deep brown-black shadow, no red light, no colored light, only natural firelight".
- **Composition is told as camera placement, not adjectives:** "Camera directly in front of her, centred on her body. Her shoulders, torso, and knees face the camera"; "Intimate medium-close three-quarter shot from slightly behind and beside her shoulder… her face is simultaneously visible in the mirror"; "Tight 50–65mm composition, close camera placement, limited headroom".
- **Negatives are specific to the failure seen:** "no bed frame, no printed quilts, no pillows with covers, no party lights, no bokeh light circles… eyes never open"; "no complete canopy arch, no roof-dominant composition, no symmetrical tunnel framing"; "There is no visible candle, no oil lamp, and no direct flame in the scene" (v2 of the ascetic prompt, which removes the candle that v1 asked for).
- **Identity is not handled in Soul.** No Soul job has an image input. "The same woman" in Soul prompts is aspirational, and identity is fixed afterwards by an image edit (see Consistency).

### Seedance 2.5 video prompts (43 jobs)
- **Settings:** `mode` default 41, `video_edit` 1, `video_extension` 1 (forward). Durations: 5 s ×12, 20 s ×6, 30 s ×6, 8 s ×4, 4/6/9 s ×3 each, 15 s ×2, 7/13/23/27 s ×1. Resolution 720p 22, 1080p 21. Aspect 4:3 38, 16:9 5. `generate_audio` true 43/43, `draft` false 43/43, `multi_shots` false 43/43 (cuts are written in the text), `bitrate_mode` high, `genre` auto, `speedramp` auto, no seed.
- **Length:** median 325 words (min 8, max 1,791). Buckets: ≤20 words 5 jobs, 21–120 10, 121–500 16, >500 12. **Hit rate in the final cut: ≤20 words 5/5, 21–120 5/10, 121–500 6/16, >500 7/12.** (The ≤20 bucket includes the 16-word `video_edit`; the 28-word one-liner `334e243d` falls in 21–120. Every prompt of 28 words or fewer, 6 of 6, made the cut.) Short prompts win when a finished start frame exists; long prompts win for multi-shot coverage.
- **Three registers:**
  1. *One-liner on a finished start frame* (8–28 words): `[camera behaviour] + [subject/action] + [one guard]`: "handheld shot of gold makig, static, no music"; "static handheld shot, no zoom in, dead man's hand". "Static handheld" means a locked frame with a breathing sway.
  2. *Single shot, 80–350 words:* duration and shot type up front ("5-second continuous shot, locked camera, preserving the reference composition and natural daylight"), one action with **timestamps** ("Around 2 seconds, she pauses, briefly scratches the side of her head… resumes cleaning by 3.5 seconds"), background life, hard counts ("exactly four birds total… no birds appear, disappear, duplicate, or change size"), physics ("Realistic weight, balance, toe movement and foot contact… grounded shadow… connected to its feet"), sound line, then a `No …` line.
  3. *Multi-shot sequence, 300–1,800 words, LLM-written:* context line with film references ("with the serious visual mood of The Witch and Bramayugam") → identity preservation ("Preserve the woman’s exact facial identity, skin tone, hairstyle, bindi, body proportions, and sari from the reference images") → emotional back-story in one sentence ("She has just come back from the forest after seeing something deeply disturbing… but she does not outwardly perform fear") → set → `SHOT 1 — DRINKING WATER` … `SHOT 4` (each with size, angle, one action, and how the emotion shows physically: "The tension is only in the speed of drinking") → `PERFORMANCE DIRECTION` (a "She does not:" list) → `CAMERA DIRECTION` ("Exactly four distinct cinematic shots with clean cuts… No whip pans, no orbiting camera…") → `VISUAL TONE` → `Negative prompt` (comma list: "overacting, horror acting, screaming, crying, looking around… obvious AI look").
- **Reference naming:** no `<<<image_N>>>` and no `@` tags in any prompt. References are named by **ordinal** in 12 prompts ("Use the second reference for the adult woman’s appearance… the first reference for the calf’s appearance… the third reference for the distant forest composition"; "references one and two… reference three… reference four"), by role ("second image is her face referance"), or generically ("matching the reference image exactly", "Use the provided video reference for exact continuity"). **10 of the 12 ordinal prompts do not match the attachments.** `968a085a`, `3a3f9fb3` and `990e40b2` attach [courtyard plate, calf plate, face, costume], so "second = woman" actually points at the calf and "first = calf" at the courtyard. `516d8d63` [face, costume, calf, courtyard] and `adea2114` [face, calf, costume] reuse the same wording. The hen prompts `ed4f5dd8`, `56ddaf88` and `ec8831f4` say "matching the second image's close side view" with only one image attached. `056863fe` and `63dfe060` describe a "fourth reference" with three attached. Only `334e243d` and `1c487453` ("second image is her face") are correct. Three of the mismatched jobs (`990e40b2`, `adea2114`, `ec8831f4`) still made the film, because each image is unambiguous by content (a face sheet, a calf, a courtyard) and Seedance bound them by what they show. That luck should not be relied on.
- **Anti-slideshow clause** for multi-image jobs (`63dfe060`): "Treat the supplied images as visual references for character appearance, forest atmosphere, and lighting only. Create new compositions and natural movement; do not reproduce the reference images as fixed opening frames, animate them one after another, or transition between them as a slideshow."
- **Camera language:** "static" / "locked" / "locked-off tripod" / "fixed camera" / "stationary shot" in 30 of 43 (28 with static/locked alone); "handheld" 14 (usually qualified: "gentle handheld breathing sway", "Very subtle handheld sway only, no pans, no zooms"); a lens focal length in only 2, both the unused cow-tying jobs `3f385c25` / `9ff89f73` ("40mm lens… Tight 85mm close-up"), while "35mm" as film grain or texture ("35mm film grain", "35mm film texture") appears in 9; "long rear telephoto shot down the forest path… Tall trunks compress the space"; a POV move written as physics ("As the cart continuously drives forward, the camera actively pulls away from her, rapidly increasing the distance"). Bans: "No whip pans, no orbiting camera, no dramatic push-ins, no aggressive handheld shake, no Dutch angles, no rapid zooms."
- **Performance language (the core of the realism):** every emotion is moved into one physical behaviour, and everything else is forbidden. "The only obvious indication of panic is the way she drinks the water quickly"; "Quiet grief, realistic: reddened wet eyes, , chin trembling slightly, lips pressed together, a small nod goodbye. No sobbing, no dramatic crying". The pasted LLM note explains why: "you need both the positive and the negative or Seedance will either skip the tear or go full weeping". Eating that slows "piece by piece until he is completely motionless… He does not stop suddenly." 23 of 43 prompts carry a restraint clause.
- **Background life with continuity** (`d754e799`, not used but instructive): "Background characters must have logical trajectories and continuity between cuts. Do not randomly regenerate different numbers of sheep, goats, cows or villagers in every shot." "Nobody exists just to decorate the frame."
- **Darkness control:** "The frame begins EMPTY… for the first seconds nothing happens"; "Make the forest substantially darker than the references… Foreground trunks are near-black silhouettes"; "Do not brighten the room"; "vast black negative space, tiny localized amber flame… no fill light".
- **Sound:** in the late prompts, a closing `SFX only, absolutely no music…` line (3 prompts: `580b6f24`, `adea2114`, `2f7ea882`; 10 prompts say "no music" in some form) listing 6–12 concrete sounds and how they evolve ("Near the end, the chewing and tearing should gradually slow and fade… not stop abruptly"). Other forms: "Plate scraping transitions to footsteps, hoofbeats, faint wind" as a sound bridge between cuts, and distance-dependent voices ("the voices never become clear or loud during the inserts").
- **Moderation-aware rewrites:** the Aghori scene was reworded after a flag. The pasted LLM preamble says so: "This version keeps the scare while simplifying appearance descriptions and making clothing explicit. The flag could also come from the reference or generated frames, so acceptance isn’t guaranteed." The changes: "fictional, unexplained ceremony", "plain off-white garments covering their torsos and legs… no jewellery", and "Their appearance is human and realistic".
- **LLM residue left in prompts:** 4 prompts contain chat text (`cd72318c` contains the creator's own correction "instead of imagine this, shot will start with closeup of the bull…" plus the LLM's "Reordered… Copyable prompt:"; `c609b70a` contains three alternative angle prompts and a "Shared craft" note; `0ad9f3e3` has the tear-direction note; `ba91994d` has the moderation note). Seedance tolerated this, and `c609b70a` produced a used shot. It is still sloppy and wastes tokens.
- **Edit / extend prompts are one sentence of change plus a guard:** `video_edit`: "…she should walk very fast to the box,do not change video structure". `video_extension` forward (8 s, with the start image and face as extra refs): "she suddenly hears a faint, unsettling voice off-screen… Static camera, restrained performance, no gimmicks". Video-ref continuation: "Use the provided video reference for exact continuity. Same woman, same room, same lighting and same position."

## Consistency system (how one woman, one house and one look survived)

1. **Character = one 3-view sheet on a neutral backdrop** (front face `9af68a87`, full-body costume `cfffbbe5`, profile `ff3e46db`; 896×1200 each). Face and/or costume go into most Seedance jobs that show her from 13 Sep on (face in 21 of 43 jobs, costume in 15). Not all: several rely on an identity-corrected or already-correct start frame alone (`d058dc74` courtyard, `bd9ebba6` sleeping, `3af69ac7` rain POV, `b797229c` / `70e9c97f` video inputs), and `334e243d`, `1c487453`, `93146d11`, `e3828082` carry the face without the costume. The text repeats the costume in one fixed phrase: "cream sari with muted red border" / "off-white sari with muted red border" (11 Seedance prompts).
2. **Plates carry the location; edits carry the identity.** The reference chain:
   - Soul `ccd69354` (courtyard, 451-word prompt) → edited with the locked woman → `38023dd9` (1200×896) → start/ref for 14 video jobs: hens `ec8831f4`, scrubbing `d058dc74`, chase `990e40b2`, calf feeding `0ea07f68` … (4 of them in the film: `0ea07f68`, `ec8831f4`, `d058dc74`, `990e40b2`).
   - Soul `71317c1b` (calf in clearing) → `51167685` → 9 jobs (calf identity + clearing geography).

## Adopt for our pipeline (concrete rules)

1. **Decide composition and light on stills, pay for motion only.** For every shot: approve a 4:3 or 16:9 still (Soul Cinematic or our image route), then fix identity with an image edit that swaps in the locked character without moving the camera. Only then go to Seedance 2.5.
2. **Match the video prompt length to the job.** On an approved start frame: 8–40 words (action + camera behaviour + one guard, e.g. `static`, `no zoom in`, `in same angle`). For one shot with acting: 80–350 words with timestamps. For scene coverage: a 20–30 s multi-shot of 300–800 words, then cut 3–7 shots out of it.
3. **One character sheet, attached every time she is on screen:** face front plus full-body costume (plus profile when the angle needs it), with the costume repeated as one fixed phrase in every prompt.
4. **Write emotion as one physical tell plus a "does not" list.** Use the pair "positive + negative" for tears and fear (Kimti: reddened wet eyes and a slightly trembling chin, then "No sobbing, no dramatic crying").
5. **Design dialogue out of lip-sync where the film allows:** V.O. over backs, POVs, darkness and inserts. Write "No dialogue. No lip movement." into the Seedance prompts for those shots and record or generate the voice in post.
6. **Count the animals and extras and fix their trajectories** ("exactly four birds total", "calf always leading", "Do not randomly regenerate different numbers of sheep…").
7. **Name references by token and role, not by ordinal,** and verify the attachment order before submitting. Kimti's ordinals were wrong in 10 of the 12 prompts that used them. Add the anti-slideshow clause whenever there are three or more image refs.
8. **Find the look at 720p, then generate new shots at 1080p** (bitrate high, audio on), and expect about a 4:1 generated-to-used ratio. Kimti did not re-render keepers: its one 1080p re-run of a 720p prompt (`516d8d63`) went unused, so re-rendering keepers at 1080p is our own proposal, not Kimti's practice.
9. **Use `video_edit` for tempo or performance fixes and `video_extension` for the next beat** before regenerating, each with a one-line change plus "do not change video structure". Re-attach the face sheet to extensions.
10. **Plan a unifying post grade** (Kimti: B/W bookends) so clips from different sessions still cut together, and keep one aspect ratio for the whole film.
11. **Strip LLM chat residue** (preambles, "Copyable prompt:", alternatives) before pasting. Keep one prompt per job.
