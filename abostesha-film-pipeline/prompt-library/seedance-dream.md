### TEMPLATE (from studies 2026-11-02) · Re-resolution transition (place A becomes place B inside one take)
- Source: U (MANIFESTATION LAW in 35 prompts, 13 of the 28 final jobs; A→B mapping lists of 4–7 pairs; the subject anchored at fixed floor coordinates; 4–21 tries per transition). U's own adopt note names our S01 pool-dream crowd morph (S01-SH04, soft at about 17.5 s): start the ladder 4–5 s before the beat.
```text
【Generation Goal】
Generate one continuous {{shot size}} shot in which {{place A}} becomes {{place B}} around {{NAME}}, who stays where she is.

【Reference Asset Roles】
@Image1 is the STARTING LOCATION {{LOC_A}}: geometry, materials and light; do not import its framing.
@Image2 is the TARGET LOCATION {{LOC_B}}: a full environment the viewer stands inside; it resolves around {{NAME}}, never as a flat picture.
@Image3 is {{NAME}}: identity only.
These references are knowledge, not shots.

【Subjects and Relationships】
ANCHOR: {{NAME}} stays at the same floor position and does not transform; she is the fixed reference while the world moves.
LAW: every existing surface itself becomes the next surface. Nothing fades in, dissolves, assembles from particles or emerges from smoke: each new thing is already in its final place and size, only unresolved. Change runs in visible discrete steps, large masses → edges → material → fine detail, each step smaller than the last. Contact shadows arrive one step late. Hands and faces resolve last. Deep shadows never finish resolving.
MAPPING: {{A1}} becomes {{B1}}; {{A2}} resolves into {{B2}}; {{A3}} becomes {{B3}}; {{A4}} becomes {{B4}}.

【Event Script】
0-{{t0}} seconds: Frame one is already running: {{place A, in motion}}.
{{t0}}-{{t1}} seconds: masses shift; {{t1}}-{{t2}} seconds: edges; {{t2}}-{{t3}} seconds: material; {{t3}}-{{t4}} seconds: fine detail — all inside the first two-thirds of the take.
{{t4}}-{{G}} seconds: {{place B}}; the action continues.
The camera is a late witness: it notices, turns, and returns to {{NAME}}.

Audio includes <{{one material sound on the change beat}}>, <{{the room resonance changing}}>. No whoosh, no glitch sound. No music of any kind.
【Maintain Consistency】
{{NAME}} unchanged; no fade, no dissolve, no crossfade, no overlay, no second image underneath, no airborne particles, no dust cloud.
```

### TEMPLATE (from studies 2026-11-02) · Object carousel (one object keeps changing what it is)
- Source: U (5 door handles, 7 desks, 10 lights, 11 hand-held objects; "The left chair never changes"; a small spark or flare on each change; "After the seventh state, stop").
```text
The {{object}} at {{position}} keeps the same footprint while it becomes, in order: {{identity 1}} → {{identity 2}} → {{identity 3}} → {{identity 4}}. Each change takes about one second in four states: fine detail disappears; material disappears; the silhouette collapses into an unresolved mass of the same size; the new silhouette and material resolve in the same place. One small physical tell on each change: {{a brief flare in the flame | one tiny spark at the edge}}. The {{control object}} never changes. After the {{fourth}} state, stop.
```

### TEMPLATE (from studies 2026-11-02) · Collapse to black, then generate from black
- Source: U ("the active light solution abruptly ceases to exist. Do not fade the exposure … the window … GENERATES. It does not fade in."). For a DREAM ending or a wake-up beat.
```text
At {{t}} s the light in the room stops existing at once — not a fade of exposure. The room loses all readable structure into darkness. Then {{the new element: a window}} generates: first a shallow structural depression in the wall, then its edges, then its material, then light through it. It does not fade in.
```
