## Templates from the 2026-11-02 film studies

Source: `studies/2026-11-02_CROSS-STUDY.md` §3A.3. Fill-in templates, not approved generations. Product shots on neutral grey were used by 9/12 films; one image per prop state by F and B. Film codes as in characters.md.

### TEMPLATE (from studies 2026-11-02) · Hero prop → `views`
- Sources: T E1 (spindle, in 25 Seedance jobs), B prop header, 03 §3.1.7 (grey #8A8A8A, shadowless per our plate laws).
```text
{{PRP_CODE}} · {{NAME}} — {{view}}, {{state}} · {{1:1 | 4:3}}
A product photograph of a single {{object}}, isolated on a seamless neutral grey #8A8A8A background,
plain and empty, soft even shadowless light, even exposure, true-to-life colour.
The object: {{dimensions in mm}}, {{material and how it was made}}, {{wear: where hands have
darkened/smoothed it}}, {{construction: seams, hinges, fasteners}}.
{{THE DETAIL THAT MUST SURVIVE, with a scale comparison: "the single thread itself is sewing-weight …
fine enough to tie and knot"}}.
The object lies {{at a slight diagonal}}, fully visible and sharp from end to end, filling about two
thirds of the frame. Plain unmarked {{material}}; the only marking is {{exact fictional text in
quotes | a blank recess}}. Only the {{object}} on the grey background, nothing else in frame.
```
- A prop image overrides the text when they disagree (S: the whistle's colour followed the prop image). Fix the image, not the wording.
