### TEMPLATE (from studies 2026-11-02) · Coverage angle, eye-level angle or same-camera phase → `angle-<n>`
- Source: H E4 (34 coverage prompts) and H phase plates.
```text
Use the uploaded master reference as the exact same location. {{Camera approximately N cm above the
floor beside {{object}}, close to the eye level of {{subject}} | Camera positioned {{where / height /
direction}}}}. {{What dominates the frame and what space is left for the action}}.
Preserve the exact architecture, room dimensions, wall positions, doors, windows, ceiling height,
floor materials, furniture, fixtures, practical lighting sources and all spatial relationships.
Do not redesign, reinterpret, simplify or improve the location. This must be the exact same physical
place viewed only from a different camera position. Objects that logically remain fixed stay in
exactly the same places. Only change the camera position, camera height and viewing direction.
```

### TEMPLATE (from studies 2026-11-02) · Blocking stills made from an approved plate
- Sources: F E4 (pencil sketch, in the final Sc_04), C (≈80 marker-still prompts), L (overhead floor-plan Element). Use for locked or dialogue shots instead of a playblast (proposal P04-2).
```text
PENCIL SKETCH: Convert this image into a simple, loosely drawn pencil storyboard sketch. Preserve the
original composition, camera angle, perspective, character pose, and placement of objects. Keep the
face indistinct, without detailed facial features.
  → In Seedance: "@Image{{n}} is a POSE SKETCH: pose and spatial reference only; its lines, strokes and
    paper texture must not enter the image."

MARKER STILL: Keep this plate exactly as it is. Add flat, simple stick figures as markers: a RED figure
at {{position}} facing {{direction}}, a YELLOW figure at {{position}}. Markers are flat diagrams, not people.
  → In Seedance: "@Image{{n}} is a BLOCKING STILL: the RED marker is {{CHR}}, the YELLOW marker is {{CHR}};
    the coloured markers are annotations, not objects; framing comes from the prompt."

FLOOR PLAN: A clean overhead floor plan of {{place}}: rooms labelled {{…}}, furniture outlines,
doors, a camera icon at each named camera position {{1–n}}, north arrow. Flat diagram, white lines on grey.
```
