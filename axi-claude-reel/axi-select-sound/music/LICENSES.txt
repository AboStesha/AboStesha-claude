2. Material created for this score (no third-party rights): the composition itself (theme, harmony, arrangement, timing),
   the synthesised parts in src/lib.py - the D-A drone (additive band-limited saw stacks), the heartbeat sub pulse, the watch
   ticks, the sub drops, the noise risers, the reverse-reverb swells, the three synthetic hall/room impulse responses -
   and all mixing/processing. No AI music service, no library music track, nothing from the old HeyGen track.
