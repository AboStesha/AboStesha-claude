## How it was made

Higgsfield Katana:
- **Locations:** stills made with Soul Cinema.
- **Shots of Rojin:** generated with Seedance 2.5 at 1080p from her photos (same face, grey suit, red tablet).
- **The water and mark scene:** built in code over the generated flats location. Its first generated version was refused by the content filter, and the refund was confirmed.
- **Code:** the Arabic typography (Alexandria), graphics, camera moves, grade and end card.
- **Cuts:** locked to the Academy ad's beat at 128 BPM.
