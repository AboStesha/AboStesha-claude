## Brand and compliance

- **Brand guide:** colours, the logo, the slash and the triangle, the safe zones and the watermark follow the Axi Iraq Brand Guidelines.
- **Voice:** the narration is in formal Arabic (fusha), as the guide requires for ads.
- **Typeface:** Alexandria stands in for Rabar, which was not available. Digits are set in Inter Light.
- **Numbers:** all three come from axi.com (29 ms average execution, spreads from 16 cents on gold, Axi Select funding up to $1M). The ad promises no profit.

## How it was made

- **Voice and lip sync:** HeyGen, using the Arafat voice and the user's Saeed avatar.
- **Locations:** three stills (grey studio, Tigris at dusk, rock arch) made with Higgsfield Soul Cinema.
- **Saeed's shots:** generated with Seedance 2.5 at 1080p from his photos and the location stills.
- **Code:** the transitions, Arabic typography, number counters, cards, grade and end card.
- **Sound and cuts:** the official Axi sting, the Axi brand-film score and recorded sound effects. Cuts follow the score's beat (129 BPM) and the spoken words.
