## Story

Ali is alone at 3 AM in Baghdad, staring at a red chart. Four legendary mentors appear in gold light and sit beside him, one by one:

- Wall Street experience
- Tokyo patience
- London precision
- the wisdom of Baghdad's merchants

Their knowledge flows into one place: Claude, connected to his Axi account. By morning he asks, Claude analyses, and he decides with confidence. The end card reads **ما راح تتداول وحدك.**, followed by the CTA **سجّل هسه**.

The mentors are fictional archetypes, not real people. The end card carries the CFD risk warning and states that Claude does not give financial advice.
