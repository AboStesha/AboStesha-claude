## Brand and compliance

- **Brand guide:** colours, the logo, the slash and the triangle, the safe zones and the watermark follow the Axi Iraq Brand Guidelines.
- **Voice:** the narration is in formal Arabic (fusha). Only "twenty-nine milliseconds" and "Claude" are in English, as requested.
- **Typeface:** Alexandria stands in for Rabar, which was not available. Digits and Latin text are set in Inter.
- **Numbers:** all three come from axi.com (29 ms average execution, spreads from 16 cents on gold, Axi Select funding up to $1M). The ad promises no profit.
- **App screens:** drawn in code in the brand palette to illustrate the features. They are not screenshots of the real app, the trader names are fictional, and the screens show no performance figures.
- **Claude link:** it reflects Axi's MT5 MCP server for Claude, which is in alpha.
- **Risk warning:** none in this version, at your request. Confirm with Axi compliance before running it as a paid ad.

## How it was made

- **Voice and lip sync:** HeyGen, with the same Arafat voice and the user's Saeed avatar.
  - The voice now runs through the new ElevenLabs account; no new voice was created.
  - The new lines are one new take in that voice, with a new lip-sync shot of Saeed.
- **Locations and Saeed's shots:** the same Higgsfield Soul Cinema stills and Seedance 2.5 shots as v2. No new Higgsfield credits were spent on v3.
- **Code:** the transitions, Arabic typography, counters and cards, plus:
  - the app UI composited onto the phone screen;
  - the globe and the Claude link;
  - the CTA and the grade.
- **Sound and cuts:**
  - the official Axi sting and outro sound;
  - the Axi brand-film score, re-cut on its beat (129 BPM) so the drops land on the tap, the globe, the Claude link and the CTA;
  - recorded sound effects.
