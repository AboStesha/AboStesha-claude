Read `../BRIEF.md` first (brand, type, Arabic rules, verified facts). Scene times come from `timeline.json`
(generated by `../tools/timeline.py` from `script_src.json`; each scene's `word_times` estimates when each VO word is

table (paste them in, no fetch from file://)** and key every reveal to the word that names it, so the picture lands

## Concept — «خط الصعود» / The climb line

One continuous **red price line** is the spine of the whole film, and the camera travels along it through one dark
world: a black ground with the deck's faint red tick dot-grid far behind (`OM.lattice`, depth 1.2) and blurred
candle charts in depth. Each chapter is a station on the line, laid out left→right and climbing (the line is a
rising chart). Between chapters the camera glides or whips along the line to the next station (1.0–1.6 s,
`quintInOut` / `expoInOut`, motion blur from the shutter); within a chapter the camera **holds dead still** while
elements land on the narrator's words, so the rests are real. The line is the carry at every boundary: it draws on
ahead of the camera, becomes the multiplier arrow, the gauge arc, the staircase of the six stages, the -7% floor, the
underline of the end line, and finally ends in the chamfer of the SELECT tag.

Each chapter shows a small chapter index top-right (RTL), grey + white like the deck: «٠٣ — شلون يشتغل التمويل؟»,
and keeps the deck's discipline: one big element, small supporting type, lots of black.

## Craft notes

- 30 fps, ~7,980 frames. Keep the render affordable: holds must be byte-identical (no float on the camera in holds,
  no ticking animation running through a hold); moves only on chapter transitions and on cue landings.
- Text sizes for 1080p: headline 64–84 px, card text 34–44 px, small caps 20–24 px. All Arabic RTL, word-level animation.
- Define `__motion`, `__track` (on-screen boxes of the main element per chapter), `__meta.inFrame`, and
  `window.__events()` for the SFX layer (whoosh on camera travel, soft tick on checklist/✕, low impact on chapter
  landings, roll on counters).
- Deterministic, no particles, no `Math.random`.
