- **Music:** Mixkit #543 "A New Life" by Eugenio Mininni (Mixkit Free License), edited to 60 s on its 128 BPM grid (`docs/MUSIC.md`). SFX: two soft impacts (30.0, 52.5) and one soft whoosh under the logo reveal.
- **Typography:** line 1 bold, revealed right to left out of a blur; line 2 light, rising out of its baseline (no letter-spacing on Arabic, so letters stay joined); one key word per card pulses and draws a red underline; a white hairline opens above each card; each card pushes in slowly and leaves word by word. The lockup wipes the logo on with a bloom, then «منذ 2007» (2007 underlined) and the Latin line track in.
- **Plate retouch:** Amedi (K3) minaret and a stray glass reflection painted out; C8 rooftop glyphs painted out.
