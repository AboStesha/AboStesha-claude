# Axi emotional ad · script v4 options (plain, direct, no drama)

The client rejected v3 because it was "not understandable, not direct". v4 is plain and direct with no drama: white MSA, ≤5 words per line, no voice-over, 60 s, 9:16. Clips will be made at 720p once a script is approved.

**Recommendation:** I recommend A because it is built on Draft C, which won two of the three lenses and had the highest total (256, against 246 for B and 236 for A), and it fixes C's weak spots. Each good moment now repeats the words of the hard moment it answers: «السوق سريع / وعليك أن تقرر الآن» comes back as «السوق سريع / وتقرر بهدوء». No card carries two ideas, no card goes above 2.5 words a second, and it keeps the lines the judges liked most from all three drafts («تتردد طويلا / فتضيع الفرصة», «بعد صفقات ناجحة / تظن أنك لا تخطئ», «تثق بقرارك / لأنك درسته», «تدخل الصفقة / وأنت تعرف المخاطر»). In both scripts: the brand line is «بناها متداولون / من أجل المتداولين»; the risk strip runs in 3 lines from 54.375 over a clear box; there is no CTA wording and no fast cuts between characters; R1 is graded to night; the light rises steadily to sunrise at 52.5; and lines like «تعويض الخسارة», «والانتظار صعب», «تنهي يومك» and «عند هدفك» are gone. B is the clearer, story-like choice: one hard day in order, then another day in the same order. The good day includes a small loss taken calmly, so sticking to the plan never reads as a promise of profit, and Axi ends on «في الأيام الصعبة والجيدة» instead of «معك في كل صفقة». Once the client approves one script, every video is produced at 720p, as requested.

## Editor and compliance changes

- Option A, card 4: «تخسر صفقة / وكنت واثقا منها» is now «تخسر صفقة / كنت واثقا منها». The 'and' made it read as a jump from present to past tense ('you lose a trade, and you were sure'). Without it, the line is a plain relative clause on the indefinite noun ('a trade you were sure of'), which is more natural MSA and clear on first read. EN: 'You lose a trade / you were sure of'. Still 5 words over 3.75 s (1.33 w/s).
- Option B, card 8: «كل متداول / يعرف هذه الأيام» is now «كل متداول / يمر بأيام كهذه». In everyday Arabic, «هذه الأيام» usually means 'nowadays', so the old line could be read as 'Every trader knows nowadays'. «بأيام كهذه» can only mean 'days like these'. EN: 'Every trader / goes through days like these'. 5 words over 3.75 s (1.33 w/s).
- Checked and unchanged. Every other Arabic line is correct MSA with no dialect, no diacritics and consistent spelling (hamzas in أن/أنك/أخرى/تخطئ, and accusatives without tanween in طويلا/واثقا/مبكرا/فورا/حذرا). Every line is 5 words or fewer, and every card is at or under 2.7 w/s (highest 2.49, Option A card 15). Every hold is at least 1.875 s, and every in/out time is a multiple of 0.9375 s.
- Compliance check, nothing to change. Neither script promises profit or success: wins are always framed as 'when' (وحين تنجح صفقة) and are paired with restraint. No line says emotions are removed, that Axi trades for you, 'best', numbers other than «منذ 2007», a 24/7 claim, a regulator claim or a founders claim. «معك في كل صفقة» (S13) and «بناها متداولون / من أجل المتداولين» (S17) match FACTS §11. «فهي جزء من التداول» is the approved paraphrase of S10.
- Risk strip check, nothing to change. In both scripts it matches FACTS §10 word for word, split into 3 lines of 70 characters or fewer. It runs from 54.375 s (under the 54.4 s limit) to the last frame without fading, so it covers well over the 3 s minimum.
