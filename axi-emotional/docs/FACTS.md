## 1. Origin, and "built by traders, for traders"

**Verdict:** "Built by traders, for traders" **is Axi's own headline.** It appears on the About page in EN (A1, plus the UK and AU copies) and AR (A2), and Axi Academy is described the same way (A8, A9). The ad can use it. Axi **does not name its founders** or describe their backgrounds. It says only that it started as a "two-person startup". So "founded by traders" is **not a documented fact** (see 1.7).

## 9. What the ad MUST NOT say or show

|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 | Any promise of profit or success: «نضمن نجاحك», «ستربح», «ستنجح معنا», "we guarantee" | No guarantees. Trading carries risk of loss beyond the deposit | 7.3, 7.7, IQ p60 |

| 9.3 | Axi trades, decides or advises for the trader: «نتداول عنك», «نختار صفقاتك», «نصحح أخطاءك», «نعوض خسارتك», "we'll get you back to profit" | AxiTrader is "not a financial adviser" and services are "execution only". ## 10. Mandatory on-screen risk line

**Verbatim from SC §4** (Axi's own Arabic risk line [7.7 / A16] plus the IQ p41 entity line):

> ينطوي التداول على مخاطر. قد تخسر أكثر مما تستثمر. الأداء السابق لا يضمن النتائج المستقبلية. تروج بواسطة شركة AxiTrader LLC.

- **Timing:** it must cover **at least the last 3 s, 42.000-45.000**. Start it on a beat at **≤ 41.5 s** so it is fully readable by 42.0, and keep it to the **last frame**. It never fades early, and only the CTA elements may fade.
- **Spec (BR §12, SC §2):** type ≥ 2% of the height (**≥ 39 px**), Rabar Regular, Carbon text on a white 92% strip. Box at y ≈ 1350-1580 and x 54-940: above the bottom 15% zone, clear of the watermark and of the Reels icon rail. At most 3 lines of ≤ 70 characters.
- **Why it is still needed in a brand ad:** the ad speaks about success. "Past performance" covers the "when you succeed" beat, and the entity line is mandatory in every promotional piece (IQ p8, p41).
- **Legal alternatives (Legal chooses):** the IQ p50 persona-ad line «تنطوي عقود الفروقات على مخاطر عالية لخسارة رأس المال، وقد لا تناسب جميع المستثمرين.», or the full IQ p60 strip (4 lines; raise the box top to y ≈ 1300). IQ p60 marks the standard strip "to be confirmed by legal".
- **Open conflict:** IQ p60 requires an **audio disclosure at the end of a promotional video**, and the brief says no voice-over. This needs a written exception from the brand manager (IQ p70), or a 3 s spoken risk line over the end card only.
- **Caption:** because the ad is meant to be shared, the post caption must carry the full risk text plus the entity line (as in SC §5). No hashtag or caption line may promise results.
