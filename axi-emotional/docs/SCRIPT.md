**Script 1: strengths.**
- Every line is the sentence a trader says only to himself, in «أنا». The viewer doesn't watch a character; he reads his own thought. Sharing it is a confession without typing a word.
- First-person Arabic carries no gender (ظننت، وعدت، قلت، أريد، أثق), so the same line is true over Aesha and over Saeed. Scripts 2 and 3 put a masculine "you" over women («ولم تكن كذلك» over Aesha).
- The فهم thread: hook «لا يفهم» → the false «فهمت السوق كله» → «نعرف هذا الشعور» in the hook's screen position → pill «أرسله لمتداول يفهمك».
- It uses the strongest native photos: Saeed stopping dead in the fog towers, Noor in the glass elevator, the cave.

**Script 1: weaknesses.** Its turn on the LIFT cuts to a new person and place (Saeed's cave), so the strongest possible picture for the turn is missed. Its brightest beat «هدأ الضجيج / وسمعت نفسي» is true to Douglas but abstract.

**Script 2: strengths.** The mirror discipline: every dark beat has a bright twin with the same plate, the same text slot, the inverse camera move and the same sound, changed. The **mirror cut on the LIFT** (Sarmad's window, the same frame night → dawn) is the best image of the film's thesis. «تحرك السعر / وقلبك في مكانه» answers «سقط السعر / وسقط قلبك معه» word for word.

**Script 2: weaknesses.** Its "you" is mixed (one «أنا» line among «أنت» lines). Seven bright twins at one bar each depend on the viewer remembering the dark half. «أصعب صفقة / هي التي لم تدخلها» edges toward advice. It leaves the cave and the elevator plates unused.

**Script 3: strengths.** The eight underlined "truth" cards, the most screenshot-ready lines («السوق لم يهزمك / هزمت نفسك»). The hairline underline as the gesture of a reader marking an old book.

**Script 3: weaknesses.** Two cards per feeling doubles the reading in a film with no narrator. The hook's "they" is never named. «عشناها مثلك» claims Axi's staff lived every feeling, revenge and shame included, which goes past FACTS 1.4. «وتستعيد كل شيء» puts "get it all back" on screen.

## 2. Who the feelings come from

The client asked for the legend who described the trader's feelings best. Full sources and checked quotes are in `EMOTION_MAP.md` §1, §3 and §4.

- **Mark Douglas**, *The Disciplined Trader* (1990) and *Trading in the Zone* (2000). He is the most-cited writer on trader emotion, and he nearly lost everything in his own first year. He gives the film its arc: the four fears, euphoria as dangerous as fear, the market as a neutral mirror, and the brightest state as the calm "zone", at peace with any outcome, not the high of winning. **Ideas only, never named** (in copyright; naming him implies endorsement).
- **Jesse Livermore**, through Edwin Lefèvre's *Reminiscences of a Stock Operator* (1923). He is the legend, and the book is the most-quoted trading book in the world. It has every feeling in the film in plain words: the "swelled head", hope that makes you "lose more than you should", "enemies within", "they beat themselves", "ashamed to go out", "sick, nervous, upset", "the game taught me the game", "at last on the right track". **One verbatim line on screen** (B17), public domain, credited to the book only, never to Livermore.
- **Van K. Tharp** in support: a mistake means breaking your own rules, and you trade well only from acceptance.

The legends and Axi's own psychology pages [3.4–3.10] agree, so the honest arc is also the compliant one. Emotion is never removed. The bright half is about **how the trader stands**, never about money.

## 3. Directing and editing grammar

1. **Three voices.** «أنا» is the trader (0–45). The 1923 book is human nature (45–48.75). «نحن» is Axi (48.75 on). The film's one dissolve marks the change at 45.0.
2. **Twins.** Every persona has a dark beat and its bright twin in the same place, the same text slot and the same sound, changed.

| **Aesha** | B07 shame: a smile for them | B12 pride: a smile for herself, the first look into the lens | The same slot. Warm voices → a roar for someone else |
| **The film** | B01 «لا يفهم هذا الشعور» | B18 «نعرف هذا الشعور» | The hook slot |

3. **The camera.** Before 30.000 it never moves *with* a trader: locked off, drifting or retreating on a long lens. From the LIFT it pushes forward, then walks beside them. Nobody looks into the lens before Aesha at 35.625. After that each persona does once (the B18 chain), and Noor closes facing it.
4. **The buzz.** One phone haptic buzz, four times, tells the whole arc: good news (0.0), the drop (4.6875), walked through (≈34.2), not even reached for (41.25).
5. **Every cut has a motive:** a sound, a gesture, a light shape, a stab, a snap, a footfall, an eye-line. No whip pans, glitches, RGB splits, flare overlays or flash frames.
6. **The red device is the thread.** Every persona carries the Axi-red phone (Noor may carry the red tablet), the only saturated colour on screen. At the end it fills the lens and becomes the red card. Screens always face away from camera.
