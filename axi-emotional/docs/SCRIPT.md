# Axi Iraq · Emotional brand ad · «لا يفهم هذا الشعور إلا متداول» · final script

- **Format:** 45.000 s, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps (1,350 frames), no voice-over. Music, sound design and on-screen Arabic carry everything.
- **Music:** `src/music/ad_music.wav` (Mixkit #543 "A New Life", 128.000 BPM, beat 0.46875 s, bar 1.875 s). Every cut sits on a beat of `src/music/grid.json`. Frame numbers (`f`) are the frame that contains the grid time, floor(t × 30). There is one deliberate exception: the red card leaves at 42.250 (f1267), 2 frames after beat 90, so that it holds a full 1.0 s (IQ p44).
- **Facts:** every on-screen claim maps to `docs/FACTS.md` (fact IDs in brackets). Brand rules come from `axi-iraq-copytrading/BRAND.md`. "IQ pNN" means a page of the Axi Iraq guidelines.
- **Cast:** the five approved faces (IQ p46): **Noor, Aesha (Aisha), Sarmad, Saeed, Rojin**. Each shot holds one persona, never two in a frame. Asmaa is not used (§12).
- **Spine in one line:** «لا يفهم هذا الشعور إلا متداول» → Axi answers as a trader, «نعرف…» → «لست وحدك» → «لأننا في آكسي متداولون مثلك» → «بناها متداولون، من أجل المتداولين».

## 1. Scoring the three directions

Each direction is scored 1–10 on the five criteria in the brief. The last column is for information only: how much of the direction already sits on the chosen music.

| Direction | Emotional truth | Shareability | Clarity without VO | Brand / compliance | Cinematic potential | **Total /50** | On the chosen grid? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · "A letter to the trader"** | 9 | 9 | 7 | 7 | **10** | **42** | No. It was written for a walking pace of about 88 BPM, with the lift at 22.0 and the breath at 20–22. Everything has to be retimed |
| **B · "The trader's arc"** | 7 | 7 | **9** | 8 | 8 | **39** | No. The lift is at 22.0 and the ident at 39.6, drifting up to about 1 s off |
| **C · "The traders' manifesto"** | 9 | **10** | 7 | 6 | 9 | **41** | **Yes.** Every cut is already on `grid.json`: the stabs, the breath, the lift, the crash, 30.0 and the arrival |

**A: strengths.**
- It has the most specific insider truths: the house asleep while your mind is not, friends asking about the market while you only smile, the thumb over the button, being right with no one to tell.
- It has the best directing idea of the three. **The camera is Axi:** it stays still at night and walks beside each trader after the lift, so the client's "you will never walk alone" is *shown*, never written.
- Every persona gets a setup and a payoff.

**A: weaknesses.**
- 17 beats at up to 2.5 words a second, inside a letter conceit, ask for a lot of reading.
- «بعد خسارة / تفكر أن تترك التداول» is answered by «نمشي معك». That can read as a broker talking a losing trader out of stopping.
- «وحين تنجح / نبقى شريكك» puts "partner" right next to success.
- It is built on a walking-pace grid that is not the chosen track.

**B: strengths.**
- It is the clearest of the three: the client's own words, one feeling per face, and a clean night-to-dawn light arc.
- It has «وكل دقيقة كأنها ساعة».
- Nobody looks into the lens until «لست وحدك».
- The red phone fills the lens and becomes the red card.

**B: weaknesses.**
- The «حين شعرت…» lines are faithful but generic, so traders have less of their own to share.
- «وشريكك في كل نجاح» implies repeated success.
- Its 128 px headline is over the IQ maximum of 120 px.

**C: strengths.**
- The hook «لا يفهم هذا الشعور / إلا متداول» lets traders pick themselves out, and the end pill «أرسله لمتداول يفهمك» closes that loop.
- «أصعب سوق نتداوله / هو أنفسنا» is the most quotable line in all three directions.
- «بعد خسارة نخفيها» comes from Axi's own insight about shame after a loss [3.10].
- The «نعرف» lines sit in a fixed screen position, so each frame works as a collectible screenshot.
- It is already cut to the real music.

**C: weaknesses.**
- The "we" slides between "we traders" and "we Axi".
- The 00:05 countdown to a news release reads like a prompt to trade the news.
- «نخسر، نتعلم، نعود» and «حين نعود في الصباح التالي» can read as "trade again after a loss".
- «ولا أحد منا وحده» implies a community that is not verified [9.14].

### Decision

**The backbone is C.** It is the only direction already cut to `grid.json`, and its «نعرف» anaphora names the client's five feelings in the client's order, one per persona, one fixed screen position each. That is the clearest structure for a film with no voice-over. The voice is tightened so that it is always **Axi speaking as a trader to *you***: the «نعرف» label is "we", and the second line is about "you".

## 2. Directing and editing grammar

1. **The camera is Axi.**
   - **Night, 0–20.625:** the camera never moves *with* a trader. It is locked off or drifts slowly on a long lens, or it retreats. Traders are small, alone, behind glass, and lit only by a phone or a city.
   - **From the lift at 22.5:** the camera walks beside each trader at shoulder height and at their pace.
   - **The look:** no one looks into the lens until Sarmad turns to it on «وحدك» (28.594). Each trader then glances at it once on the dawn chain. Noor closes the film facing it.
   - "You will never walk alone" is never written. It is what the camera does.
2. **Same places, night then dawn.** Every persona's dawn shot returns to their own night location or its daylight twin, and B16 copies B02's composition exactly. The film loops: its dark last frame cuts back to B01's night close-up.
3. **Every cut has a motive.** Cuts come on a blink, lights going out, a head turn, the phone baton, a tap, a stab, the glow dying, a dawn line becoming a ridge, a footfall, hair across the lens, a sun flare, an eye-trace or an exit and entry. There are no whip pans, glitches, RGB splits, lens-flare overlays or flash frames. Sound leads picture (J-cuts) at B02→B03, B03→B04 and B08→B09.
4. **Rhymes the viewer feels without noticing.**
   - The night montage (four faces, 0.47 s each, «وكل دقيقة كأنها ساعة») mirrors the dawn chain (four faces, 0.47 s each, «معك في كل خطوة»). The eyes sit in the same screen position and the type in the same lower-third spot.
   - The «نعرف» label never moves, so on the lift hit «الضعف» becomes «القوة» as a one-word swap.
5. **Type is the voice.** With no narrator, size and motion stand in for volume:
   - **night:** 96 px labels and a 60 px whispered second line, words blooming in slowly
   - **stress:** 2-frame snaps and 72 px lines
   - **breath:** the slowest reveal, at 80% opacity, drifting down
   - **lift:** the words rise out of a mask
   - **Axi voice after the lift:** 104 px, revealed by a light sweep
   - **«لست وحدك»:** 120 px, the IQ maximum, used nowhere else
6. **The red device is the thread.** Every persona carries the Axi-red phone or tablet, and it is the only saturated colour on screen. In the end the device fills the lens and becomes the Axi red card. Phone screens always face away from the camera, or show only glow.
