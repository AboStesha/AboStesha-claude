## Decisions

- **Lesson 14 is not in the final-render folders yet.** Its title is «سيكولوجية التداول», in course 3. The client picked this; the source audio is `14 - trading psychology`.
- **Gold lessons 3 and 4 follow the videos, not the Drive file names.** The two files are swapped:
  - `أوقات تداول الذهب - lesson 3` plays the numbers lesson.
  - `الذهب بالأرقام - lesson 4` plays the times lesson.
  - The thumbnails say lesson 3 = الذهب بالأرقام and lesson 4 = أوقات تداول الذهب. Rename the two files to match.
- **Spelling and grammar fixed on the thumbnails** (the Drive file names are unchanged):
  - أنواع (was انواع)
  - إيداع (was ايداع)
  - الشراء والبيع (was الشراء و البيع)
  - متداولاً محترفاً in the course 3 name
- **Brand colour fixes after generation:**
  - Two arrows came out classic green, which the brand forbids. They were recoloured to mint `#5EE0BB` (lesson 11 B and gold 2 B, the `mint` field in `jobs.json`).
  - Lesson 12 B was regenerated with a full moon instead of a crescent, so it reads as "night" and nothing else.
- **No text is baked into the images.** Every word, the lesson tag and the logo are typeset afterwards, in Readex Pro and Inter with the official Axi Academy lockup, so the Arabic is exact on all 44. Numbers use Western digits in Inter.

## How it was made

1. **References.** The presenter photos and the client's studio stills were imported from the persona Drive folder.
   - Asmaa: the face close-up plus a body photo.
   - Sarmad: the close-up plus the main photo.
   - Course 2 design A also used that lesson's studio still as a set reference.
2. **Art.** GPT Image 2.5 (Sunburst variant), quality xhigh, 2k, 16:9, at 4.5 credits each.
   - There were 48 generations, 216 credits in all. That covers a 6-image pilot and one redo (lesson 12 B).
   - Each prompt has: an identity lock on the reference photos, a fixed composition (presenter on one side, the title side kept empty), a lesson prop, a lighting rig, "no text or logos", and the brand palette.
   - Job IDs and result files are in `generations.json`.
3. **Compositing** was done in the Higgsfield sandbox:
   - `tool/prep.py` picks the logo colour from the art under it (ink on light, white on dark).
   - It can also slide a design B image right, extending its vertical-gradient backdrop, and repaint classic green to mint.
   - `tool/compose.mjs` (Playwright) sets the title, sizing it to the largest that fits the column, plus the pills and the lockup.
   - `tool/sheet.py` builds the review contact sheets.
   - `jobs.json` holds every thumbnail's title, colours, design and per-lesson layout tweaks.

```
python3 tool/prep.py jobs.json
PW_MODULE=<path>/playwright/index.mjs node tool/compose.mjs jobs.json assets
```

`assets/` needs `ReadexPro.ttf`, `Inter.ttf`, `lockup_white.png` (included) and `lockup_ink.png` (the same lockup in ink #090A0D with the red tip kept).
