# Axi Academy — Gold series · Lesson 04 (finale): أوقات الذهب

The last lesson of the gold series is built in the grammar of the reference *Prompt Motion 7*, a SaaS launch film:
- a typed input with a red caret that squashes into a pill;
- a click with a ripple and radar rings;
- a dotted globe with live pings under a huge odometer;
- 3D-tilted dashboard cards with tabs and area charts;
- notification toasts and an orbit network;
- a one-word-per-screen montage;
- a light end card.

**Concept:** «الساعة بتوقيت بغداد». Every time in the lesson is a digit wheel that rolls into Baghdad time: GMT+3, COMEX open and close, the sessions dial, the news clocks, summer → winter.

The film is synced word by word to the original voice-over, `lesson_4.wav` (Iraqi dialect). The voice-over itself is **not included**.

- `GoldLesson04_Gold_Hours.mp4` — 1920×1080, 60 fps, 4:20. The soundtrack is **music + sound effects only**, and the music already dips wherever the voice speaks.
- **To add your voice-over:** place the voice file so it starts at **00:00:02.000**, at its original level. Then master to −14 LUFS with a −1 dBTP true-peak limit.
  - The voice file repeats its farewell (≈04:00–04:15). The picture covers both takes with a recap montage, so you can keep or trim either one.
- **Palette:** night grid (eclipse/carbon), with gold as the series accent. Axi red is used only for the caret, pings, losses and warnings. The end card is light.
- **Type:** Readex Pro for Arabic (simplified MSA on screen), Inter for numbers and tokens.
- **Music:** a new score — 124 BPM in A minor, with a kick on every beat, off-beat stabs, glass plucks and 16th arps on the big moments, plus 261 sound cues.
- **The on-screen table** (02:05–02:33) lists the sessions and the US news times in Baghdad time, for summer and winter.
- **Directing prompt:** `PROMPT.md` is the reference prompt adapted to this lesson (direction · structure with timecodes · build · start), plus the information check.
- **Stills:** `stills/` holds four key frames.
- **Notes:**
  - Prices, the activity bars and the MT5 window are illustrative.
  - COMEX and MetaTrader appear as plain text only, with no third-party logos.
  - CFD sessions depend on the broker, as the narration says.
