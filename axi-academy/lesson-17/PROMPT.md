# Lesson 17 — Trading Strategy · adapted motion prompt

This prompt adapts the reference screen recording (a kinetic-typography motion reel: BOUNCE / STRETCH / SPIN / SNAP words, easing graphs with handwritten notes, follow-through particle trails, a ring loader, ripple and stepped-bar transitions, and a chaptered timeline along the bottom). It is matched to the Axi Iraq brand and to the narration of `17 - trading strategy.wav`, which runs 285.9 s.

All times below are **video time**. The narration starts at **2.00 s**, after the brand open.

```
<direction>
Build a 293-second, world-class kinetic explainer for Axi Academy (أكاديمية آكسي), lesson 17:
"استراتيجية التداول". Every word on screen is Arabic (RTL); English only as trading tokens
(EUR/USD, XAU/USD, BTC/USD, WTI, US500), terms in parentheses on first use — (Entry), (Exit),
(Support), (Resistance), (Take Profit), (Stop Loss), (Breakeven), (Trailing Stop) — and numbers
in Western digits.

Style = the reference reel, re-skinned for Axi:
- Huge heavy display words whose letters animate one by one (Arabic-safe: the word is rendered
  once, then cut into letter strips, so the joins never break). Each word is staggered by about
  45 ms and picks one motion that fits its meaning:
  - DROP: letters fall with a tilt and an underdamped spring.
  - BOUNCE: a gravity bounce.
  - STRETCH: elastic horizontal scale.
  - SPIN: rotateY, 450° (720° for «مرّتين»).
  - SNAP: no easing, with red construction lines through the frame.
  - SLAM: scales down from 240%.
  - RISE: through a slot.
  - SLIDE: travels in the reading direction.
- The reference's blue dot becomes the Axi red dot: it bounces in with squash and lands as the
  full stop of the key word («استراتيجية.», «الدرس القادم.»), and it is the knob, the target
  centre and the leader of every follow-through trail.
- Handwritten notes become brand-font notes in slate (on light) or silver (on dark), written
  right→left with hand-drawn arrows, underlines, strike-throughs, scribble circles, ✓ and ✗.
- Real motion blur: 6 sub-frames per frame (18 on whips), averaged, so fast letters smear like
  the reference.
- Transitions are hard-edged, never crossfades: stepped bars, ripple rings, whip pans, slices.
- HUD (never blurred): timecode top-left; a full-width chapter timeline along the bottom, running
  right→left from 00:00 to the end. Chapters are diamonds (current = red, passed = grey,
  future = hollow), with a red playhead and the chapter number + name flipping in above it.

Brand (Axi Iraq guidelines):
- Palette 70·20·10: off-white/frost light scenes, eclipse dark scenes, slate. Axi red ≤ 10%
  (one red word per headline, losses, actions). Mint #5EE0BB only for gains and passed checks.
- Gradients are vertical only (light #FBFBFB→#EEF0F1, dark #17181B→#0B0D0D) with a dot grid;
  no radial vignette.
- Logo top-right and white X watermark bottom-left at 60% opacity. The director keeps them fixed
  on screen through every transition, in the ink of the scene beneath.
- Open: white logo on the primary gradient (#EEF0F1→#607880), 1.5 s, identity sound.
- Close: centred X sting (1 s), then the CTA «تعلّم ثم تعلّم ثم تدرّب ثم تداول.» with the red
  pill «تعلّم مع Axi», and the risk strip for the last 3 s.
- Type: Rabar is not available here, so Readex Pro (Arabic) + Inter (Latin/numbers) stand in.
</direction>

<sound>
120 BPM electronic bed — Dm9 · Bbmaj7 · F · C6/9, built from pads, hats, 8th plucks,
four-on-the-floor kick + clap with sub/bass pump, and a 16th arp — with energy following the
chapters. It is side-chained under the voice (−66% while speaking) and mastered to −16 LUFS /
−1 dBTP. Every kinetic word has its own sound:
- whoosh + pop (drop), bloop (bounce), rubbery sweep (stretch), filtered spin, snap, slam;
- ticks for checks, buzz for crosses, scribble for hand-drawn strokes;
- shutter for bars, ripple for ripples, whip for pans, chime for passed moments, glitch for the
  word that disintegrates.
</sound>
```
