<direction>
Build a 252-second educational motion piece for Axi Academy (أكاديمية آكسي), lesson 16:
"التحليل الأساسي مقابل التحليل الفني". Every word on screen is Arabic (RTL, right-aligned);
English appears only as trading tokens: pair symbols (EUR/USD), indicator codes (RSI, MACD),
timeframes (M1 … MN), data codes (GDP, CPI) and prices — always Western digits.

Concept, in one line: one price line is promoted three times — ink on paper (the fundamental
analyst's report), white wire on slate (the technical analyst's chart), light on black (the
trading screen where both meet) — and it never leaves the screen.

Every shot is made out of the previous one: the rail that crushes the title becomes a price
ruler, the ruler stands up and splits the frame into two traders, the time-horizon bars become
two meshing gears, the heartbeat line flattens into a decision fork, the calendar rows become
the bricks of a foundation and its pillar becomes the summary's divider. No crossfades.

  Axi red #E4002E ≤ 10% (actions, one red word per headline, losses); mint #5EE0BB = gains only.

  60% / 4% width bottom-left all film; 10% top and 15% bottom kept clear; risk strip in the last 3 s.

Three inks. The same geometry is drawn in whichever ink is on screen:
- PAPER (fundamental): off-white with a 60 px slate grid at 11% (every fifth line 22%) and a
  fine grain. Lines are ink pencil: resampled and pushed off true by slow sines whose phase steps
  8 times a second, stroked twice (full width at 90%, half width at 40% with more wobble). Fills
  are 45° hatching drawn one line at a time. Brand white cards with a red corner triangle.
- SLATE (technical): flat #607880 with the same grid in white. Clean white lines 3–4 px, dashed
  for projections; fills are fine white hatching. Candles: hollow up, solid down.
- SCREEN (trading): eclipse black with a faint white grid. Lines carry a white core 30% of the
  width; candle bodies are horizontal scan lines on a 6–8 px pitch — mint up, red down. No glow.

Banned: particle bursts, glows, lens flares, camera shake, RGB split, crossfades, generic
gradients, bouncy easing (the X sting's single settle is the only exception), holds longer
than the sentence they illustrate, template looks, invented claims or banned compliance words.
