The second lesson of the gold series is built in a **new style**: the grammar of the reference *Prompt_Motion_3* (LismCSS 1.0 film). That means:
- a light documentation page;
- a section label at top-left;
- flat diagrams that morph into one another;
- full-bleed chapter panels;
- no cuts at all.

The whole film is tied together by **one gold thread**. It runs from the seed the logo shrinks into at 00:02, through every frame, to the point the X sting grows out of at 03:29. The thread is:
- the 2025 price line;

- the Fed building;
- the bars;
- the ground under the gold block;
- the calendar;
- the strike through «قوانين»;
