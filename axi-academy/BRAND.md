## Typography
- Arabic: **Noto Sans Arabic** (official). RTL, right-aligned.
- Latin: Axi Font (Display Foundry) → fallback **Inter**.
- Numbers: monospace lining.
- Headlines: large, Regular weight. Body: Light weight.
- Headline pattern: first phrase in black + continuation in grey (`#939699`), same size.
- Eyebrow label: small UPPERCASE Latin, letter-spaced, red, followed by a red ▶ triangle.

## Layout system (16:9)
- Header: top-left "AXI / Academy", next column "subtitle / subtitle", top-right `axi` logo.
- Footer: `©2026` left · `AXI.COM` center · page number right.
- Content right-aligned (RTL), generous margins (~6% sides).
- Section opener: gradient bg, huge thin number (`01`) with red ▶ before it, giant white Arabic title, thin English subtitle beneath.

## Components
- **White card**: white fill, thin grey border, ~12px radius, small red corner triangle (top-right), red uppercase Latin tag at bottom.
- **Pill buttons**: primary = red capsule white text; secondary = white with grey outline.
- **Status badges**: mint `PASSED`, red-outline `GATE`, grey neutral.
- **Outlined red tags** (`PRINCIPLE`, `STEP 4`): red 1px outline, small caps text.
- **Bullets**: small red ◀ triangles (RTL).
- **Journey steps**: circles — white/grey = content, red outline = gate, mint fill = reward; connected by red ◀ arrows.
- **Code chips**: grey rounded rectangle, mono text.

## Imagery & ornament
- Neutral portraits on cool grey backgrounds with exactly one red element in frame; calm landscapes graded with the same gradient.
- The ONLY decorative pattern: a faint grid of small red triangles.

## Voice

- Outro must carry the official risk warning (CFD high risk; educational, not investment advice).
