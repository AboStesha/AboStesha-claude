# Axi Academy — Beginners series · Lesson 18 (finale): قبل أول إيداع

The last lesson of «تعلّم التداول للمبتدئين» is built in the grammar of the reference *Prompt Motion 7*, a SaaS launch film:
- a typed input with a red caret that squashes into a pill;
- a click with a ripple and radar rings;
- 3D-tilted dashboard cards;
- search palettes and toasts;
- an onboarding stepper;
- a dotted globe;
- a one-word-per-screen montage;
- a light end card.

**Concept:** «قبل أول إيداع». The film is the checklist a careful beginner (John, a fictional character) runs before the first dollar: verify the broker → practise on a demo → avoid the four common mistakes.

The film is synced word by word to the original voice-over, `lesson_18_final.wav` (Iraqi dialect). The voice-over itself is **not included**.

- `Lesson18_Before_First_Deposit.mp4` — 1920×1080, 60 fps, 2:38. The soundtrack is **music + sound effects only**, and the music already dips wherever the voice speaks.
- **To add your voice-over:** place the voice file so it starts at **00:00:02.000**, at its original level. Then master to −14 LUFS with a −1 dBTP true-peak limit.
