- a dotted globe with pings;
- 3D-tilted dashboard cards;
- search palettes, toasts and onboarding steppers;
- a one-word-per-screen montage;
- a light end card.

The film is synced word by word to `lesson_18_final.wav` (Iraqi dialect, 71 phrases, 325 words). The voice-over is **not** in the master; place it at **00:02.00**. All times are video time, `MM:SS.cc`.

**Concept (one line):** «قبل أول إيداع» — the film is the checklist a careful beginner runs before the first dollar: verify → practise → avoid the four mistakes.

## Information check

Every claim in the narration was checked:
- **CFD + leverage:** a CFD lets you open a position with a margin that is only a fraction of the position's value.
  - The on-screen example is labelled: 1:20 → $500 margin for a $10,000 position.
  - A 1% move = $100 = 20% of that margin.
  - Leverage magnifies losses exactly as it magnifies profits, so a small account can be lost quickly.
- **Checking a broker:**
  - Check that the broker is licensed.
  - Check which legal entity will hold your account, and that it is regulated.
  - Look at client protections and reputation.
  - Verify on the regulator's own public register, not only on the broker's website.
  - A licence does not mean you cannot lose money.
  - Protections (for example segregated funds, compensation schemes, negative-balance protection) differ by entity and by the client's country of residence.
- **Axi demo:** Axi offers a free demo account on MetaTrader for practice before using real money.
- **Getting started on a demo:**
  - Symbol names differ by broker (suffixes such as .a or m).
  - Add the symbol to Market Watch.
  - Open its Specification to read the contract size (EURUSD standard lot = 100,000 units) and the costs (spread, swap, commission).
- **Demo ≠ live:**
  - Execution can differ around news and at the weekend open.
  - Slippage and gaps may not appear the same way.
  - There is no real money, so emotions differ.
- **The four mistakes:**
  - Depositing without verifying the broker and the regulator's register.
  - Going live on day one without demo practice.
  - Trading without checking the symbol's specification and costs.
  - Trusting guaranteed-profit offers, or people selling signals or portfolio management with guaranteed promises. These are known scam signs.
- **On-screen quotes,** the terminal, Market Watch and the register are generic, illustrative mock-ups with no third-party marks. John is a fictional character drawn as a generic avatar.
