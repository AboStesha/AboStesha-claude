It keeps the reference's grammar:
- one continuous 2D take;
- text rising from a mask line;
- buttons expanding into pages;
- colour flooding out of one object and shrinking into another;
- a glowing composer;
- photo cards bursting in on the drop;

It is re-skinned with the Axi brand book that was supplied: the «Combinations» gradient swatches, the 3D metal objects, and the Commodities photography. On top of that comes a glassmorphism layer.

All times are video time, `MM:SS.cc`. The voice-over is **not** in the master; place it at **00:02.00**.

## Information check

Every claim in the narration was checked:
- **Gold as a commodity:** gold is a precious metal and is traded as a commodity (Axi lists it under Commodities).
- **The maths:** 3 oz × $4,000 = $12,000. At $4,400 the chain is worth $13,200, a difference of +$1,200, which is +10%. A 10% fall gives −$1,200.
- **Hypothetical prices:** the $4,000/oz price is stated as an assumption, and the shop's buy-back offer is labelled as hypothetical. Karat and workmanship are noted.
- **Safe haven:** described as a tendency, not a guarantee, and gold can fall even in crises. This is accurate and balanced.
- **Ways to hold gold:** physical gold carries a premium plus storage and insurance costs. ETFs (some physically backed), futures and CFDs are all real routes.
- **CFDs:** a CFD is a derivative contract with the broker. P/L is exit minus entry, affected by the spread and costs.
- **Retail margin:** the EU (ESMA), UK (FCA) and Australia (ASIC) set retail margin for gold at no less than 5%, which is maximum leverage of 1:20. Other regulators may allow less margin.
- **Leverage:** a 5% adverse move at 5% margin wipes out the margin, and the broker may close the trade first.
- **Negative-balance protection:** it varies by regulator.
- **Short selling:** selling first is possible depending on the account, and in theory the loss on a short is unlimited.
