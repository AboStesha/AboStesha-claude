The first lesson of the new gold series. It is built in the grammar of the reference *Food craving launch film* (Prompt Motion), re-skinned with the Axi brand book that was supplied (gradient swatches, 3D metals and Commodities photography) plus a glassmorphism layer. It is synced word by word to the original voice-over, `lesson_1_full.wav`, which is in Iraqi dialect. The voice-over itself is **not included**.

- `GoldLesson01_How_to_Trade_Gold.mp4` — 1920×1080, 60 fps, 3:56. The soundtrack is **music + sound effects only**, and the music already dips wherever the voice speaks.
- **To add your voice-over:** place the voice file so it starts at **00:00:02.000**, at its original level. Then master to −14 LUFS with a −1 dBTP true-peak limit.
