# Axi Iraq · Buy Crypto: a five-video social campaign

Five vertical 9:16 videos (1080×1920, 60 fps, Arabic) about Axi's new **Buy Crypto** feature.

- **What the feature is:** you buy real crypto on the spot market and keep it in the **Axi Wallet**.
- **How the five fit together:** each video covers a different thing you can do, and each has its own hook, style, music and CTA. In order they answer:
  1. **What** you can now do.
  2. **Where** your coins are kept.
  3. **How** to buy.
  4. **Which** way to trade suits you.
  5. **Your questions**, answered by a person.
- **Sound:** every video has its music and sound effects. Voice-over and presenter footage come later, from the scripts below.

## 3. Scripts

### Before recording

- **Music:** in each film it is already lowered in the windows below: −5 dB for 2–4 and −7 dB for 5. Lay the voice on top as it is.
- **Pace:** start each line at its time; if a line runs long, cut a word rather than speed up.
- **Level:** aim for about −16 LUFS for the voice. The beds are −17 LUFS.
- **Pronunciation:**
  - Buy Crypto: «باي كريبتو».
  - Axi Wallet / محفظة آكسي: «محفظة آكسي».
  - CFDs: «عقود الفروقات».
  - Perpetuals: «العقود الدائمة».
  - 0.8%: «صفر فاصلة ثمانية بالمئة».
  - 24/7: «على مدار الساعة».
  - 200+: «أكثر من مئتي».

#### Presenter brief and compositing

- **Shoot:**
  - Vertical 9:16, 1080×1920 or larger.
  - Presenter centred, chest-up, eyes in the upper third.
  - Light grey studio background.
  - Brand look: white, black or grey clothing with **one red element**, and no other logos in frame.
- **The window:**
  - 640 × 880 px, rounded corners (radius 40).
  - It only **moves**; it never scales. Each move is a 0.6 s cubic ease in/out.

| Time | Window top-left (x, y) | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | 220, 560 | Centre (hook) |
| 3.0 → 3.6 | 380, 640 | Right (Q1) |
| 9.0 → 9.6 | 60, 640 | Left (Q2) |
| 15.0 → 15.6 | 380, 640 | Right (Q3) |
| 21.0 → 21.6 | 60, 640 | Left (Q4) |
| 27.0 → 27.6 | 380, 640 | Right (Q5) |
| 33.0 → 33.6 | 220, 440 | Top centre (CTA) |
| 36.6 → 37.0 | — | Fades out (opacity 1 → 0) |

- **Files for the edit:**
  - `AxiIraq_BuyCrypto_05_QA_40s.mp4` is the preview, with a grey stand-in where the presenter goes.
  - `…_05_QA_40s_GREEN.mp4` is the same film with pure green (#00B140) inside the window, plus the music.
  - `…_05_QA_40s_MATTE.mp4` is the window's shape, white on black.
  - `AxiIraq_BuyCrypto_05_QA_window_track.csv` gives the window's position for every frame (60 fps).
- **Compositing:**
  1. Put the presenter clip **under** the GREEN film.
  2. Key out the green.
  3. Scale the presenter so the face sits in the window.
  4. Keyframe the presenter's position at the times above, with ease in/out. They are the same moves, so the presenter stays framed.

  Alternatively, use the MATTE as a track matte on the presenter clip.

  For the fade-out (36.6–37.0 s), the green fades with the window. The MATTE's grey level and the CSV's `opacity` column carry the exact fade, so use either of them to fade the presenter.

  Answer stickers slightly overlap the window frame on purpose, so they sit on top of the presenter.

## 4. Brand and compliance notes

- **No risk-warning strip, at the client's request.** `../BRAND.md` requires a risk strip in the last 3 seconds, and crypto promotions in many markets require one. Compliance should sign off before publishing.
- **Availability:**
  - Buy Crypto is offered by AxiTrader LLC (St Vincent and the Grenadines). Confirm it is available to Axi Iraq clients before running paid media.
  - Transfers to external wallets are not claimed anywhere.
- **No banned wording:**
  - No «ربح مضمون», «بلا مخاطر» or similar.
  - No return promises.
  - Prices are marked «أرقام توضيحية».
  - The explainer says leverage «تضاعف المخاطر بالقدر نفسه».
- **Logo and colour:**
  - The Axi Iraq logo is used everywhere, never the X on its own.
  - Red is used for the CTA pill, one headline word and small accents.
  - Mint is used only for rising prices and gains.
  - Crypto is drawn as silver/white material coins with tickers, not coin brand logos.
- **Hook first.** BRAND.md opens videos with a 1.5 s logo ident. For social hooks, these films open on the hook instead and close on the logo sting with a red CTA pill.
