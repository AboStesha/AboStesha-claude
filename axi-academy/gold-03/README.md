# Axi Academy — Gold series · Lesson 03: أرقام الذهب

The third lesson of the gold series is built in the grammar of the reference *The showreel prompt* (Prompt Motion). That means:
- flat colour fields that change only through masks (slats, vertical bars, a hexagon, a contracting circle);
- kinetic type assembled from sliding slices;
- shapes that morph into each other;
- a 2D → 3D moment;
- data/UI moments;
- 120 BPM, with something happening on every beat.

**Concept:** «ذهب ← وزن ← عقد». A gold bar breaks into the numbers of gold (gram, ounce, mithqal) and stacks back up as the contract: 100 coins → 1 lot.

The film is synced word by word to the original voice-over, `lesson_3.wav` (Iraqi dialect). The voice-over itself is **not included**.

- `GoldLesson03_Gold_Numbers.mp4` — 1920×1080, 60 fps, 3:32. The soundtrack is **music + sound effects only**, and the music already dips wherever the voice speaks.
- **To add your voice-over:** place the voice file so it starts at **00:00:02.000**, at its original level. Then master to −14 LUFS with a −1 dBTP true-peak limit.
- **Palette:** 3 colours + black and white, all flat:
  - gold, slate and Axi red (accent only);
  - ink and frosted paper;
  - mint appears only on a gain bar.
- **Type:** Readex Pro for Arabic, Inter for numbers.
- **Music:** a new score — 120 BPM in G minor, with a kick on every beat, off-beat stabs, FM plucks, a riser into the summary and a hit on every field change.
- **Directing prompt:** `PROMPT.md` is the reference prompt adapted to this lesson (direction · structure with timecodes · build · start), plus the information check.
- **Stills:** `stills/` holds four key frames.
- **Notes:**
  - The $4,000/oz example is hypothetical and labelled as such on screen.
  - Lot size (100 oz) depends on the broker and the account type.
  - The Market Watch window is a generic mock-up.
