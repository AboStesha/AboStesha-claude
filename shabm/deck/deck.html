I · 01THE NAME

شَبِم — بَرْدُ الماء

Arabic has a general word for cold — بارِد — and it is used for weather, for food, for a person and for a joke. It also has one word that means the coldness that belongs specifically to water. That word is شَبِم.

«الشَّبَمُ: بَرْدُ الماءِ. وماءٌ شَبِمٌ: بارِدٌ.»

لسان العرب، ابن منظور (ت ٧١١هـ)، مادّة «شبم» — “al-shabam: the coldness of water. And māʾ shabim: cold water.”

It is a word almost nobody uses in speech any more, which is exactly why it is available. It is unmistakably Arabic, it is unmistakably classical, and it means precisely and only the thing this business sells.

Al-Mutanabbī put it in the most quoted opening hemistich in the language. The brand's name is already inside a line every educated Arab can finish.

شَبِمThe Arabic wordmark — always vocalised, kasra on the bāʾThe diacritic is not decoration. It is what separates the brand from a medical homograph. See §26.
SHABIMThe Latin wordmarkALA-LC correct. Pronounced SHA-bim.
SHBMThe monogram onlyFour etched letters for lids, uniforms and the facade — never the spoken name.
A change from the original brief. The name was drafted as SHABM in Latin. Verification says do not ship that. Three reasons, each independently sufficient: (1) no vowel between B and M means no English, French or Turkish speaker can pronounce it on sight — fatal for a franchise business that lives or dies on word of mouth; (2) the obvious fix, SHABAM, sits one letter from SHIBAM — the UNESCO city in Ḥaḍramawt and Shibam Coffee Co., a live Yemeni-coffee chain with a filed USPTO mark (97667800) in the same Nice classes; (3) SHABIM is the ALA-LC transliteration of شَبِم, the exact vocalisation in al-Mutanabbī's line. Keep SHBM as an etched monogram. Spend the name on SHABIM.
I · 02THE INSIGHT

الفكرة

134days a year above 40°C in BaghdadAnd ~46 days above 45°C. Record 51.8°C, 28 July 2020.
63%of Iraqis are under 30Median age 20.9. Population 46,118,793 — 2024 census, the first in 37 years.
85branded coffee outlets in the whole of IraqOne per ~542,000 people. Saudi Arabia has one per ~6,600.

Three facts that only look unrelated. Iraq is one of the hottest inhabited countries on earth, it is demographically the youngest large market in the Arab world, and its organised café sector barely exists — 85 branded outlets against Saudi Arabia's 5,130. The category everyone else in the region is fighting over has not arrived here yet.

Meanwhile the cold-drink product itself has been standing still. An iced latte in Baghdad in 2026 is the same object it was in 2016: espresso, milk, and a cup of ice cubes that dilute it to water in eleven minutes. In a city where the July average high is 45°C, that is not a product. It is a compromise everyone has agreed to stop noticing.

SHABIM is the answer to one question: what does a cold drink look like if you design it for 48°C instead of apologising for it? Not a colder recipe — a colder object. A vessel at −80°C. A cup carved out of a block of ice. Fruit frozen into the shape of the fruit. And a building that is itself a block of ice, visibly melting on a Baghdad pavement.
I · 03THE MENU, IN SIX LINES

ستّة خطوط

صَقيع ٨٠−
SAQĪʿ −80 · The −80 dirty latte

A heavy-walled vessel is held at −80°C, lifted out with steel tongs, and cold milk is poured in. The milk flash-freezes into a white crust up the inner wall while the core stays liquid. A ristretto is poured through it and carves a dark channel. Nothing is diluted, because there is no ice.

−20°C pre-chill, then −80°C holdServed in tongs, on a chilled base75 secondsNo ice, no dilution

Why it earns its place. The proof that this works commercially already exists: Regulars in Melbourne sells around 600 glasses a day of a single −85°C drink, and the format has queues measured in hours in Singapore and Tokyo. Nobody in Iraq or the Gulf is doing it.

$5.23 Baghdad · 8,000 IQD
القالَب
AL-QĀLAB · Coffee inside the block

A block of crystal-clear ice, bored with a cylindrical cavity, is the cup. Coffee or tea goes into the block. It stays cold for over half an hour and dilutes almost not at all, because the melt surface is a fraction of what cubes give you. When the drink is gone, the block is eaten with a spoon.

136 kg directional-freeze block≈35 vessels per block150 secondsEaten at the end

Why it earns its place. Kobe's Nishimura Coffee has served this since 2016; Cafe 33 at the Hyatt Regency Kyoto charges ¥2,000 — about $13 — for it. It is the most photographed cold drink in Japan and it has never crossed into Arabic-speaking markets.

$9.80 Baghdad · 15,000 IQD
لَفائِف
LAFĀʾIF · Rolled ice cream

Liquid base is poured onto a −30°C steel pan, chopped with the fruit or chocolate the customer chose, spread thin, and scraped into tight rolls in front of them. Two minutes of theatre, made to order, no two portions identical.

Twin −30°C pansMade to order, in view165 secondsCustomer picks the mix-in

Why it earns its place. The format's first viral cycle was 2015-2018 and it never properly landed in Iraq. It is the workhorse: it draws the crowd that films the −80, and it carries the family and kids occasion the coffee items cannot.

$4.58 Baghdad · 7,000 IQD
جَنى
JANĀ · Frozen fruit, in the shape of the fruit

Fruit sorbet moulded and coloured into the exact form of the fruit it came from — strawberry, apricot, fig, lemon, blackberry, melon. It reads as fruit in the hand and breaks open to dense, bright sorbet. Sold as a single piece, or as a nine-piece box on crushed ice.

Batch-produced, not to orderSingle or box of nine22 seconds to serveThe take-home SKU

Why it earns its place. This is the only line that leaves the store: the box is a gift, a majlis offering, a Ramadan and Eid item, and a delivery product. It is also the one line that can later be sold through retail without a café.

$14.38 Baghdad · 22,000 IQD
بَرَد
BARAD · Slush and cold-pressed juice

Fresh-pressed juice and fruit slush — the everyday, high-frequency, low-consideration end of the menu. Pomegranate, orange, mango, mint-lemon.

Pressed on site70 secondsThe repeat-purchase linePriced against the street

Why it earns its place. Every destination brand needs an item that a customer buys twice a week without thinking. Barad is the frequency engine underneath the spectacle.

كانون
KĀNŪN · The winter menu

From November the lighting shifts from 6500K white to 2700K amber, the ice-material surfaces glow like frozen honey instead of glacier, and the menu adds flat whites, Spanish lattes, hot chocolate, karak and Arabic coffee. The cold menu never leaves.

2700K amber schemeNov – Mar60 secondsCold menu stays on

Why it earns its place. Baghdad's January high is 16°C. A pure ice concept would lose four months a year. Kānūn is why the model's winter floor is 73% of the annual average and not 45%.

$3.92 Baghdad · 6,000 IQD
The menu is built as a barbell, deliberately. Two slow, expensive, unrepeatable spectacle items (صَقيع ٨٠−, القالَب) generate the footage and the queue. Two fast, cheap, high-frequency lines (بَرَد, جَنى) generate the repeat visit. One line (لَفائِف) brings families and children. One line (كانون) keeps the doors worth opening in January. Take any one away and something structural breaks.

III · 07THE POETRY, AND WHAT VERIFICATION FOUND

الشِّعر على العبوة

The packaging carries classical Arabic verse containing the word شبم. Before a single line went to print, all eight of the verses originally selected were checked against the corpus. Seven of them do not exist.

Verification scorecard — the eight supplied verses
1authentic, correctly attributed
أبو الطيّب المتنبّي
7not attested anywhere in the corpus

  • الأخطل
  • عنترة بن شداد

  • البحتري

The verse attributed to عنترة is provably not his: his Muʿallaqa rhymes in ميم, and the line rhymes in باء. The one attributed to العباس بن الأحنف uses شَبائِم — a plural of شبم that no dictionary lists and that returns zero corpus hits. The set has the signature of a chatbot: one real anchor verse, seven metrically competent pastiches, each attached to a famous name.

Why this belongs in an investor deck. Printing fabricated classical poetry on packaging, in markets where every educated customer can check it in ten seconds, is a brand-ending error that costs nothing to avoid and everything to make. Catching it before print is the difference between heritage and decoration.

III · 08THE STORE IS THE PRODUCT

المبنى

A block of ice, four metres on a side, sitting on a steel plinth on a Baghdad pavement, lit from inside, with meltwater spreading across the concrete around it. It is not a shop with an ice-themed interior. It is an object that looks like it should not survive the afternoon, and does.

  • Facade: cast acrylic (PMMA) blocks with a hand-chipped glacial texture over a steel frame, edge-lit so the internal fractures glow. UV-stable, 50°C-rated, replaceable panel by panel.
  • Summer scheme: 6500K cold white, high CRI, raking up through the block from concealed base LEDs.
  • Winter scheme كانون: the same fittings driven to 2700K amber. The material stops reading as glacier and starts reading as frozen honey. One DMX scene change, no rebuild.
  • The melt: a filtered, chilled, recirculating film across the plinth. Small volume, visible effect.
The melt detail needs engineering, not just design. Warm recirculated water in 45°C ambient is a Legionella risk, a slip hazard, and a municipal permission question about water on a public pavement. The specification is a chilled, dosed, closed-loop system with a drained catchment — or, where a landlord refuses it, a fog line and a cast-resin "frozen drip" that reads identically on camera. Budgeted either way.

III · 10THE DISTRIBUTION IS THE PRODUCT

لماذا يُصوَّر

This brand does not buy reach. It manufactures reasons to be filmed, and every one of them is structurally hard to copy.

The four filmable moments, and what makes each defensible
  1. Tongs out of the −80. Cryogenic fog rolls over the counter; milk freezes to the glass in real time. Copying it needs a lab-grade ULT freezer, a two-stage pre-chill protocol, and a burn-safe serving SOP.
  2. Boring the block. A cylinder driven into 136 kg of optically clear ice. Copying it needs a directional-freezing plant and three days of lead time per block.
  3. Scraping the rolls. Familiar, fast, endlessly re-shootable — the everyday content that fills the weeks between the set-pieces.
  4. Breaking open the fruit. A strawberry that is not a strawberry. The single most re-postable still image in the range, and the one that travels without the store.
What the evidence says about scale
  • FIX Dessert Chocolatier, Dubai: one bar, viral December 2023, and in Q1 2025 alone 1.2 million bars sold through Dubai Duty Free — around $22M of revenue from a single point of sale.
  • Regulars, Melbourne: about 600 glasses a day of one −85°C drink.
  • Iraq's audience is already assembled. 39.6M internet users, 23.6M on Instagram, 18.5M on Snapchat — the third-largest Snapchat market in MENA after Saudi and Egypt. A single Baghdad food account, @restaurants_of_baghdad, carries 1.1M followers, and the whole Iraqi influencer sector is a $15–20M market, which means reach here is still cheap.
  • And the mechanic that makes it last. Crumbl rotates six flavours every week. It is the most transferable durability device in the whole case set, and SHABIM's version is already in the product: جَنى is fruit-led, so the range rotates with the Iraqi season by necessity — apricot in spring, watermelon and fig in high summer, pomegranate and quince in autumn. A reason to come back that is built into the supply chain rather than invented by marketing.
And the honest counter-argument, with the actual numbers. Capital-gated retail formats like this one have a documented peak-attention window of roughly 12–24 months. Crumbl is the cautionary case, not the inspiration: it passed 1,000 locations, and then per-store average unit volume declined materially from its 2021 peak with same-store sales negative through 2024. Rolled ice cream ran 2015–18 and was largely gone by 2021. SHABIM's answer is structural rather than hopeful — the barbell menu means the spectacle recruits while بَرَد and جَنى retain, كانون holds the winter, the seasonal fruit rotation gives a reason to return, and the جَنى box outlives any single store. The bear case assumes the novelty does fade and still leaves the unit cash-positive every month.

V · 20WHY THIS IS HARD TO COPY

الخندق

Anyone can buy a rolled-ice-cream pan. Four things stack into something that is not worth a competitor's trouble.

01 · The cold chain is the barrier

A −86°C ULT freezer, a directional-freezing ice plant with a three-day block cycle, a blast freezer and a sorbet mould system are not a café's normal equipment list. They need a dedicated generator, a specialist service contract, and staff trained in handling surfaces that cause cold burns. A competitor can copy one item on the menu. Copying the operation means rebuilding the back of house.

02 · The kiosks depend on the Cube

A Shard carries no ice plant. Its blocks come from the city's flagship Cube. That is a deliberate architecture: it lets kiosks open at $168,792 instead of $299,118, and it makes the network a supply chain rather than a logo licence — the franchisee cannot shop around for the one input the signature product depends on.

03 · The name cannot be taken

شَبِم is a real classical word with a verifiable dictionary entry, no existing F&B registration anywhere in MENA, and a documented poetic lineage. Filed across five Nice classes in each market it is a defensible mark — unlike a descriptive name like "Ice" or "Cold", which is unregistrable in every one of these jurisdictions.

04 · The archive compounds

The verse programme, the vocalised typography, the seasonal كانون identity and the verification discipline behind them are a body of work, not a look. A competitor can copy a facade in a season. Assembling a defensible classical-Arabic identity takes scholarship they have no reason to fund.

The four constraints that shaped the design

Engineering that was checked before it was drawn. Each of these would have been discovered on site, expensively, by anyone who did not check first.

A −80°C freezer cannot live in a Baghdad kitchen

Commercial ULT freezers are rated to a maximum 30–32°C ambient. Above that they lose the ability to hold temperature, and they reject 0.7–1.5 kW of heat into the room while doing it. The ULT sits in a separately conditioned back-of-house cell, not behind the bar — and that cell's cooling is in the MEP budget, not discovered later.

A glass cube costs 2.2× a normal café to cool

A 25 m² all-glass kiosk at 50°C ambient carries a 15.4 kW peak cooling load — 0.22 kW/m² against 0.08–0.10 for a conventional café — and needs 5–6 tons of T3-rated AC. 32% of that load is solar gain, which is the cheapest part to design out: selective-coated laminates, a ventilated cavity behind the acrylic, and condensers shaded and off the roof.

Solid acrylic ice blocks are unaffordable

100 mm cast acrylic runs $476–1,071/m² from a Chinese mill and up to $2,975/m² branded. A facade of genuine solid blocks is not a budget decision, it is an impossibility. The ice depth is achieved with 20–30 mm sheet, surface texture, internal lamination and edge lighting — ~70 m² installed for $30,000.

Water is an input, not a utility

The Tigris near Kadhimiya has been measured at 540,000 coliform per 100 mL. Ice is a food, and a carved block that a customer eats has to meet food-grade microbiological standards. A reverse-osmosis plant is not optional equipment — Basra's 2018 water crisis put roughly 118,000 people in hospital — and clear ice needs deionised water anyway to freeze without occlusions.

One correction worth showing, because it goes to how these numbers were built. An early version of this model used twelve monthly seasonality multipliers that were intended to average 1.0 and actually averaged 1.117 — quietly inflating every revenue line by 11.7%. It was caught in review and the vectors are now normalised, so the monthly shape is the forecast and the level comes only from the transactions-per-day assumption where it can be argued with. Every number in this deck is downstream of that fix.
V · 21RISK REGISTER

المخاطر

Ten things that could go wrong, ranked by severity, each with what is actually being done about it. Nothing here is a discovery an investor should be making on their own.

Critical

The −80°C vessel can injure a customer

Skin contact with metal at −80°C causes cold burns in seconds, and a vessel taken straight from −80°C into 45°C ambient can fracture from thermal shock.

Mitigation. A two-stage protocol, exactly as the Melbourne operator runs it: −20°C pre-chill, then −80°C hold. Steel tongs, never bare hands. Thick-walled borosilicate, never thin glass or bare metal. A chilled base and a sleeve on every serve, a hold-time before hand-off, and a written SOP that is part of the franchise manual and the training certification. Liability insurance sized for it before the first store opens.

Critical

Power failure destroys the freezer load

Baghdad's grid delivers 8–12 hours a day; 2025 peak demand was 55 GW against 27 GW of supply, and the December 2025 Iranian gas halt cut a further 4,000–4,500 MW. A ULT freezer that thaws loses its entire contents.

Mitigation. A dedicated 60 kVA generator with automatic transfer, not a neighbourhood generator subscription — this is why the model carries $26k of annual utilities rather than the $5.4k a shared connection would cost. Plus UPS on controls, temperature telemetry with alarms to a phone, and enough thermal mass that a two-hour outage is survivable.

High

The name has a medical homograph

الشَّبَم is the standard modern Arabic term for phimosis. Arabic Wikipedia's article at the exact string شبم is the urology page. A customer, journalist or competitor will find this.

Mitigation. Vocalise the wordmark as شَبِم with a kasra — the medical term is consistently الشَّبَم with a fatḥa. Lock the definition into the lockup so the word never travels alone. Own every modified search query within twelve months. And brief the team with a confident answer rather than an embarrassed one.

High

Key money is illegal and universal

Property Lease Law No. 87 of 1979 prohibits خلو. It is nonetheless demanded on essentially every good Baghdad pitch, observed at 25× monthly rent.

Mitigation. Structure it with counsel as a documented premium or fit-out contribution rather than an undocumented cash payment, and prefer landlords who will take a longer lease at a higher rent instead. Budgeted at 15×.

High

The novelty fades — and this is the likeliest way to lose

Capital-gated spectacle formats have a documented peak-attention window of 12–24 months. Crumbl passed 1,000 stores and then watched per-store volumes fall from their 2021 peak with same-store sales negative through 2024. Rolled ice cream ran 2015–18 and was largely gone by 2021. Separately, the base rate is unforgiving: about 26–27% of restaurants fail in year one and 59–60% within three — lower than the folklore 90%, but not low.

Mitigation. The barbell menu is the structural answer: spectacle recruits, بَرَد and جَنى retain, كانون holds the winter, the seasonal fruit rotation supplies a Crumbl-style reason to return that is built into the supply chain rather than invented by marketing, and the جَنى box is a retail product that survives the café. The bear case models exactly this fade and still leaves the unit cash-positive every month at 3% EBITDA — it simply does not repay its capital inside five years.

Medium

Currency and pricing

The dinar trades at 1,310 official and ~1,530 parallel — a 16–17% premium. Equipment and beans are bought in dollars; the menu is sold in dinars.

Mitigation. The model uses the parallel rate throughout, never the official one. Menu prices are reviewed twice a year. The stress_fx case models a further 14% adverse move with no price response and still clears 13% EBITDA.

Medium

Classification as a deluxe venue

Iraq has no general VAT but levies a 10% sales tax on deluxe restaurants and hotels. A café selling a $9.80 drink may well be classified deluxe.

Mitigation. Assumed in the base case, not treated as an upside. A further 10% on top is modelled separately and the unit still clears 17% EBITDA.

Medium

Foreign ownership and repatriation

Foreign ownership of an Iraqi LLC is generally capped at 49%, and five banks plus three payment firms have been barred from the CBI dollar auction.

Mitigation. The Iraqi operating company is majority Iraqi-owned by design. The franchisor IP entity sits offshore and is paid royalties under a registered licence — the standard structure, and the reason the IP must be filed before the first franchise agreement is signed, not after.

Medium

Security and perception

The US State Department maintains a Level 4 ‘Do Not Travel’ advisory for Iraq.

Mitigation. It is also true that Iraq recorded $5.7bn of tourism revenue in 2024, up 25%, that Baghdad was Arab Tourism Capital 2025, and that Half Million, %Arabica and Kyan are all opening there. Both facts belong in the same paragraph. Practically: 24-hour site security is in the cost base, and no international staff are required to operate a unit.

Low

The word's darker senses

Lisān al-ʿArab notes that the Arabs called poison and death شَبِم, for their coldness.

Mitigation. A lexicographer's footnote, not live usage. Disclosed here so it is not discovered later, and consciously kept out of the family-facing brand voice.

What is still unknown

A plan that does not list its own gaps is a pitch, not a plan. These are the open items, in the order they should be closed.

  1. Every equipment line needs a real supplier quote. Clear-ice plant, ULT freezers and the sorbet mould system are quote-only categories and the CAPEX table will move when they come back.
  2. Baghdad rent and key money are anchored on two sourced listings and one observed key-money ratio. Three real landlord conversations on actual target corners would replace an estimate with a number.
  3. Talabat's and Lezzoo's Iraq commission rates are regional figures, not Iraq-verified. A merchant conversation settles it.
  4. Trademark registers have not been searched. SAIP (Saudi) and the Iraqi Ministry of Industry and Minerals need formal clearance searches before any spend on the wordmark — SAIP's public database shows registered rights only, not pending applications.
  5. Whether Iraq is a Madrid Protocol party determines whether one international filing works or seven national ones are needed. Unverified.
  6. Three of the six replacement verses (al-Ṣanawbarī, al-Buḥturī, al-Farazdaq) are high-confidence on attribution but need their exact wording confirmed against printed critical editions before they go on a primary SKU.
  7. Whether شبم carries any unintended sense in Baghdadi, Basrawi, Khaliji, Egyptian or Levantine colloquial has not been tested. Research covered Modern Standard Arabic and the medieval lexica only.
  8. Whether SHABIM falls inside Iraq's 'deluxe restaurant' classification for the 10% sales tax. Assumed yes; needs a ruling from a local tax adviser.
