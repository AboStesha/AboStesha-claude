# Axi Iraq · Copy Trading reel · script v2 (reviewed)

- **Format:** 9:16, 1080x1920, 30.0 s at 30 fps, no voice-over. Music and SFX only, and all meaning sits in on-screen text and visuals.
- **Grid:** 124 BPM (beat 0.484 s, bar 1.935 s), locked to `src/music/grid.json` (Mixkit #1012 "Take this Higher", see MUSIC.md). Structure: hook (intro bar, claps and plucks, no kick), drop A, break (stop bar), drop B, final hit and ring-out. Every cut lands on a grid line. Frame numbers (`f`) are the frame that holds the kick attack.
- **Facts:** every claim maps to `FACTS.md` (fact IDs in brackets).
- **Cast:** Noor (the copier, primary Axi Iraq face) and Saeed (the trader being copied, Axi global ME persona). That makes two personas, the brand maximum (G-IQ p.46). No new faces.

## Language decision (read first)

The workflow brief asked for Iraqi dialect. The Axi Iraq guidelines (p.58) set **الفصحى البيضاء** (white, simplified Modern Standard Arabic) for all official material and allow dialect only in comment replies and casual Stories. The client asked for everything "like it's coming directly from the brand guidelines", so the reel copy is white fusha tuned for Iraqi ears: everyday words, no literary constructions, readable from Mosul to Basra. There is one Iraqi-dialect variant, for Stories only, in section 6.

**Display-copy rules (v2):** no diacritics (no shadda, tanween or harakat). Hamza, taa marbuta and alif maqsura are spelled correctly. No full stop at the end of a display line, but question marks stay. Each line has five words or fewer.

## 1. Hook

### Five options

| # | Line 1 | Line 2 (lands on the drop) | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | «لا وقت؟ لا خبرة؟» | «انسخ صفقات أصحاب الخبرة» | Double objection, then the mechanism |
| H2 | «تسهر على الشاشات كل ليلة؟» | «ودع السهر، وانسخ» | Pain scenario [1.7] |
| H3 | «صفقات أفضل المتداولين» | «في هاتفك» | Curiosity and brand claim [1.5] |
| H4 | «لا تعرف من أين تبدأ؟» | «ابدأ بالنسخ» | Beginner self-identification |
| H5 | «ماذا لو نسخت صفقاتهم؟» | (over Saeed's trader card) | "What if" curiosity |

### Chosen: H1, «لا وقت؟ لا خبرة؟» then «انسخ صفقات أصحاب الخبرة»

Why H1 wins:
1. **It reads in under a second per hit.** Each question is two words and lands on its own pluck accent (0.000 s and 0.968 s). The intro bar has claps and plucks but no kick. The thumb is still moving when the viewer has read both.
2. **It names the two real barriers.** The guidelines describe the Learner as 20-30 years old, learning on the phone and afraid of losing (p.7), and Aisha as the professional who wants "less time" (p.48). "No time" and "no experience" self-qualify both groups. That gives the widest lead net without a profit hook.
3. **The answer is the product.** «انسخ صفقات أصحاب الخبرة» explains copy trading in four words, landing on the drop at 1.935 s. It mirrors Axi's own «انسخ صفقات أفضل المتداولين» [1.2] and «المتداولين الأكثر خبرة» [1.6], and keeps the wordplay خبرة → أصحاب الخبرة. It makes no promise and shows no number. v1's «انسخ أصحاب الخبرة» could read as "imitate the experts". You copy their trades, not the people.
4. **It is on-voice.** It respects the beginner and creates no FOMO (p.58-59). It also follows the cover rule "strong question on a persona image" (p.42).
5. **It loops.** The film ends on the same Noor close-up, so the replay opens on the two questions again and the reel invites a second watch.

### Persistent layers
- **Watermark:** white Axi logo (alone) at 60% opacity and 54 px wide (5% of frame width), on the left margin (x = 54), for the full 30 s (p.44). It is lifted to y ≈ 1590-1630 so the Reels caption and username overlay don't cover it (BRAND §5 adaptation).
- **Opening lockup:** white «آكسي العراق» Arabic lockup, at least 120 px wide (the IQ minimum), top-right on the 54 px margin at y ≈ 200-360, below the top 10% zone. It shows 0-1.5 s, laid over the hook frame. The hook frame already sits on the brand's grey gradient. **Needs the brand manager's sign-off**, because p.44 describes a dedicated 1.5 s lockup open, and a 1.5 s logo before the hook would cost the scroll-stop.
- **Illustration tags:** «للتوضيح فقط» on every mock trader card or UI with stats (beats 04, 05, 06, 14). Under beats 04-05, add «الأداء السابق لا يضمن النتائج المستقبلية».
- **Risk strip (26.613-30.000):** a box at y ≈ 1350-1580 (above the bottom 15% zone and above the watermark), x 54-940 (clear of the Reels icon rail). White 92%, Carbon text, Rabar Regular ≥ 39 px, at most 3 lines of ≤ 70 characters.

### Text system (applies to every beat)
- **Fonts:** Rabar 018 Light for display headlines, Rabar 016 Bold for the emphasis word and the pill, Rabar 017 Regular for sublines, micro lines and the risk strip. Use Axi Font Regular (Inter fallback) for Latin, step numbers `01 02 03` and tokens (p.32). Rabar is not installed here. Get the TTFs from the asset pack (IQ p.69); if they can't be obtained, the sanctioned fallback is Noto Sans Arabic (GL p.35/37).
- **Sizes at 1080x1920:** headline 96-120 px (IQ p.33), subline 48-56 px, micro 34-38 px, risk strip ≥ 39 px (2% of the height).
- **Placement:** keep all text inside y 192-1632 (10%-85%, p.44) and x 54-1026. Below y ≈ 1150, keep it at x ≤ 940, clear of the Reels icon rail on the right.
- **Animate Arabic by word or by mask, never by letter.** Splitting letters breaks the joining, and that includes "typing" effects: a caret steps one word at a time. Every reveal, wipe and whip moves right-to-left, the reading direction.
- **No diacritics in display copy, no terminal full stops, Arabic tracking 0. No kashida, no fake bold or italic, no justify** (p.33, p.35).
- **Colour:** white text on dark glass and Carbon #242528 text on light glass. Red only for the step numbers, the underline stroke, the pills, the devices and the end card, which keeps red at 10% or less of each frame outside the end card. The full-red frames in beat 03 last 4 frames or fewer. **No mint, and no green anywhere**: the reel shows no gains (p.28).

## 6. Three alternative hooks (for A/B tests)

1. **H2:** «تسهر على الشاشات كل ليلة؟» then «ودع السهر، وانسخ». Best for **active traders** who already trade and burn out. Open on beat 09's laptop-close shot. [1.7]
2. **H3:** «صفقات أفضل المتداولين» then «في هاتفك». A curiosity open on Saeed's trader card (beat 04) with the red phone in hand. Best for **cold, broad** audiences. Lowest objection-handling, highest intrigue. [1.5]
