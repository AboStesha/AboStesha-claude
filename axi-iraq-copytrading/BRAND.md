## 1. Brand architecture and naming
- Axi Global is the master brand. **Axi Iraq is a regional sub-brand** sitting directly beneath it, and Arabic programmes and campaigns sit under Axi Iraq. Programmes such as Axi Select keep their own logos and are never locked up with the Axi Iraq logo (IQ p6).
- Correct names (IQ p8): Arabic running text **آكسي العراق**, and **آكسي** for the master brand. English text uses **Axi Iraq**. Inside the logo the words are always lowercase (`axi` / `iraq`). Handles are **@axi.iraq · #AxiIraq · #TradeYourEdge**. Never write أكسي عراق, اكسي العراق, آكسي عراق, AXI IRAQ, Axi-Iraq or AxiIraq in body text.
- Profile display name: **آكسي العراق | Axi Iraq** (IQ p40).
- **Tagline:** `Trade Your Edge` stays in English everywhere. The approved Arabic companion is **تداول بميزتك**. Never use تداول حافتك, تداول ميزتك or كن الأفضل (IQ p5, p59).
- Brand promise (IQ p5): «نمنح المتداول العراقي ميزته: أدوات عالمية، وتعليم بالعربية، ودعم يفهم سوقه.»
- Approved headline lines seen in IQ: «ميزتك في كل سوق» (p33/38), «من بغداد إلى كل الأسواق» (p38), «تداول بلا حدود» (p64), «أكثر من 220 منتجاً بمنصّة واحدة، ودعم بالعربية» (p50). GL equivalent: "Your Edge In Every Market".
- Language order (IQ p7): **Arabic first, Kurdish second, English only for tokens** (pair codes, technical terms, tagline). Target cities: Baghdad, Erbil, Basra, Mosul, Sulaymaniyah.

## 3. Typography (IQ p31–35; GL p30–37)
| Role | Font | Size at 1080 px (IQ p33) | Line height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Main headline, covers, hero lines | **Rabar 018 Light** | 96–120 px | 1.25 |
| Subhead | **Rabar 017 Regular** | 48–56 px | 1.35 |
| Body | Rabar 017 Regular | 28–32 px | 1.7 |
| Caption | Rabar 017 Regular | 20–22 px | 1.5 |
| Hero number (for example `220+`) | **Axi Font Regular** (Displaay Foundry). **Fallback: Inter** | 200–320 px | 1.0 |
| Button / CTA | **Rabar 016 Bold** | 28 px (CTA pill on IQ p43: 30 px) | 1.0 |
| Latin, numbers, tickers, codes | Axi Font Regular / Medium / Semibold. **Inter fallback** (IQ p32; the IQ PDF itself uses Inter) | — | — |

- The hierarchy is **"headline big and light, body regular and comfortable, emphasis bold and rare"** (IQ p33). The Light weight carries the brand voice. Never use Bold for headlines.
- **Arabic tracking is always 0.** Latin tracking: headlines −1.5%, captions +4% (IQ p33). GL p33: headline Regular, −1.5%, line height 115%, Title Case; body +2%, 130%; caption +4%, 130%, UPPERCASE. Dashboard numbers: Semibold, 0%, 120%, **monospace lining** figures (GL p34).
- **Numbers** (IQ p34):
  - Always Western digits 0–9 in the Axi Latin font, including inside Arabic text. For example «زوج EUR/USD عند 1.0842 · سبريد 0.0 نقطة».
  - Eastern digits (٠–٩) are allowed only in long narrative text, never in prices, percentages or tables.
  - Pair codes stay in Latin script (EUR/USD, XAU/USD, BTC/USD).
  - Terms: Arabic first, with the English term in parentheses at first appearance only, for example الهامش (Margin).
  - Formats: `$500`, `%14`, decimal **point** (not comma).
- **Arabic typesetting** (IQ p35, mandatory):
  - Right-aligned. Centre only short headlines.
  - **Headline line ≤ 5 words.** Text line ≤ 70 characters.
  - Diacritics only where a word would be ambiguous. Hamza, taa marbuta and alif maqsura must be correct.
  - No space before punctuation, one space after.
  - No kashida stretching. No fake italic or bold. No full English words mixed into an Arabic sentence (tokens are allowed). No justify on short text. No English caps inside an Arabic sentence.
  - **Check RTL shaping before export.**
- **Font availability:** Rabar is **not installed** on this machine. The PDF embeds only subsets of Rabar_016/017/018. Get the TTFs (`Rabar_016/017/018`) from the asset pack (IQ p69). If they cannot be obtained, use GL's sanctioned Arabic fallback **Noto Sans Arabic** (GL p35/37). Inter is installed (`/usr/share/fonts/opentype/inter/`).

## 4. Logos (IQ p9–26; GL p7–17)
- **Primary lockup** (IQ p10): a vertical lockup with two equal versions. The original Axi wordmark stays untouched, with the word placed beneath it, right-aligned under the `xi`.
  - **Arabic `العراق`** in Rabar Bold, weight-matched to the wordmark. This is the default for all Arabic material, so it is **the version for this reel**.
  - **Latin `iraq`** is built from the Axi letters. Use it for English or bilingual material.
  - Never show both words together, and never put any other word (Baghdad, Erbil, Academy) under the mark (IQ p11).
- **Construction** (IQ p12): 1 unit = stroke thickness. The word is as wide as `xi` (5.7 units) and its right edge aligns with the right edge of `i`. The vertical gap is 0.4 × x-height.
- **Horizontal lockup** (IQ p13): `axi | iraq` on a shared baseline, word at 62%, divider 0.16 unit thick at 35% opacity of the logo colour. Use it only when the available height is under 60% of the width (headers, strips).
- **Clear space** (IQ p14): **2 units = 2× the width of the `i`**, measured from the i-dot and the bottom of the word. **Minimum size:** 120 px digital, 25 mm print, horizontal lockup 180 px. Below that, use the Axi logo alone.
- **Axi logo alone / compact** (IQ p20–22, GL p8–12):
  - Use it for the app icon, avatar, favicon, **video watermark**, **video close / end card**, small merchandise, and anything under 120 px.
  - Clear space is 1 unit (the `i` width). Minimum 20 px digital, 6 mm print. Sizing ladder: 120/100/80/50/30/20 px.
  - Never derive a sub-mark from it: no `X` alone, no `ax`, no lone i-dot (IQ p21, p26). The old "X" sub-mark is retired (IQ p69).
- **Approved colours** (IQ p15):
  - Ink on white or light. This is the default.
  - **White on Axi red, dark gradient, or primary gradient.**
  - Red on white, only when the logo is the sole element.
  - Promo-only exception: axi in ink with **العراق / iraq in red** on white.
  - GL primary variants: white on main gradient, red on off-white. Alternatives: black on white, white on black, white on red (GL p10–11).
- **Placement** (IQ p16):
  - In RTL layouts the logo sits **top-right** on the margin. The opposite corner (bottom-left) holds the small red triangle or the CTA.
  - Centre the logo only on covers and opening screens.
  - Logo width is **14% of layout width in posts and 10% in video**, but never below the 120 px minimum. At 1080 px wide, 10% is only 108 px, so **use ≥ 120 px**.
- **Misuse** (IQ p17, p26): no stretching, recolouring, rotation, re-ratio, shadow, gradient, outline or stroke. Don't move or re-typeset the word, don't place the logo on busy imagery, and don't combine both versions.
- **Avatar** (IQ p23): white Axi logo on red. Circle: logo at 56% of the container. Square app icon: corner radius 22%, logo at 62%.
- **Partner lockups** (IQ p18): not needed here. **No other brand logos in frame** (IQ p41, p44).

**Extracted files.** `assets/logo/` contains transparent PNG cut-outs made from 600-dpi renders of IQ p10, p13 and p21. Ink is `#090A0D`, red is `#E4002E`. IQ p69 rejects work built on screenshot or low-res logos. These cut-outs are about 2,970 px wide, so they are clean for 1080p, but request the official SVG pack (`AxiIraq_Logo_[Arabic|Latin]_[Ink|White|Red].svg`, `Axi_Logo_Official_[Red|Ink|White].svg`) before final delivery.

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|

| Subtitles / captions | Rabar Regular at **4% of frame height (≈77 px)**, max 2 lines, centred, white on a semi-transparent strip in the lower third, above the platform UI |

| **Disclosure** | **Risk-warning strip during the last 3 s** of every promotional video |
| Presenters | **Noor** for Arabic content. Sarmad and Rojin for Kurdish. Otherwise only official Axi personas, dressed in the palette with **one red element**, on **cool grey studio**. No other logos in frame |

- **9:16 grid** (IQ p37): **5% margins (54 px)**, 4 columns. Text occupies 60–70% of the width (648–756 px). Text flows from the right, and the CTA closes on the left or below the text. Images fill the frame or a full column; never place small floating images centre-frame.
- **Watermark conflict:** the bottom-left watermark falls inside the area Instagram covers with the caption and username overlay. Keep it on the left margin (x = 54) but lift it to just above the 15% zone (about y ≈ 1590–1630) so it stays visible. This adapts the rule; IQ does not specify it.
- **Right rail (recommendation, not in IQ):** keep text out of the right 140 px from mid-frame down, because of the Reels like, comment and share buttons.

## 6. Layout system and components (IQ p25, p36–43; GL p66–93)
- **Graphic device** (IQ p25, GL p66): the **only** graphic device is the **small red Axi triangle**.
  - Shape (measured): a right-angle wedge, right angle at bottom-right, apex slightly flattened, hypotenuse about 50° (it echoes the í accent). File: `assets/ui/axi_red_triangle_device.png`.
  - Uses: a regular **low-opacity grid** in backgrounds; a single triangle in **image and card corners** (1% of layout width, **max 4**); **bullets** (0.6 × letter height).
  - Never use logo letters as a pattern.
  - GL social posts put tiny red triangles in all four corners of each card (GL p69–72, p87–93). IQ cards use **two, in the bottom corners** (IQ p41, p43).
- **Five core layouts** (IQ p38): big headline · hero number (`220+`) · highlight quote (one red word) · data card (white card: `EUR/USD · اليوم`, `1.0842`, mint `+0.42%`) · portrait plus message.

Inner slides are white, with an image or **app mockup** at top, a **red numbered title** and Rabar Regular body. One idea per slide. The risk strip goes on **every** slide.

## 10. Motion
**From the guidelines.** Only the reel structure in §5 is specified: 1.5 s ident, constant watermark, 1 s red end card, CTA screen, 3 s risk strip. The GL key visual implies a tilted card carousel with depth (GL p68, p103). Nothing defines easing or transitions.

**Derived recommendations** (not in IQ or GL). Energetic but clean, matching the "calm expert" voice:
- **Cuts and wipes:** cut on beat. Use the **50° triangle wedge** (the í angle) as the wipe and mask shape, plus the **44 px chamfer** as a reveal edge.
- **Glass:** cards slide in with blur ramping 0 → 22 px and opacity 0 → 22% / 72%. Use a short white sheen sweep across the card, as on GL p86.
- **Headlines (Rabar Light):** per-word rise and fade (RTL order), 8–12 frames per word, ease-out cubic. The red word lands last with a small scale settle (1.06 → 1.0).
- **Numbers:** monospace lining roll-ups in Inter or Axi Font.
- **Camera:** parallax pushes on the 3D hero objects. Card-stack "copy" motion: a trade card duplicates into the viewer's card.
- **Avoid:** glitch, RGB split, neon, lens flares, kashida stretches, spinning or 3D-extruded logo, coloured text on gradients.

## 11. Voice, tone and copy (IQ p57–59)
- **Calm expert who respects the trader's mind:** clear, precise, **no promises, no shouting**.
  - **Clear:** short sentences, one idea each, terms explained.
  - **Confident:** no "maybe" about facts, no "guaranteed" about forecasts.
  - **Respectful:** no condescension, **no FOMO threats**. "A reference, not a seller": educate before offering, disclose before promising.
  - **Local:** knows Baghdad, Erbil and Basra, local times and holidays, and mentions them only when they serve the message.
- **Language level:** "white fusha", simplified MSA understood from Mosul to Basra. **No Iraqi dialect in official materials.** Dialect is allowed only in friendly comment replies and casual stories, sparingly. **This reel's on-screen copy should be simplified MSA.**
- **Do and don't** (IQ p59):
  - "بونص 30% على إيداعك الأول. تُطبّق الشروط." ✓ vs "اربح فلوس مضاعفة!! عرض لن يتكرر" ✗
  - "ابدأ بالأساسيات: الرافعة والهامش ووقف الخسارة قبل أول صفقة." ✓ vs "صير محترف بأسبوع" ✗
  - "الذهب عند $2,412 بعد بيانات التضخّم…" ✓ vs "الذهب سيصعد اليوم بإذن الله، ادخلوا شراء" ✗
- **Copy-trading framing:**
  - Write "choose, review past performance, copy automatically, stay in control (stop or adjust anytime)".
  - **Don't** write passive or guaranteed-income promises ("let them earn for you", "دخل ثابت", "relax while you profit").
  - The holiday and family idea must read as "time and flexibility", not "money without risk".

## 12. Compliance (IQ p60, p8, p40–44; GL p70)
- **Banned words and claims:** ربح مضمون · بلا مخاطر · دخل ثابت · مضاعفة رأس المال · توصية مؤكدة · إشارة 100% · لا تخسر أبداً · «مرخّص في العراق» (unless literally true under the licence) · **any specific numeric return promise**.
- **Mandatory in every promotional piece:**
  - Risk-warning line with type **≥ 2% of design height (≥ 39 px on 1920)**.
  - **Licensed entity name in the footer.** Posts use «تروّج بواسطة شركة AxiTrader LLC» (IQ p41 strip). Legal names its form, and it appears only in footers and disclosures (IQ p8).
