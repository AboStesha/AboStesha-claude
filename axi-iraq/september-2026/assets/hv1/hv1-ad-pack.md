## 1–3 · Research insight, trend, relevance
- **Insight (file 02):** Exness proved cinematic identity films work for brokers with zero profit claims; the algorithm's keep/kill decision happens in ~1.5–3s and pattern interrupts + surprising familiar imagery are the top-retaining openers; in Iraq, authentic local texture beats corporate polish, and with the "forex = scam" press climate, identity/education is the only message with tailwinds.
- **Trend used:** "cinematic locality": macro textures of everyday economic life, sound-led open, 2–2.5s cut rhythm, no talking head, burned captions.
- **Why now:** relaunch statement piece. It tells the existing audience "this page changed" without saying it, carries zero compliance surface (no numbers, no promises, no platform shots), and anchors the VIDEO family on the refreshed grid.

## 4–7 · Audience, objective, message, concept
- **Audience:** potential traders 18–35 (deck segment 5) + the broad Everyman audience; Arabic account (Kurdish edition follows with learn-only CTA).
- **Objective:** reframe "the market" from distant casino to daily literacy; establish the premium relaunch voice; produce a save/share brand film the paid team can run for awareness.
- **Core message:** السوق مو مكان بعيد. هو الأسعار اللي تعيشها كل يوم. وتعلّم قراءته مهارة حياة.
- **Concept:** four pairings, each cutting from a familiar Iraqi price moment to the global market behind it: gold souq ↔ gold market · exchange office ↔ currency pairs · Basra cranes/fuel ↔ energy markets · supermarket receipt ↔ inflation and rates. The film never shows a trading screen. It ends on the invitation to learn the language.

## 20–24 · Edit, sound, references
