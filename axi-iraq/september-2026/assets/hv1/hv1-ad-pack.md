The concept, script, sound and structure SURVIVED the trends audit (daily-price habit = Iraq's #1 content loop validates the premise). Two things did not, and are changed:

1. **Production method: AI-cinematic → FILMED-LOCAL macro b-roll.** Evidence: documented 2026 AI-ad authenticity backlash (54% AI fatigue; TikTok's mandatory AI labels measurably depress trust; nothing shows AI-cinematic collecting Iraqi audiences, while June's biggest Iraqi wave was a real street frame). Master still locks Aug 29.
2. **A human face enters the first 3 seconds.** Evidence: face-led opens retain best; street money vox-pops are the best-verified collectors globally and the hottest frame in Iraq. The scale-slam hook stays (now real footage), but shot 2 (goldsmith's hands + face) moves up to start at ~1.2s. And a fourth hook variant joins paid testing:
   - **Hook D (street-question open):** real passerby/vendor asked on the street: «شگد سعر الدولار اليوم؟» smash-cut montage of three different people answering instantly, then: «كلنا نعرف الأسعار. اليوم نتعلم شنو تقول.» → film continues. This merges Iraq's hottest visual grammar with our exact premise and is the strongest test candidate against Hook A.
3. Distribution note for management: mirror the Reel organically to TikTok (Iraq's largest audience) while IG stays the brand anchor; if any AI-assisted realistic imagery is ever used there, it must carry the platform's AI label — one more reason the hero stays filmed.

## 1–3 · Research insight, trend, relevance
- **Insight (file 02):** Exness proved cinematic identity films work for brokers with zero profit claims; the algorithm's keep/kill decision happens in ~1.5–3s and pattern interrupts + surprising familiar imagery are the top-retaining openers; in Iraq, authentic local texture beats corporate polish, and with the "forex = scam" press climate, identity/education is the only message with tailwinds.
- **Trend used:** "cinematic locality": macro textures of everyday economic life, sound-led open, 2–2.5s cut rhythm, no talking head, burned captions.
- **Why now:** relaunch statement piece. It tells the existing audience "this page changed" without saying it, carries zero compliance surface (no numbers, no promises, no platform shots), and anchors the VIDEO family on the refreshed grid.

## 4–7 · Audience, objective, message, concept
- **Audience:** potential traders 18–35 (deck segment 5) + the broad Everyman audience; Arabic account (Kurdish edition follows with learn-only CTA).
- **Objective:** reframe "the market" from distant casino to daily literacy; establish the premium relaunch voice; produce a save/share brand film the paid team can run for awareness.
- **Core message:** السوق مو مكان بعيد. هو الأسعار اللي تعيشها كل يوم. وتعلّم قراءته مهارة حياة.
- **Concept:** four pairings, each cutting from a familiar Iraqi price moment to the global market behind it: gold souq ↔ gold market · exchange office ↔ currency pairs · Basra cranes/fuel ↔ energy markets · supermarket receipt ↔ inflation and rates. The film never shows a trading screen. It ends on the invitation to learn the language.

## 8–10 · Hooks
- **A (recommended):** macro gold scale slams, coins ring, freeze. VO: **«هذا مو ذهب. هذا سعر.»**

- **C (type-only pattern interrupt):** black frame, red type: «السوق مو مكان. السوق حواليك.»

## 20–24 · Edit, sound, references
- **Rhythm:** hook ≤2.5s → cuts every 2.5–3.5s → hold shot 8 (human) longest → calm end card. Burned captions (cream box, red keyword) per file 09 §16 style.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts only; one match-cut (scale pan → crane silhouette shape); no whips/zoom transitions.
- **Sound design:** scale clink (the hook IS the sound), souq room tone, note-counting flutter, port low hum, receipt paper, tea pour; music enters shot 3: low percussive pulse + santur/oud accent, builds to shot 8, drops for end card sting.
- **Music mood:** restrained cinematic Iraqi-modern hybrid; licensed library track (no AI music on brand hero).
- **Visual references (described):** Exness "Born to Trade" (discipline-identity register, not its imagery); premium bank brand films' macro-texture language; documentary souq photography (Qaysari/Shorja light) as color reference.
