# 07 — Real-Life Film: «أهل السوق» (People of the Market)

**Slot:** HV4 · Shoot **Tue–Wed 9–10 Sept** · Publish **Wed 23 Sept, 19:00 Baghdad** · 9:16, 45–60s main + 30s + 15s cuts
**Format family (research-based):** street-documentary interview + reveal — currently the highest-performing real-life class in MENA finance/education Reels (authentic > polished; Rudaw's Reels-first playbook; hook science file 02 §C8). Related to the vision deck's "Trade with the People" idea, rebuilt as **education-first** (no prize accounts, no chart-reading contest — those trip compliance and the KRI promotion ban).

## 1. Main concept
Iraq's oldest markets already think in spreads, liquidity and risk — they just don't use those words. We interview real market people — a goldsmith, a currency-exchange dealer, a wholesale merchant (Erbil Qaysari Bazaar; optional Baghdad Shorja/gold souq day for the Arabic account) — asking them how they price, when they refuse a deal, how they protect themselves from a bad day. Then we name what they described: «هذا اسمه سبريد.» «هذي إدارة مخاطر.» The reveal: the global market runs on instincts Iraqis already respect — and the difference between instinct and skill is structured knowledge.

## 2. Why it can work
- Local pride + recognition (grandfather's souq) meets aspiration (global markets) — the exact emotional bridge the Everyman archetype wants.
- Interview/vox formats are algorithm-native, cheap, and endlessly cuttable (each vendor = a bonus clip).
- It converts the "forex = scam" suspicion by grounding Axi in the most trusted commercial institutions in the country.
- Kurdish-first shoot location (Erbil HQ advantage) gives the Kurdish account a NATIVE hero asset, not a translation — while staying education/brand-only (KRI-safe framing).

## 3–4. Hooks
- **1-second hook:** gold scale slams onto the counter, coins ring — freeze — «هذا أقدم "شارت" بالعراق.»
- **3-second hook:** dealer counting notes at speed, direct question VO: «تدري شنو مشترك بين هذا الرجّال وبين وول ستريت؟»
- **Alt hooks:** A: «سألنا أقدم صايغ بالسوق: شلون تسعّر الذهب؟» · B: «هذا الرجل يدير مخاطر أحسن من متداولين كثار — بدون ما يعرف.» · C (Kurdish edition native hook): the Qaysari alley reveal shot.

## 5. Script / interview spine (Arabic master; Kurdish takes same-day)
1. Hook (scale slam).
2. Q1 to goldsmith: «شلون تحدد سعر اليوم؟» (answer will reference global gold price + margin → we caption: «سعر عالمي + هامش = سبريد»).
3. Q2 to exchange dealer: «متى ترفض صفقة؟» (→ caption: «إدارة مخاطر»).
4. Q3 to merchant: «شنو تسوي بيوم السوق الهادئ؟» (→ caption: «سيولة»).
5. Turn (VO): «نفس المبادئ… بس بسوق أكبر. الفرق؟ المعرفة المنظمة.»
6. End card: «السوق بدمنا. المعرفة تكملها.» + Axi + Academy CTA (Arabic) / learn-only CTA (Kurdish) + `[RISK-AR/KU]`.

## 6. Talent
Real vendors (2–3), recruited via the Erbil office's local relationships; signed release forms (prepare AR + KU versions); offer name/no-name choice; one Axi team member off-camera as interviewer (warm, respectful, unhurried). No actors.

## 7. Location
Primary: **Qaysari Bazaar, Erbil** (character, permission via bazaar administration + vendor consent; shoot 09:30–12:30 before crowds peak). Optional day 2: Baghdad gold souq / Shorja for Arabic-account-native footage. Verify local filming permission requirements — flag to office manager.

## 8. Props
None fabricated — the souq provides everything (scales, ledgers, note counters, tea). Bring: lav mics ×2, phone gimbal or mirrorless + 35mm, ND filter, reflector, release forms, petty cash for courtesy purchases.

## 10. B-roll bank (shoot extra)
Coins, ledger pages, scale needle settling, shutters opening, prayer beads + phone side by side, generational shot (older vendor + young assistant) — this becomes September story-frame stock.

## 11. Camera movements
Gimbal push-ins only (no whips), locked-off interviews, one slow-mo wide, macro handheld with elbow brace. Every move motivated — souq is busy enough.

## 12. Lighting
Natural + practicals; reflector for interview fill; avoid noon top-light (shoot window 09:30–12:30); white balance to warm (souq tungsten), protect Axi-red accuracy on the end card.

## 13. Wardrobe
Vendors: exactly as they are (that's the point). Interviewer/team: plain navy/charcoal, no logos except a small Axi pin `[if approved]`.

## 14. Performance direction
Questions conversational, let silences breathe; never correct a vendor's answer — we caption the concept name over THEIR words; re-ask, don't script; capture their pride ("منو علمك؟" — "who taught you?" — gold for the longer cut).

## 15. Editing rhythm
Hook ≤1.5s → cuts every 2–2.5s during answers → hold 4s on the best human beat (laugh/hands) → fast caption-reveal montage at the turn → calm 3s end card. Subtitles burned (Iraqi transcription verbatim, clean-MSA concept captions).

## 16. Subtitle style
Per file 09: cream box, dark text, 2 lines max, keyword in Axi red (the named concept: سبريد/مخاطر/سيولة), safe-zone compliant.

## 17. Sound
Lavs on vendors; wild sound of the souq recorded 60s for the bed; music: minimal percussion entering at the turn only; end sting.

## 18. CTA
