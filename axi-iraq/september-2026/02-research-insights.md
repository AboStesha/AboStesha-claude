## A. The two findings that change how we operate

### A1. ⚠️ Kurdistan Region legal risk — escalate before any Sorani publishing
- **April 2025:** Erbil's Asayish banned forex/crypto trading **and its promotion/advertising**, warning companies and brokers of legal repercussions; the owner of "Twana FX" was **arrested** for promoting forex via social media and paid courses (964media; The New Region; Rudaw). KRG Interior Ministry **repeated the warning May 2026** (Rudaw).
- Central Bank of Iraq licenses **no** retail forex/CFD firms; investigative press frames forex as a scam wave ("victims lose billions" — NIRIJ, Nov 2024). Public sentiment links "forex" with fraud.
- **Operating consequences (already applied across this system):**
  1. **Kurdish account = education & brand only.** No deposit/trade CTAs in Sorani. CTAs become "learn / follow / ask." Even demo-account CTAs held until legal sign-off.
  2. **Mandatory item:** Axi compliance/legal reviews the entire Kurdish September plan before anything publishes. This is request #0 to management.
  3. On the Arabic account, education-first framing is not just brand strategy — it is risk management. Anti-scam literacy content ("شلون تعرف المنصة الموثوقة؟") positions Axi as the credible adult in the room and rides the one forex narrative with tailwinds.
  4. **Sept 11** = September (Eylul) Revolution anniversary saturates Kurdish feeds — no promotional posting on the Kurdish account that day.
