## E2. Current-trends addendum: Iraq/MENA feeds, June–Aug 2026 (researched 15 Aug)
*Method: proxy blocked all direct platform fetches; findings via search-index captures of rankings, regional press and platform pages. Tiers as marked.*

1. **[ENGAGEMENT SIGNAL] The street-interview frame is Iraq's hottest visual grammar right now.** June 2026's nationwide meme wave («عيونج عرضن كبالي», the Al Sharqiya night vox-pop clip) turned the news-interview frame (night street, channel mic, lower-third) into a template millions played with, using AI face-swap as participatory meme tooling. Salary/price vox-pops («شكد راتب…», Shorja price content) are a native Iraqi genre with whole TikTok discover clusters.
2. **[VERIFIED ecosystem / inferred engagement] Daily dollar/gold price content is Iraq's most habitual content loop.** ~20% official-vs-parallel spread (parallel ~152–157k per $100 vs CBI ~130k, Jun–Aug 2026), minute-by-minute Telegram bourse channels (t.me/dollariraqi), dedicated rate apps, every outlet publishing dailies, even provincial governments reposting the Kifah rate; 964 Arabic TikTok ~514K followers. **Implication for «نبض السوق»: strongest possible product-market fit; handle USD/IQD content carefully (CBI sensitivity, explain never speculate — file 02 §B).**
3. **[ENGAGEMENT SIGNAL, multi-source] 2026 AI-ad authenticity backlash is documented** ("consumers call out anything that sounds machine-generated"; winning brands use AI as invisible infrastructure); nothing retrieved shows AI-cinematic ads collecting Iraqi audiences; creator-content appetite is soaring (eMarketer ME 2026). **Verdict: filmed-local beats AI-cinematic for HERO ads; AI belongs backstage (variants, cleanup, captions) or playfully inside meme templates.**
4. **[VERIFIED] Platform strategy:** TikTok is Iraq's #1 platform (~34.3M) but carries ban risk (parliament request pending, 410 accounts geo-blocked H2-2024, CBI banned TikTok-agent payments Dec 2024); Instagram (~23.6M, male-skewed 25–34) is the stable brand rail. Reels-first, TikTok-mirrored organic.
5. **[VERIFIED] Compliance climate hardened:** Rafidain Bank public warning against forex/crypto; KRG ordered crypto/forex offices closed (Jun 2025); UAE SCA finfluencer licensing (May 2025) signals the regional direction. Education-information framing is the only durable lane.
6. **[ENGAGEMENT SIGNAL] Dialect > MSA** for engagement (multi-source industry consensus); Rudaw's Sorani hard-news Reels average 500–600K views (up from 70–80K after Reels-first revamp; 3.72B views in the first war month, Mar 2026) — Kurdish audiences reward native-language urgency, not translations.
7. **[HYPOTHESIS] Volatility-reactive slots:** the 2026 Iran-war news cycle moves Iraqi rates and attention within days; pre-approved rapid-turn "what today's move means" frames let the brand show up inside spikes. **[HYPOTHESIS] Creator collabs are cheap right now** (TikTok monetization squeeze → brand deals are creators' main income).
