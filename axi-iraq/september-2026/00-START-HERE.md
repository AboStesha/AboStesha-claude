4. **Axi Select told as discipline, not jackpot** — the program's own mechanics (Edge Score, −10% max loss) are the most compliance-proof differentiation story Axi owns.
5. **Kurdish account runs education/brand-only behind a legal gate** — Erbil banned forex *promotion* (Apr 2025, re-warned May 2026). This is escalation item #0.
