## Design/production notes (all carousels)
- One master template (file 09): cover, content slide, example slide, honest-mirror slide (C4-style), final slide. Build once in week of Aug 24, reuse all month — per-carousel effort drops to copy + 2 custom diagrams.
- RTL swipe logic: slide order right→left; arrows mirrored; cover text zone right-aligned.
- Kurdish builds: duplicate artboards, swap text layer per translated copy, re-fit line lengths (+10–20%), glyph QA (ە ڵ ڕ ێ ۆ), re-export. Never stretch Arabic artwork.
- Alt-text every slide (accessibility + SEO): plain-Arabic description.
