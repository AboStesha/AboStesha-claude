### Visual identity (as observable in the deck — exact values pending the guideline file)
- **Palette:** Axi coral-red + deeper crimson (signature diagonal split), warm cream/off-white, near-black charcoal (Axi Select uses a dark theme with neon-red accents), white. **Exact HEX/CMYK: take from the brand guideline file — do not eyeball final values.**

## 3. Compliance rulebook (applies to every asset, both languages)

### Absolute prohibitions (from your brief + industry standard)
1. **No profit promises** — never: ربح مضمون، أرباح مضمونة، دخل مضمون، نتائج مضمونة، تداول بلا مخاطر، أرباح سهلة، الثراء السريع، "فرصة مؤكدة".
2. **No investment advice / signals** — never: اشترِ الآن، بِع الآن، توصيات، إشارات، "السعر راح يصعد/ينزل", entry/exit levels framed as recommendation. Market info must stay **informational**: what happened / what it means as knowledge — never what the viewer should do with money.
3. **No invented facts** — spreads, execution speeds, client numbers, awards, licenses, offers, instruments, statistics, prices. Unverifiable claim → label `UNVERIFIED — DO NOT USE`.
4. **No fake numbers in visuals** — no AI-generated platform screens with invented balances/prices. Real approved UI recordings only; decorative charts must be clearly abstract (no readable fake prices).
5. **Live market data**: research on publication day, two-source verification, timestamp + Baghdad time + source line, market status honest (open/closed). If unverifiable → `DATA NOT VERIFIED — DO NOT PUBLISH`.
6. **Anti-aesthetic list** (brand protection): no Lamborghinis/mansions/cash rain, no fake lifestyles or balances, no candlestick-wallpaper clichés, no casino energy, no cheap motivational quotes.

### Standing disclaimer system
- Every feed post caption ends with: `[RISK-AR]` → placeholder for the **approved Arabic risk warning** (must be obtained from Axi compliance — Missing Info #2). Until then, nothing publishes without it.
- Kurdish assets end with `[RISK-KU]` (professional translation of the approved text — never machine-translated).
- Market stories carry a micro-line: `معلومة سوقية للأغراض التعليمية — ليست توصية استثمارية` + `[RISK-AR]` short form (pending compliance approval of this exact wording — flag as DRAFT until approved).
- Axi Select content must include eligibility/regulation note per brochure + risk line.

### ⚠ Conflicts found in the vision deck (do not carry into public content without compliance sign-off)
| Deck item | Problem |
|---|---|
| "Deposit Just $500 and You Could Win $1,000,000!" | Win/lottery framing of a funding program — high regulatory risk. Reframe: funding criteria, discipline, up-to language with conditions. |
| "Invest Your Money Instead of Saving It" | Implied advice to invest. Do not use organically. |
| "Success Metrics Stories" (real client profit growth) | Profit showcasing → implied expected returns. Excluded from September system. |
| Elite card "Trading Advice & Market Insights" benefit | "Advice" wording conflicts with no-advice rule — needs compliance wording if ever promoted. |
| "Empower youth to achieve financial freedom" / "easiest ways to grow wealth" | Fine as internal mission language; public copy must avoid wealth-promise phrasing. |
| Part-timers want "signals/recommendations" (segment insight) | A need we explicitly do NOT serve in content; we answer it with education instead. |

### Editorial guardrails for education content
- Teach mechanics (what a pip IS), never outcomes (what you'll earn).
- Every "how it works" example uses clearly hypothetical, labeled numbers: `مثال توضيحي فقط`.
- Psychology content frames risk as something to manage and respect, never to beat.
- Balanced framing: mention losses as normal, leverage cuts both ways — this *builds* the premium credibility we want.
