# 04 — Women's Campaign — حملة «عن دراية»

**Window:** Sun 13 Sept → Fri 25 Sept 2026 (2-week arc inside the month) · **Pillar:** PEOPLE / COMMUNITY
**Languages:** Arabic master → professional Kurdish localization · **Formats:** 1 hero film, 1 announce post, 1 carousel, 2 portrait posts, 12+ story frames

---

## 1. Campaign strategic insight (research-based)

- Iraq has one of MENA's widest financial-inclusion gender gaps: women's account ownership ≈ **19–20% vs 41% for men** (World Bank Global Findex 2025), and Iraq's first **National Financial Inclusion Strategy (2025–2029)** explicitly names women a priority. Education-first content aligns Axi with a legitimate national agenda.
- The industry's default fix is wrong: eToro's 2025 audit of 80+ industry reports found **57% framed women's confidence patronizingly**, and **1 in 5 women said "you lack confidence" messaging put them off investing entirely**. Deficit framing repels the audience.
- The evidence points the other way: Fidelity's 10-year analysis of 5.2M accounts found women's results slightly **better** — driven by discipline and patience, the exact traits markets reward; BNY Mellon found women exceptionally risk-conscious. Risk-awareness is a *trading skill women already practice*.
- Instagram Iraq skews male (Facebook ad audience only ~27.5% female), so the creative must **self-select women in the first second** — female faces, feminine grammatical address (لكِ / تعلّمي), explicit "this is for you."
- No forex/CFD brand has done a serious women's-education campaign in Iraq (none found in research). **Open territory.**

**The insight in one line:**
> Iraqi women don't lack confidence — they've been left out of the knowledge. So we don't cheer them on; we hand them the knowledge, address them directly, and respect them enough to tell the truth about risk.

## 3. Campaign core

1. **Campaign name:** **«عن دراية»** (ʿan dirāya — "with full knowledge / knowingly")
   - Short, premium, MSA-but-warm, works in feminine address, and encodes the whole strategy: informed decisions.
   - Kurdish name: **to be chosen by the professional translators** as a native equivalent (brief them: "the feeling of doing something knowingly and prepared, not bravely" — do not accept a literal word-for-word rendering). `[KU — PENDING TRANSLATION]`
2. **Main concept:** Two weeks in which the Axi Iraq accounts speak directly to women — teaching real market fundamentals, flipping the confidence myth with cited evidence, and portraying real Iraqi and Kurdish women as disciplined learners. No pink, no girl-boss, no lifestyle promises — knowledge, preparation, discipline, risk-awareness.
3. **Main Arabic headline (campaign line):**
   > **«القرار الأذكى يبدأ بالمعرفة. عن دراية.»**
4. **Supporting campaign message:**
   > «ما نقولچ "كوني شجاعة". نقولچ: تعلّمي شلون يشتغل السوق، اعرفي المخاطر، وخلّي قرارچ — إذا قررتي — يكون عن دراية.»
   (We won't tell you "be brave." We'll teach you how the market works, show you the risks, and let your decision — if you make one — be an informed one.)
5. **CTA (campaign-wide):** «ابدئي من الأساس — تعلّمي مجاناً» → Axi education resources / demo account `[VERIFY: exact education asset + link for Iraq clients]`. Secondary CTA on stories: question sticker «اسألي أي سؤال عن السوق — بدون أحكام».

## 4. Hero film — «الأسئلة الصح» (The Right Questions)

**Format:** 9:16 Reel, 35–40s + 15s cut · **Production:** hybrid — real filmed portraits strongly preferred (see file 07 for shoot logistics); AI-generated B-roll only for abstract market visuals, never for the women themselves (authenticity is the campaign).

**Concept:** Four real women in real Iraqi/Kurdish settings — a pharmacist closing her register in Erbil, an engineering student in Baghdad, a shop owner in Sulaymaniyah, a mother reviewing a budget at her kitchen table at night. Each looks up and asks the camera one sharp market question in her own dialect. Their questions get progressively more expert. The film never answers — the questions themselves ARE the expertise forming.

**Script (Arabic master, draft for compliance review):**
- Woman 1: «شنو يعني سعر الصرف يتحرك؟» (What does it mean that the exchange rate moves?)
- Woman 2: «ليش الذهب يصعد لما الدنيا تضطرب؟» (Why does gold rise when the world shakes?)
- Woman 3: «شنو الفرق بين المضاربة والاستثمار؟»
- Woman 4 (beat, quiet confidence): «وشلون أحمي راس مالي قبل ما أفكر بأي ربح؟» (And how do I protect my capital before I even think about profit?)
- Card: **«الأسئلة الصح هي البداية الصح.»**
- Card: **عن دراية — تعلّمي الأسواق. Axi.** + `[RISK-AR]`
- VO alt (if VO version preferred): «الثقة ما تنطى… تنبنى. بالمعرفة.»

**Three alternative hooks (first 1–3s):**
- **Hook A (pattern interrupt, on black):** «يقولون المرأة ما تفهم بالسوق.» → hard cut to Woman 4's question. (Sets up the trope only to demolish it.)
- **Hook B (direct address):** Woman 1 mid-task, looks straight into lens: «عندي سؤال.» (I have a question.)
- **Hook C (stat-led):** on-screen: «١ من كل ٥ نساء ابتعدن عن الاستثمار بسبب رسائل "ما عندچ ثقة"» — صغير: (eToro/Appinio 2025) → «إحنا ما راح نقولها.»
- **Recommended: Hook A** for the film, Hook C reserved for the myth-busting story series.

**Talent & performance direction:** real women (staff, clients-of-friends, community — with signed releases), not models; minimal makeup, natural wardrobe they choose themselves (hijab-diverse representation exactly as reality is — never styled as a statement either way); direction = "you're asking us, not performing"; one take where they almost laugh stays in the edit — warmth is credibility.

**Camera/lighting:** 35mm feel, shallow depth, practical warm light (shop fluorescents, kitchen lamp, golden-hour window); handheld micro-movement; each woman gets one slow push-in. No teal-orange grading — keep Axi cream warmth with a red accent object in each frame (scarf, tea glass, ledger, sticky note).

**Sound:** room-tone-forward; soft oud/electronic hybrid pulse enters at question 3; music drops to silence on Woman 4's question; brand sting on end card.

**Thumbnail (3 options, per file 09 system):**
1. Woman 4 mid-question, eyes to lens, cream panel right with «الأسئلة الصح» (3 words).
2. Split of all four faces in a 2×2 with the campaign mark «عن دراية» center.
3. Black frame, red Arabic type only: «عندي سؤال.» (curiosity play).
- Recommended: 1 (faces beat type on profile grids).

## 8b. Iraq/Kurdistan-specific guardrails (from Iraq context research — file 02)
- **Vocabulary:** frame money knowledge around **family security, preparation, and independence-within-family** (the register proven locally by Asiacell's Almas program, +1.2M female customers in year one — GSMA case) — never rebellion-against-family/religion framing, never "escape" narratives.
- **Representation:** hijab-diverse casting exactly as urban reality is; modest professional dress; agentic roles (pharmacist, engineer, shop owner, lecturer); no sensualized styling. Iraqi female LFP is 10.8% and the inclusion gap ~22pts — the campaign speaks to a real structural gap, so the tone is respectful invitation, not critique of anyone's choices.
- **Presenter protection (serious):** female creators in Iraq face real harassment (the 2024 killing of TikToker Umm Fahad is the grim marker). Offer every woman in the film anonymity options (first name only / no name), pre-arm Arabic + Sorani comment-filter keyword lists on both accounts, assign daily comment moderation during the campaign window, and agree a takedown-on-request promise with each participant.
- **Kurdish edition legal gate:** Erbil authorities banned forex *promotion* (Apr 2025, re-warned May 2026). The Kurdish edition of this campaign runs **education/brand-only with zero trading CTAs** and publishes only after Axi legal/compliance signs off the KRI plan (file 02 §A1).

## 9. Compliance considerations (campaign-specific)
- The Fidelity/BNY stats are **behavioral research about discipline**, never presented as expected returns; always cited on-screen (source + year); pre-clear this mechanic with compliance once.
- No "trading = financial independence/empowerment" equations anywhere; empowerment is knowledge, the only promise is education.
- Q&A answers are informational; advice-seeking questions get the standard redirect line.
- Every asset: `[RISK-AR]` / `[RISK-KU]`; film end-card carries risk line at legible size.
